About

Why Joe Hartmann Finds exists

Joe Hartmann Finds is a simple storefront for Amazon finds that first showed up as Pinterest pins. Each pin gets its own article: who the item is for, the features listed for that product, ideas for wearing or using it, and a clear path to check the current price on Amazon or save the pin.

The collection is meant to grow. New articles are added as new pins are published, across boards such as Cozy Fits, Simulation Station, TikTok Finds, White Collar Electronics.

About the photos

The images on this site are lifestyle or mood photos. They are not photographs of the exact products in the articles. Use them for vibe and styling inspiration, and rely on the Amazon listing for official product photos, colors, and sizing.

About the links

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. When you buy through an Amazon link on this site, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. We do not show static prices because Amazon prices and availability change.
