How to use it

Keep the shears near your cutting board so they are easy to grab. Some everyday jobs where they come in handy:

  • Herbs. Snip chives, parsley, or basil right over a bowl or pan.
  • Poultry. Trim chicken thighs or cut through skin and fat.
  • Pizza and flatbread. Cut slices without dragging toppings like a wheel can.
  • Packaging. Open bags, cut vacuum-sealed packs, or snip twist ties and coupons.

After each use, rinse the blades and dry them. The listing says they are dishwasher safe, but hand washing is recommended to keep the blades at their best. Slide the sheath back on before you put them in a drawer so the blades stay protected and fingers stay safe.
