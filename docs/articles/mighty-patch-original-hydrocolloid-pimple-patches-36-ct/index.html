Who it's for

These patches are aimed at anyone who gets the occasional pimple and wants a hands-off way to deal with it overnight. They are especially useful if you tend to touch, squeeze, or pick at blemishes, because the patch physically covers the spot. Hero Cosmetics also designs them to be worn day or night, so they suit people who want something discreet under a hoodie at home or on a quick errand.

The listing focuses on whiteheads and pimples. If you are dealing with persistent or painful acne, a dermatologist is the right person to ask; these patches are a spot-care tool, not a substitute for professional advice.

How to use it

The routine is short:

1. Cleanse your face and pat the area completely dry. Hydrocolloid sticks best to clean, dry skin, so skip heavy creams on that spot right before applying. 2. Peel one dot from the sheet and press it over the blemish, smoothing the edges. 3. Leave it on overnight. In the morning, peel it off gently.

A few practical tips: keep a sheet in your bathroom drawer so it is there when you need it, and apply the patch as the last step of your night routine. Because the patch is thin and translucent, some people also wear one during the day around the house.
