Who it's for

Vinsguir aims this wheel at people who want to build a stronger, more defined core at home. The brand says its slightly rounded wheel has a smaller contact surface, which makes balance and stability harder. It describes it as great for more advanced ab workouts.

That means beginners should go slowly. Start with short rollouts from your knees and build range over time. If you have back or shoulder problems, check with a professional before using an ab wheel.

How to use it

Put the handles on, then find a clear floor area long enough to stretch your whole body out. Kneel on a mat or a folded towel to cushion your knees. Hold the handles with the wheel in front of your knees.

Brace your core, keep your back flat, and slowly roll the wheel forward as far as you can control. Then pull it back to the start using your abs, not your hips. Begin with small rollouts and a few reps. As you get stronger, roll farther out. Rolling at an angle to each side brings in your obliques.

Advanced users can try rollouts from the toes, but only once knee rollouts feel smooth and controlled. Stop if you feel any strain in your lower back.
