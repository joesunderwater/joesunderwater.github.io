How to use it

Neutrogena's directions are to apply it evenly to the face and neck daily after cleansing. A small amount spreads a long way because of the gel texture. Pat or press it in, then give it a minute to sink in before the next step.

In the morning, follow it with sunscreen, since this moisturizer does not contain SPF. Then apply makeup if you wear it. The fast-absorbing gel is meant to layer without feeling heavy. At night, use it on its own after cleansing, or under a richer cream if your skin needs more in winter.

Keep the lid tightly closed between uses and use clean fingers to scoop it out. If you have sensitive skin or react to fragrance, patch test first or choose the fragrance-free version instead.
