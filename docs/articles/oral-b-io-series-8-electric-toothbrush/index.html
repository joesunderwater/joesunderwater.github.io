Who it's for

The iO Series 8 makes sense for anyone moving up from a manual brush or an older electric model who wants more feedback about how they brush. If you tend to scrub too hard, the pressure sensor is the headline feature. If you like data, the Oral-B app tracks how and where you brush.

It is also a fit for frequent travelers. This listing includes a travel case, so the brush can go in a dopp kit for work trips and weekends away without rattling around loose.

Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN (B088CL4RL2) and Oral-B's official iO Series 8 product page:

  • iO magnetic technology. Oral-B describes the iO drive as combining gentle micro-vibrations with oscillating-rotating motion through a round brush head.
  • 6 cleaning modes. Oral-B lists Daily Clean, Whitening, Gum Care, Sensitive, Intense, and Super Sensitive.
  • Colored interactive display that tracks brushing time and gives you a "great clean" message when you are done.
  • Smart pressure sensor that signals if you are brushing too hard, too soft, or just right. Oral-B's page describes red for too hard, white for too light, and green for optimal pressure.
  • A.I.-powered Oral-B app that tracks how and where you brush in real time and coaches you toward better habits.

Oral-B also makes clinical-style claims about plaque removal and gum health compared with a manual brush. Those are the brand's own claims, so they are not repeated here as fact; see the listing if you want the details and footnotes.

How to use it

Charge the handle fully, then pick a mode. Daily Clean is the obvious starting point; Sensitive or Super Sensitive are there if your gums need a gentler session, and Whitening or Intense when you want something different.

Let the brush do the work. Guide the round head slowly from tooth to tooth instead of scrubbing back and forth, and watch the pressure light — green means you are in the right range. The display keeps track of time so you do not have to count.

If you want more guidance, pair the brush with the Oral-B app. It shows which areas you are hitting and which ones you are missing, which is the fastest way to catch habits like always skipping the back molars.

For trips, pop the brush into the included travel case. Check the Amazon listing for current details on replacement brush heads so you can keep spares on hand.
