How to use it

1. Pour drippings or stock into the removable top. The strainer catches larger bits. 2. Let it sit briefly so the fat rises to the top. 3. Hold it over your saucepan or bowl and squeeze the handle. The juices release from the bottom. 4. Stop squeezing when the fat layer gets close to the bottom, so the fat stays in the separator. 5. Clean up. The external drain helps it rinse out, and it can go in the dishwasher.

A few practical ideas:

  • Thanksgiving gravy. Separate turkey drippings, then use the defatted juices as your gravy base.
  • Homemade stock. Pour finished stock through to strain bits and remove fat in one step.
  • Use the markings for measuring. Since it shows cups, ounces, and milliliters, you can measure how much juice you have for a recipe.
  • Save the fat if you want it. Some cooks keep a spoonful for roux or roasting vegetables.
