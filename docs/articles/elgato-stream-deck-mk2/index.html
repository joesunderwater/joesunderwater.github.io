Most of the clicks in a workday are repeats: opening the same apps, muting the same mic, switching the same scenes, typing the same shortcuts. Who it's for

Elgato pitches the Stream Deck at anyone who wants faster control of their apps and tools. That includes streamers and content creators running OBS, Twitch, or YouTube, as well as people who edit video, produce music, or work through photography workflows and want hotkeys at their fingertips.

It is also a fit for a desk-bound professional who simply wants fewer keyboard gymnastics: one key to mute the mic, one to launch a set of apps, one to change smart lights. It works with both Windows and macOS.

Key features

Details below come from Elgato’s technical specifications and the Amazon listing for this ASIN (model 10GBA9901):

  • 15 customizable LCD keys. Each key displays its own icon, so you can see what it does, and gives visual feedback that a command has run.
  • One-touch actions: trigger a single action or a chain of multiple actions, launch social posts, adjust audio, mute a mic, play sound effects, turn on lights, and more.
  • Plugins: Elgato lists support for its own products along with OBS, Twitch, YouTube, Discord, Spotify, Philips Hue, and many others through the Stream Deck Store.
  • Hotkey actions for film editing, music production, and photography workflows.
  • Automatic profile switching: key layouts can change automatically as you move between apps.
  • Personalization: custom icons, screensavers, and interchangeable faceplates.
  • Connection: USB 2.0 interface with a detachable USB-C cable. Elgato notes that current units include a USB-C to USB-C cable; earlier units shipped with USB-C to USB-A.
  • In the box: Stream Deck, desktop stand, USB-C cable, and quick start guide.
  • Size: about 4.6 x 3.3 in, and 145 g without the stand, per Elgato.
  • Compatibility: Elgato’s spec page lists Windows 10 (64-bit) or newer and macOS 10.15 or newer. Check the Amazon listing for the current requirements before you buy.

How to use it

Start small. Install the Stream Deck app, plug in the device, and set up one row of keys for the things you do dozens of times a day: mute and unmute, open your calendar, launch your main work apps. Once that becomes habit, add a folder for a specific workflow, such as editing shortcuts or a set of lighting scenes.

For streaming or recording, plugins for OBS and other tools let you switch scenes, start recording, and trigger sound effects without leaving the moment. For creative work, map your most-used editing or production shortcuts to labeled keys so you stop hunting through menus.

The included stand angles the keys toward you, so it sits comfortably beside a keyboard. Turn on automatic profile switching and the keys can change to match whichever app is in front.
