Who it's for

SUPVAN pitches the T50M Pro at three groups: home organizers labeling storage bins, bottles, jars, and food containers; small businesses printing addresses, product info, QR codes, barcodes, and logos; and school or office users making name tags and folder labels.

It's a good fit if you'd rather design on your phone than on a built-in keypad, and if you want labels in different shapes, such as round jar labels or cable flags. If you want a printer that needs no ink cartridges, this one is inkless.

How to use it

1. Install the app on your phone or tablet and connect to the printer. 2. Load a label roll. The printer recognizes the label size automatically. 3. Design the label. Pick a font, frame, or icon, or drop in an image or QR code. 4. Print and stick. Peel the label and apply it to a clean, dry surface.

Organizing ideas: label clear pantry bins by category, put round labels on spice and flour jars, mark storage bins with their contents and season, tag charger cables near the plug end, and label file folders. For a consistent look, keep one font and frame style for a whole room.
