Who it's for

DURATECH pitches this tape at engineers, builders, carpenters, and anyone measuring indoors or out. It's a good fit for DIYers who often measure alone, since the magnetic hook can hold onto a steel stud, a metal door frame, or ductwork while you walk the blade out. The fraction markings also make it friendly for people who would rather read "5/8" than count sixteenth-inch lines, which is handy for anyone newer to woodworking or home projects.

Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN and DURATECH's own product page:

  • 25 ft blade, 1 inch wide. The blade is thickened carbon steel with a nylon coating for durability. DURATECH says it is stiff enough to feed out about 7 ft without bending.
  • Double-sided markings. The blade is printed on both sides, which helps when you're measuring from an awkward angle or holding the tape up overhead.
  • 1/8 inch fractional marks. Fractions are printed on the blade, along with foot marks and triangle markings every 19.2 inches for layout work.
  • Magnetic hook. The end hook has strong magnets designed to grip metal surfaces, making one-person measurements easier.
  • Zero-point sliding hook. The hook slides to account for its own thickness, so inside and outside measurements stay accurate.
  • Sliding thumb lock. A thumb lock slides up and down to lock and release the blade.
  • Shock-absorbent case. The housing combines hard ABS plastic with TPR rubber, which DURATECH describes as wear-resistant and drop-proof. It also has a metal belt buckle and a hanging lanyard.
  • Options. The listing also offers 12 ft and 16 ft sizes and a 2-pack.

How to use it

For most measurements, hook the end over the edge of a board or wall, pull the blade out, and slide the thumb lock to hold it in place while you mark. When you're working with steel studs, metal framing, appliances, or pipe, let the magnetic hook stick to the surface so you have both hands free.

For inside measurements, like the width of a window opening or a cabinet, press the case against one side and read the blade where it meets the case. The sliding hook is designed to account for its own thickness, so you don't need to add or subtract anything for the hook.

The triangle marks every 19.2 inches are useful if you lay out joists or trusses on that spacing. For everyday jobs like hanging shelves, cutting trim, or checking furniture sizes before you buy, the printed fractions make it quick to read and write down measurements.

Clip it to your belt or pocket with the metal buckle, or hang it from a pegboard hook by the lanyard so it's always easy to find.
