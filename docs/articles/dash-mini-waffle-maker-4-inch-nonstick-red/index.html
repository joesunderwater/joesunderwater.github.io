How to use it

Plug it in and wait for the indicator light to tell you it is ready. Add a small amount of batter to the center of the plate, close the lid, and let it cook. Because the plate is only 4 inches across, a little batter goes a long way, so start small and adjust. Once the waffle is done, lift it out and repeat. Follow the included manual for timing and care.

Beyond classic waffles, try the ideas Dash mentions, such as hash browns and cornbread, or use the included recipe booklet for starting points. Make a stack of mini waffles for a breakfast board, press a chaffle for a low-carb sandwich, or make a batch of small waffles for kids. After cooking, let it cool and wipe the nonstick surface clean. Red adds a pop of color if you leave it out on a small counter.
