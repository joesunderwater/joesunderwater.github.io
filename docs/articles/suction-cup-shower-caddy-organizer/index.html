Who it's for

This caddy is for renters, anyone who doesn't want holes in their tile, and people who dislike hanging caddies that swing from the showerhead. It also suits small bathrooms where counter space is scarce, since the brand says the baskets work on counters and in the kitchen too.

The key question is your wall. The listing says it ONLY works on smooth, uncracked ceramic tile, glass, mirrors, polished metal, stainless steel, and acrylic. It is not meant for painted walls, drywall, wood, wallpaper, or rough or cracked surfaces, and it shouldn't be placed over a grout line.

  • No-drill install. No screws, glue, or tools. You press the suction plate firmly onto a smooth, dry surface.

  • Drainage design. Many drainage holes and a bottom tilted at -6° help water run off so bottles dry faster.
  • Built-in hooks. The fence-style front has hooks for towels, brushes, or a loofah.
  • Removable baskets. The baskets lift off, so you can clean them or use them elsewhere.

How to use it

1. Choose the spot. Pick a smooth, flat area of tile or glass that avoids grout lines. 2. Prep the surface. Clean and dry the wall and the suction cup. 3. Install. Remove the protective cover and push the plate strongly until you hear the suction set. 4. Load it. Put tall bottles in the large basket and soap, razors, or small bottles in the medium one, then hang a loofah or brush on the hooks.

If it won't hold, the brand suggests washing the suction cup, drying it with a hair dryer, and testing it on smooth glass. If it holds on glass, the original wall probably isn't suitable, so try another spot.
