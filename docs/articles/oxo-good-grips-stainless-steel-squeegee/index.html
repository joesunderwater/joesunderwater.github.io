How to use it

The easiest routine is to squeegee right after every shower, while the glass is still wet. Start at the top of the door or wall and pull the blade straight down in overlapping strokes. Wipe the blade on a towel or flick it off now and then so you are not dragging water back across the glass.

For tile walls, work from top to bottom the same way, then finish with a quick pass on the glass. On mirrors, use it after you clean the glass to clear excess liquid without leaving lint behind.

Stick the suction holder on a smooth surface inside the shower, such as glass or tile, so the squeegee is within reach. Keeping it where you can see it is what turns the swipe into a habit.

Outside the bathroom, a squeegee like this is useful for clearing car windows after washing, or for drying a stone countertop after you wipe it down.
