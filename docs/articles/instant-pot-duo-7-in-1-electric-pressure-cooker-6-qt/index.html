How to use it

The Duo is built around one-pot cooking. A typical weeknight might look like this:

  • Sauté first. Use the sauté function in the stainless steel pot to brown onions, garlic, or meat. The tri-ply bottom helps it cook evenly.
  • Then pressure cook. Add broth, beans, or vegetables, lock the lid, and choose a Smart Program like soup, beans, or poultry. Dinner is done in one pot with less cleanup.
  • Keep it warm. The warming function holds food until everyone is ready to eat.

Other ideas:

  • Rice and grains for the week's meal prep.
  • Slow cooker recipes like pot roast or stew, started in the morning.
  • Homemade yogurt with the yogurt program.
  • Steamed vegetables as a quick side.

When you're done, the lid, inner pot, and accessories go in the dishwasher.
