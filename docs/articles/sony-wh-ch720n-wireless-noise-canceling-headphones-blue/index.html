You don't have to buy Sony's flagship headphones to get its noise canceling. The WH-CH720N is a lighter, more affordable over-ear model, and Sony describes it as its lightest wireless noise canceling headband. It shares the Integrated Processor V1 used in the WH-1000XM5. This listing is the Blue colorway (model WHCH720N/L), a nice change from the usual black and a fun pick for gift-giving.

The photo on this page is a lifestyle image for mood and inspiration. It is not a photograph of these headphones. For official product photos, use the Amazon listing.

Who it's for

The WH-CH720N is aimed at everyday listening: studying, commuting, working from home, and travel. The light, ergonomic build is designed for long sessions, and multipoint connection makes it easy to move between a laptop and a phone. Students, remote workers, and frequent travelers who want noise canceling without the weight or cost of a premium model are the natural fit.

Key features

  • Noise canceling. Sony uses Dual Noise Sensor technology and the Integrated Processor V1. An adjustable Ambient Sound mode lets outside sound in when you need it.
  • Long battery life. Sony lists up to 35 hours of music playback with noise canceling on and up to 50 hours with it off. Quick charging gives up to an hour of playback from a 3-minute charge, according to the listing.
  • Lightweight design. Sony puts the weight at about 192 g (roughly 6.8 oz).
  • Sound tuning. The listing mentions DSEE (Digital Sound Enhancement Engine) to restore detail lost in compressed audio, EQ settings in the Sony Headphones Connect app, and 360 Reality Audio certification.
  • Clear calls. Precise Voice Pickup Technology, beamforming mics, and a wind-noise-reduction structure help your voice come through on calls.
  • Easy connection. Multipoint connection, Swift Pair, and Fast Pair. Bluetooth 5.2 with SBC and AAC support, per Sony's spec sheet.
  • Wired option. A connection cable and a USB cable come in the box. Sony's specs list passive operation through the headphone cable.

How to use it

Pair the headphones with your phone using Fast Pair (Android) or the usual Bluetooth menu, then add your laptop as a second device so multipoint can switch between them. Install the Sony Headphones Connect app to adjust the EQ and the Ambient Sound level. Leave noise canceling on in a coffee shop or on a plane, and turn up Ambient Sound when you need to hear an announcement or a coworker.

For travel, top up the battery before you leave. Sony quotes about 3.5 hours for a full charge, and a short quick charge helps if you forget. Bring the included cable for in-flight entertainment systems. The Blue finish is easy to spot in a bag and stands out in a gift box.
