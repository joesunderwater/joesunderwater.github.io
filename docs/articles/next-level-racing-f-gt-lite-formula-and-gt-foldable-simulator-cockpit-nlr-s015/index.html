Next Level Racing F-GT Lite Foldable Sim Cockpit – Formula & GT Positions

Next Level Racing F-GT Lite Formula and GT Foldable Simulator Cockpit (NLR-S015) · Posted 2026-11-11

Some races feel right sitting upright in a GT car. Others feel right almost on the floor, with the wheel in your lap, as in a Formula car. The Next Level Racing F-GT Lite is a foldable cockpit built to do both: a fabric seat and a steel frame that can change from a low Formula position to a more upright GT position, then fold away when the session is over.

The photo on this page is a lifestyle image for mood and styling inspiration. It is not a photograph of this cockpit. For official compatibility, product photos, and assembly notes, use the Amazon listing linked below.

Who it's for

The F-GT Lite (model NLR-S015) is for people who want a proper racing seat at home, switch between open-wheel and GT games, and still need to store the cockpit after they race. Next Level Racing calls it a portable, minimalist cockpit for limited space, with hubs that use a quick-release adjustment to change driving positions. It is a different product from the GT Lite, which is GT-position only.

It is a fit if you use entry- to mid-range wheels and pedals from brands such as Logitech, Thrustmaster, or Fanatec, in either a clamped or a hard-mounted setup. That is the official guidance; it is not described as a chassis for the strongest direct-drive bases. The published user range is 120–200 cm (4 ft to 6 ft 6 in) in height and up to 130 kg (287 lb).

Key features

Details below come from Next Level Racing’s official F-GT Lite support page and the Amazon listing for NLR-S015:

  • A foldable cockpit with two driving positions, Formula and GT, changed through Next Level Racing Hubs with a quick-release adjustment. Each hub is described as able to withstand 150 kg of force.
  • A powder-coated steel frame and a breathable fabric seat.
  • Adjustable wheel, pedal, and shifter positions and angles, with pre-drilled hard-mount points for wheels, pedals, and shifters, plus support for clamp-on wheels. Official materials list compatibility with major wheels and pedals and pre-drilled patterns for Logitech, Thrustmaster, and Fanatec.
  • A gear-shifter mount included in the box, installable on the left or right, and described as compatible with a range of Thrustmaster, Logitech, MOZA, and Fanatec shifters.
  • The cockpit can be folded with electronics still installed for storage or for moving it from room to room.
  • Official dimensions: 175 x 75 x 127 cm (68.5 x 29.5 x 50 inches) in the GT position; 164 x 75 x 94 cm (64.5 x 29.5 x 37 inches) in the Formula position; and 75 x 40 x 87 cm (29.5 x 15.7 x 34 inches) when folded. The cockpit weighs 19 kg (42 lb).
  • In the box: the cockpit, instructions and tools, and bolts to hard-mount a wheel, shifter, and pedals, plus the shifter mount.

How to use it

Assemble the frame, then pick a starting position. For GT or touring cars, use the more upright layout, with the wheel at chest-to-shoulder height and the pedals in a comfortable reach. For Formula or other open-wheel games, lower the seat and wheel into the Formula geometry so you are more reclined, with the wheel closer to a Formula-style lap position. Tighten the hubs fully after each change; a half-latched hub will flex.

Hard-mount the wheel and pedals when the plate allows it, especially if you use a firmer brake. Fit the shifter on the side you need, then test both positions before you race a league session, because pedal angle and reach will change when you switch. When you are done, fold the cockpit as the manual shows. Official materials say it can pack away with the electronics still installed, which is the practical reason this model exists for small rooms.

Stay inside the published height and weight ranges, and treat this as an entry-to-mid-range cockpit. If you only want one upright GT position and a slightly lighter fold, the GT Lite is the simpler sibling.

Quick FAQ

Can it really switch between Formula and GT positions?

Yes. That is the main design difference from the GT Lite. The hubs use a quick-release adjustment to move between a low Formula position and an upright GT position.

What wheels is it meant for?

Official materials say it is ideal for entry- to mid-range wheels and pedals from brands such as Logitech, Thrustmaster, and Fanatec, in clamped or hard-mounted setups. Confirm your exact hardware on the current notes.

Does it include a shifter mount?

Yes. A gear-shifter mount is included and can be installed on the left or the right side.

How big is it, and can it fold?

The GT position is 175 x 75 x 127 cm, the Formula position is 164 x 75 x 94 cm, and the folded size is 75 x 40 x 87 cm. Official materials say it can fold with electronics installed. It weighs 19 kg.
