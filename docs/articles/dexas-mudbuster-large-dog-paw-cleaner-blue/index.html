How to use it

Measure the widest part of your dog's paw to confirm the large size is right. At the door, pour a little water into the cup, enough to wet the bristles. You can add a drop of pet shampoo if you want. Lift one paw into the MudBuster, twist the cup back and forth, lift the paw out, and dab it dry with a towel. Repeat for the other three paws.

When you are finished, empty the dirty water, remove the silicone insert, and rinse it or run it on the dishwasher's top rack. Let it dry before the next walk. Make the first few uses a calm, treat-filled routine so your dog is willing to offer a paw.
