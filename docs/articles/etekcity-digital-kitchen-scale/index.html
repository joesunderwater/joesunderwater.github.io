How to use it

Set the scale on a flat surface, turn it on, and place your bowl on top. Press tare to zero it out, then add your ingredient until you reach the weight you need. Switch units as needed for the recipe.

A few practical ideas:

1. Bake by weight. Weighing flour and sugar in grams is more consistent than scooping, and you can tare between ingredients to use one bowl. 2. Dial in coffee. Weigh beans and water for the same ratio every time. 3. Portion meals. Split meat, rice, or snacks into even portions for meal prep. 4. Measure liquids. Use the mL or fl'oz mode for milk and water. 5. Wipe, don't soak. The listing says to wipe down the stainless steel surface to keep it clean.
