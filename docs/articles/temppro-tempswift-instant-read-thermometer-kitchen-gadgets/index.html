How to use it

Release the probe with the button and insert the tip into the thickest part of the food, away from bone. Wait a moment for the reading to settle, then check the screen. If you are not at your target temperature yet, keep cooking and check again. Clean the probe between checks of raw and cooked food.

A few ideas from the listing's own suggested uses:

  • Grilling and BBQ. Check steaks, burgers, chicken, and pork chops before taking them off the heat.
  • Roasting. Test a turkey or roast in a couple of spots before you pull it from the oven.
  • Frying. Check the oil temperature before adding food.
  • Candy and milk. Track sugar syrups or warm milk on the stovetop.

When you are done, fold the probe back in and stick the thermometer on the fridge with the magnet so it is always within reach.
