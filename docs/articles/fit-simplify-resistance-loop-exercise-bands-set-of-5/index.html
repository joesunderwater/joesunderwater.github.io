How to use it

Start with a lighter band and loop it just above your knees or around your ankles. Good first moves are glute bridges, lateral walks, clamshells, squats, and donkey kicks. Do them slowly and keep tension on the band through the whole rep. When you can finish your sets with good form, move up one level.

Bands also work for upper body moves. Hold one between your hands for pull-aparts, or loop it around your wrists for shoulder work. The instruction guide that comes with the set walks through more options by body part.

Toss the carry bag in a suitcase or gym bag so you can keep the same routine at a hotel, the park, or the office. Check bands for nicks or tears before each use and replace any that look worn.
