How to use it

Start with towel-dried hair rather than soaking wet hair, and apply a heat protectant if you normally use one. Detangle, then divide your hair into sections and clip the top layers out of the way.

Pick a heat setting that suits your hair: lower settings for fine or fragile hair, higher for thicker hair. Place the brush under a section at the roots, hold for a moment for lift, then slowly glide it down toward the ends. For a bouncy finish, rotate your wrist and roll the ends under or flip them out. Switch to the cool setting at the end to help set the style.

Work section by section until your hair is dry, then smooth over the top. When you are done, let the tool cool, press the release button to detach the head, and store it.
