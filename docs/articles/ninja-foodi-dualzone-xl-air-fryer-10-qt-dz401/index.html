  • Two independent baskets. Up to 10 quarts of total capacity, split across two 5-quart baskets.

How to use it

Load each basket, pick a program and time for each zone, then choose how you want them to finish:

  • Main and side together. Put chicken wings in one basket and fries in the other, set each one, and press Smart Finish. The basket with the shorter time waits so both finish together.
  • Cooking for a crowd. Fill both baskets with the same food, like a double batch of wings or roasted vegetables, and use Match Cook so you don't have to set each side twice.
  • Snacks and extras. Use dehydrate for dried fruit or homemade jerky, reheat for leftovers that stay crisp, and bake for small desserts.

Ninja's examples include crispy fries, juicy chicken, roasted veggies, baked goods, dehydrated fruit, and jerky. When dinner is done, the crisper plates go in the dishwasher.

Quick FAQ

How big are the baskets?

The listing describes two independent 5-quart baskets, for up to 10 quarts in total.

What's the difference between Smart Finish and Match Cook?

Smart Finish cooks two foods with different settings so they finish at the same time. Match Cook copies one basket's settings to the other for a full 10-quart batch.

What can it do besides air fry?

It has six programs: air fry, air broil, roast, bake, reheat, and dehydrate.
