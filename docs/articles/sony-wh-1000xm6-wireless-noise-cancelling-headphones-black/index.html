Who it's for

These headphones are a fit if you want a closed, over-ear wireless pair for work calls, travel, and everyday listening. Sony positions the WH-1000XM6 for people who need noise cancellation that can adjust in real time, plus clear calls and all-day comfort. Multipoint Bluetooth makes them useful if you move between a laptop and a phone.

They also make sense if you want a wired fallback. A detachable cable and stereo mini jack are included, and the headphones can operate passively when the battery is depleted. If you already use Sony’s Sound Connect app, this model is designed to work with that software for EQ, noise-canceling settings, and extra features.

Key features

Details below come from Sony’s official product and specification pages for the WH-1000XM6:

  • Closed, over-ear wireless headphones with 30 mm drivers.
  • Adaptive noise cancellation is handled by the HD Noise Canceling Processor QN3, which Sony says is 7x faster than the QN1 in the WH-1000XM5, working with a 12-microphone system. The Adaptive NC Optimizer adjusts to external noise, air pressure, and wearing style, including use with a hat or glasses. Ambient Sound and Quick Attention modes are included.
  • Battery life is up to 30 hours of music playback with noise canceling on, or up to 40 hours with it off. Talk time is up to 24 hours with noise canceling on, or 28 hours with it off. A full charge takes about 3.5 hours over USB. Sony also lists quick charging with a USB Power Delivery adapter.
  • Bluetooth 5.3 with SBC, AAC, LDAC, and LC3 codecs, plus multipoint connection.
  • DSEE Extreme and other listening features can be managed in the Sony | Sound Connect app.
  • Folding earcups and a compact case are part of the travel design. The headset weighs about 254 g (8.96 oz).
  • A detachable 1.2 m cable allows wired listening, including passive operation when the headphones are powered off.

This listing is for the black model. Other colors may be available; check the Amazon page for current options.

How to use it

For a workday, pair the headphones with your primary computer and keep your phone as the second multipoint device so calls and music can hand off without a full reconnect. Use the Sound Connect app to set your preferred noise-canceling level, turn on Ambient Sound when you need to hear a gate announcement or a coworker, and adjust the EQ if you want.

On travel days, the foldable design and included case are the practical reasons this model fits a carry-on. Sony’s atmospheric-pressure optimization is aimed at flights, where cabin pressure can change how noise cancellation feels. If you expect a long stretch without a charger, start with a full battery and remember that turning noise canceling off extends playback time.

For calls, the same 12-microphone system and QN3 processor are used to improve voice pickup. Set up Google Assistant, Alexa, or other partner services in the Sound Connect app rather than assuming every feature works out of the box. Firmware and compatibility can change, so Sony’s support pages and the Amazon listing are the place to confirm current details.
