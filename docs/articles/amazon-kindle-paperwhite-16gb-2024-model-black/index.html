Kindle Paperwhite 16GB (2024) – 7-Inch Glare-Free E-Reader, Weeks of Battery

A phone is a convenient place to start a book and a poor place to finish one. Notifications, glare, and a battery that needs charging every night all get in the way. The 2024 Kindle Paperwhite is Amazon’s current standard Paperwhite, built around a larger 7-inch glare-free display, faster page turns than earlier models, and a battery meant to last weeks rather than hours.

Who it's for

This model is a fit if you want a dedicated e-reader that is easy to read outdoors, in bed, or on a trip, without social apps competing for attention. Amazon positions the 12th-generation Paperwhite (2024 release) as a travel-friendly reader: thin, waterproof, and charged over USB-C. The 16 GB of storage is intended to hold thousands of books, plus personal documents and, if you use it, Audible content over Bluetooth.

It is also a practical upgrade if you are still reading on a smaller Kindle or on a phone and want a bigger page, a warmer front light at night, and less frequent charging. This listing is the black 16 GB Paperwhite; confirm the exact model, because Amazon also sells a Signature Edition with extra features.

Key features

Details below come from Amazon’s official product information for the 2024 Kindle Paperwhite (16 GB):

  • 7-inch Paperwhite display with a built-in front light, 300 ppi resolution, optimized font technology, and 16-level grayscale. Amazon describes this generation as having a higher contrast ratio and faster page turns than previous Paperwhite models.
  • Adjustable light that can shift from white to amber for bright sun or a dark room.
  • 16 GB of on-device storage, plus free cloud storage for Amazon content.
  • Battery life of up to 12 weeks on a single charge, based on a half hour of reading per day with wireless off and the light setting at 13. Battery life varies with use, and Audible streaming over Bluetooth will reduce it. A full charge takes less than 2.5 hours with a 9W USB-C adapter (the adapter is not necessarily included).
  • USB-C charging. The box includes the Kindle, a USB-C cable, and a Quick Start Guide.
  • Waterproof rating of IPX8, tested to withstand immersion in 2 meters of fresh water for 60 minutes.
  • Wi-Fi on 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks, plus Bluetooth for audio features such as Audible and the VoiceView screen reader.
  • About 5 x 7 x 0.3 inches and 7.4 oz (211 g). Accessibility options include VoiceView, Dark Mode, and adjustable fonts and margins.

How to use it

Treat the Paperwhite as a single-purpose reading device. Download books over Wi-Fi, then turn wireless off if you want to stretch the battery toward that multi-week estimate. The adjustable warm light is most useful in the evening: a cooler white light for daytime, and a warmer amber tone when you are reading in bed.

Because the screen is glare-free and the device is rated IPX8, it is a reasonable companion for a pool bag, a beach trip, or a bath, with the usual caution that waterproof ratings have limits and salt water or sand still need care. For travel, the thin chassis and USB-C charging make it easy to share a cable with a phone, though you should confirm whether a power adapter is included in the current package.

If you listen to audiobooks, pair Bluetooth headphones or speakers and use Audible on the device. Always use the live listing for storage options, colors, and whether a given unit is ad-supported.

Quick FAQ

Is this the 2024 Paperwhite or the Signature Edition?

This listing is the 16 GB Kindle Paperwhite (12th generation, 2024). The Signature Edition is a separate model with additional features such as more storage. Confirm the exact name and ASIN on the Amazon page before you buy.

How long does the battery last?

Amazon rates it at up to 12 weeks, based on a half hour of reading a day with wireless off and the light at setting 13. Heavier use, higher brightness, or audiobook streaming will shorten that.

Is it waterproof?

It is rated IPX8 and has been tested for immersion in 2 meters of fresh water for 60 minutes. That does not cover every kind of water exposure, so it is still worth reading Amazon’s waterproofing notes.

Can I adjust the light for nighttime reading?

Yes. The front light can be adjusted from white to amber, which is the feature most people use for reading in the dark.
