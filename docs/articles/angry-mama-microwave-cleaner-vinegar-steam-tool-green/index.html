How to use it

1. Fill Angry Mama with vinegar and water (add a little lemon juice if you like the scent). 2. Set her in the microwave, upright, with the steam holes in her head facing up. 3. Microwave for 5-7 minutes, as the listing suggests, and watch the steam vent from her head. 4. Let it sit a minute, then carefully remove her. The liquid and plastic will be hot. 5. Wipe the inside of the microwave with a cloth or sponge while the grime is still soft. 6. Rinse her out or put her in the dishwasher.

A few practical ideas:

  • Make it a weekly habit. Regular quick steams keep splatters from building up.
  • Use an oven mitt to lift her out after heating.
  • Keep her on display. She is small enough to sit on top of the microwave or on a shelf, ready for the next round.
