Who it's for

This light is for households dealing with pet accidents, especially on carpet, upholstery, and hard floors where stains hide. It is useful after a cleanup to check whether you found everything, and for spotting older marks before you rent a cleaner or treat a room. The listing also mentions other UV uses, such as looking for dry stains, inspecting for bed bugs, finding scorpions, checking IDs or currency, and curing UV resin.

How to use it

Wait until the room is dark, or at least dim. Turn the flashlight on and hold it a short distance from the surface you want to check. Sweep slowly across carpets, baseboards, mattress edges, sofa cushions, and the spots your pet uses most. Dried urine often glows under UV light; mark those areas and clean them with a pet-safe enzymatic cleaner, then re-check after the area dries.

Keep the beam away from eyes, yours and your pet's, and do not use it as a toy. If you are inspecting bedding or furniture, lift cushions and scan seams, not just the obvious middle of a rug.
