How to use it

  • Find your target pressure. Check the sticker in the driver's door jamb or your owner's manual for the recommended PSI. Do not use the maximum number printed on the tire sidewall as your target.
  • Check when tires are cold. For the most consistent numbers, check before driving or after the car has sat for a while.
  • Take a reading. Turn the gauge on, remove the valve cap, and press the nozzle straight and firmly onto the valve stem. Read the display, then replace the cap.
  • Adjust. Add air if you are low, or let a little out if you are high, and check again.
  • Don't forget the spare. Spares often run at a different pressure, so check its own recommended value.
  • Battery care. AstroAI says to replace the battery when "LO" appears, and to remove it if you won't use the gauge for a long time.
