Who it's for

The FreeSip is for anyone who wants a cold-water bottle they can carry all day: to work, in the car, on a bike ride, or in a backpack, which are the uses Owala names in the listing. It is a good pick for people who cannot decide between a straw bottle and a chug bottle, since this one does both.

Owala is clear that the FreeSip is meant for cold drinks and is not intended for hot liquids, so it is not a coffee mug replacement. The brand offers it in 16, 19, 24, 32, and 40 oz sizes and notes that the 32 and 40 oz versions are not cup-holder friendly. The 24 oz keeps the slim, cup-holder-friendly base.

Key features

  • Two ways to drink. The FreeSip spout lets you sip upright through the built-in straw or tilt back to swig from the wide-mouth spout opening.
  • Push-button lid. One press opens the lid, and Owala says the design keeps the spout clean.
  • Leakproof when locked. The carry loop doubles as a lock to help prevent accidental opening, and Owala describes the bottle as 100% leakproof when locked.
  • Triple-wall insulated stainless steel. Owala says it keeps drinks cold for up to 24 hours.
  • Cup-holder friendly. The slim design of the 24 oz size fits most cup holders.
  • Safe materials. Made without BPA, lead, or phthalates.
  • Easy care. A wide opening makes it easy to add ice and hand wash the cup. The lid is dishwasher safe.
  • Warranty. The listing mentions a manufacturer's limited lifetime warranty.

How to use it

Fill the bottle with ice and water, press the button to pop the lid open, and choose how you want to drink. Sipping through the straw works well at a desk or in the car, while tilting back to swig is faster after a workout. When you toss the bottle into a bag, flip the carry loop over to lock the lid so it cannot pop open.

For cleaning, Owala recommends hand washing the cup and putting the lid in the dishwasher. Skip hot drinks; the brand says the FreeSip is not intended for them. The Denim color is an easy neutral that goes with gym bags, backpacks, and work totes, and the bottle makes a practical gift for students and commuters.
