Who it's for

This serum is for people who want hydration and a brighter-looking complexion from one step. The Amazon listing says it suits all skin types and points it toward dull, dry, and sensitive skin. It is a natural next step if you already like COSRX snail products and want to add niacinamide (vitamin B3) without buying a separate serum.

How to use it

After cleansing and toning, press the pump once or twice into your palm. COSRX notes that you should mix the two textures together well before applying, so swirl them with a fingertip until they look even. Then smooth the serum over your face and gently pat it in.

Follow with your moisturizer to seal everything in. In the morning, finish with sunscreen; at night, it can sit under your usual night cream. If you also use other COSRX snail products, this serum layers easily before a snail cream. Stop using it and talk to your doctor if irritation occurs, as the brand advises.
