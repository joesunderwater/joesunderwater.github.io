Packing for an international trip often means a tangle of plug adapters and a fight over the one outlet in the hotel room. An all-in-one travel adapter fixes both problems. Who it's for

This adapter is aimed at international travelers who carry more than one device: phones, tablets, laptops, cameras, headphones, and power banks. It's a good fit for families or couples sharing one outlet, business travelers, and cruise passengers (the listing marks it as cruise friendly). EPICKA says the plugs cover over 200 countries and regions, including the USA, Canada, Japan, the UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, China, Australia, and New Zealand. The listing notes that South Africa and India are not covered, so check your destination first.

Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • Four plugs in one. The adapter includes four plug types (Type A, Type C, Type G, and Type I) for US, EU, UK, and AU style outlets.
  • Charge up to 6 devices. It has 1 AC socket, 3 USB-C ports (3A), and 2 USB-A ports (2.4A).
  • Fuse protection. It has a replaceable 10A fuse, and one spare fuse is included. If the total current goes over 10A, the fuse is designed to blow and cut power to help prevent overheating and damage.
  • Extra safety design. The listing mentions safety shutters and a plug lock system.
  • Compact size. The listing gives dimensions of about 2.9 x 2.07 x 2.15 inches and a weight of 5.1 ounces.
  • Warranty. EPICKA lists a 2-year limited warranty.
  • In the box. 1 travel adapter and 1 user manual. The listing notes that a storage bag is not included.

One important note: this is not a voltage converter. EPICKA says it may not be suitable for high-power appliances like hair dryers, curling irons, and straighteners.

How to use it

Before you leave, look up the plug type used at your destination. When you arrive, slide out the matching plug, lock it in place, and plug the adapter into the wall. Then connect your devices to the USB-C and USB-A ports, or plug a device charger into the AC socket.

Some packing ideas:

  • Bring one adapter instead of a separate charger for every device.
  • Keep your phone, earbuds, and watch charging overnight from one outlet.
  • Pack the spare fuse somewhere you'll remember, in case you ever need it.
  • Leave high-power hot tools at home, or bring dual-voltage versions with the right plug.

Since there's no storage bag in the box, a small pouch or packing cube keeps it together with your cables.
