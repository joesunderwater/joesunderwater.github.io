Over-ear noise-cancelling headphones are the one gadget that earns a spot in both the work bag and the carry-on. They have to handle back-to-back calls, open-plan office noise, and long flights without getting uncomfortable. That is why the Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones (2nd Gen) landed on the White Collar Electronics board: Bose built them around its noise cancellation, a spatial audio mode, and a set of call and USB-C features aimed at people who live on headsets.

The photo on this page is a lifestyle image for mood and styling inspiration. It is not a photograph of these headphones. For official product photos and the color options, use the Amazon listing linked below. The listing this pin points to is the Driftwood Sand limited-edition color.

Who it's for

These are aimed at the person who spends a big part of the day with headphones on: commuters, frequent flyers, and anyone whose calendar is full of video meetings. Bose describes them as its best noise cancellation for headphones, with an Aware Mode for the moments you need to hear the room or a gate announcement.

They also fit if you watch a lot of video on the go. The new Cinema Mode is designed for movies and shows, and the USB-C connection lets you use the built-in mic for calls and video conferencing while plugged in. If you want one pair of headphones for the office, the plane, and the couch, that combination is the pitch.

Key features

The details below come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • Three listening modes. Quiet Mode for full noise cancellation, Aware Mode to hear more of your surroundings, and Immersion Mode, which combines noise cancellation with Bose Immersive Audio.
  • Spatialized audio. Bose Immersive Audio is designed to move the sound out of your head and place it in front of you.
  • Cinema Mode. New for this generation, it spatializes and balances background sound and effects for video content.
  • USB-C audio and voice. You can use the built-in mic for two-way calls and video conferencing while connected over USB-C, and you can charge with the included USB-C cord while you keep listening.
  • Battery. Bose lists up to 30 hours of play time, or up to 23 hours with Immersive Audio on.
  • Call microphones. Noise-rejecting mics focus on your voice, and AI-based noise suppression is designed to filter out surroundings and wind.
  • Comfort-focused design. Plush ear cushions and a metal headband designed to spread pressure evenly for all-day wear.

How to use it

On a workday, use Quiet Mode at your desk to cut background chatter, then switch to Aware Mode when you need to talk to someone without taking the headphones off. If you take calls from a laptop, the USB-C connection lets the headphones work as a wired headset with the built-in mic, which is useful when you also want to top up the battery during the day.

On a travel day, start with a full charge. If battery life matters more than the spatial effect on a long trip, keep Immersive Audio off, since Bose lists more play time without it. Save Cinema Mode for the in-flight movie or a show on your tablet.

At home, Immersion Mode is the setting to try for music when you want both noise cancellation and the spatial effect.

Quick FAQ

How long does the battery last?

Bose lists up to 30 hours of play time, or up to 23 hours with Immersive Audio on. Actual time depends on volume and the features you use.

Can I use them for video calls?

Yes. The headphones have built-in noise-rejecting microphones, and Bose says you can use the mic for calls and video conferencing while connected over USB-C.

Can I hear my surroundings without taking them off?

Yes. Aware Mode lets in more of the sound around you, so you can hear announcements or a quick question without removing the headphones.

What is Cinema Mode?

It is a listening mode added in this generation for video. Bose says it spatializes and balances background sound and effects for a more movie-like experience.
