How to use it

1. Assign the cubes. Give each person a cube, or sort by category: tops, bottoms, sleepwear, outerwear, and extras. 2. Pack and zip. Fold or roll clothing into each cube and zip it closed. 3. Compress. Use the compression to squeeze the cube down and make room in the suitcase. 4. Balance the load. If one bag is close to the weight limit, move a cube to another bag. 5. Unpack in one move. At the hotel, put each cube in a drawer and you're settled.

Packing ideas:

  • One cube per kid. Kids can find their own pajamas and swimsuits without emptying the suitcase.
  • Outerwear cube. Use one large cube for a sweater or light jacket that would otherwise eat up space.
  • Split checked bags. On family trips, spread cubes across bags so a delayed suitcase doesn't hold all of one person's clothes.
  • Laundry on the way home. Fill one cube with worn clothes to keep them apart from clean items.

Compression saves space, not weight, so keep an eye on airline weight limits.
