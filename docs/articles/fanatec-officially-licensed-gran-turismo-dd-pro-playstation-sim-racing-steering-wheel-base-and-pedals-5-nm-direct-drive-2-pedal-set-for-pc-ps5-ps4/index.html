If Gran Turismo 7 is the reason you want a better wheel, an officially licensed direct-drive kit removes a lot of guesswork. The Fanatec Gran Turismo DD Pro combines a 5 Nm FluxBarrier direct-drive base, a steering wheel designed by Polyphony Digital, and a two-pedal set, with native support for PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, and PC.

Who it's for

This bundle is for racers who want a plug-and-play direct-drive wheel for Gran Turismo 7, and who also want that same base to work on PC. Fanatec presents it as a compact, officially licensed entry into direct drive: enough torque for detailed feedback, a wheel laid out for PlayStation and GT, and a table clamp in the box.

It is a fit if you prefer a complete official kit over mixing brands, and if a two-pedal set (throttle and brake) is acceptable at first. A clutch and a load-cell brake are available later as official upgrade kits, sold separately. If you need 8 Nm of peak torque, that also requires the optional Boost Kit 180, which is not included in the standard 5 Nm bundle.

Key features

Details below come from Fanatec’s official Gran Turismo DD Pro (5 Nm) bundle page:

  • Officially licensed PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4 direct-drive wheel base, also compatible with PC and major racing games through Fanatec’s SDK.
  • 5 Nm peak torque as standard, with an optional Boost Kit 180 (sold separately) that unlocks 8 Nm. The motor uses patented FluxBarrier technology and a carbon-fiber-reinforced steering axis. The aluminum housing is a fanless passive heatsink.
  • FullForce, a low-latency force-feedback protocol added by firmware update, described as delivering fine detail through motor frequency control. Fanatec notes that a current firmware version is required.
  • QR2 quick-release system, preinstalled on this bundle, for a tighter wheel-to-base connection than earlier QR designs.
  • A 280 mm wheel designed by Polyphony Digital, with a rubber grip, a blue center stripe, ergonomic shifter paddles, a white OLED display, a diffused RevLED strip, PlayStation buttons, and a set of 5-way directional sticks.
  • A two-pedal set in heavy steel, with contactless 12-bit Hall sensors on throttle and brake, a stiffer brake spring, and a PU foam damper for a more progressive brake feel. Pedal spacing and face height are adjustable. Optional clutch, load-cell, and metal-face kits are sold separately.
  • A table clamp for desks 5 to 60 mm thick, plus T-nut rails for hard-mounting to a cockpit. Ports include USB-C, pedals, two shifter connections, and a handbrake.

How to use it

Clamp or hard-mount the base, attach the GT wheel on the QR2 release, and connect the pedals to the wheel base rather than directly to the console. Use the included USB-C cable and 5 Nm power supply, then install the current Fanatec app and firmware, including the update that enables FullForce if your unit is not already on that version.

In Gran Turismo 7, start with the standard Tuning Menu if you want a simple setup, then move to the Advanced Tuning Menu once you know which effects you want stronger or smoother. Direct drive is immediate, so begin at a moderate strength and raise it as the wheel and desk prove they can hold the torque. Practice brake modulation on the Hall-sensor pedal; if you later want a pressure-based brake, the official Load Cell Kit is the upgrade path.

The 280 mm wheel is sized for a wide range of GT cars, from road cars to race cars. If you add another Fanatec wheel later, remember that PlayStation compatibility depends on a PlayStation-licensed base, not on the wheel alone. A sturdy desk or cockpit is still required; 5 Nm on a light table will flex.

Quick FAQ

Does it work on PS5, PS4, and PC?

Yes. This is an officially licensed PlayStation wheel base, and Fanatec also lists PC compatibility with major racing games.

How much torque does it have?

The standard bundle is 5 Nm peak. The Boost Kit 180, sold separately, unlocks 8 Nm. That kit is not included in this 5 Nm package.

How many pedals come in the box?

Two: throttle and brake. Fanatec sells optional clutch and load-cell kits if you want a third pedal or a load-cell brake later.

Is the wheel officially designed for Gran Turismo?

Yes. Fanatec states that the steering wheel was designed by Polyphony Digital especially for Gran Turismo, with GT-oriented controls, an OLED display, and a RevLED strip.
