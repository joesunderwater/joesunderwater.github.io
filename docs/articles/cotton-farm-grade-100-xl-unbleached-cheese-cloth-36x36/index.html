How to use it

1. Rinse the cloth before first use if you like, then wring it out. 2. Line a strainer or bowl with the cloth, leaving extra fabric hanging over the edges. 3. Pour in what you are straining, such as yogurt, nut milk pulp, or stock. 4. Gather the corners and twist or squeeze to press out liquid, or tie the bundle and let it hang to drain. 5. Wash and dry the cloth so it is ready for next time.

A few practical ideas:

  • Thick, Greek-style yogurt. Let plain yogurt drain in the cloth to remove whey.
  • Nut milk. Blend soaked nuts with water, then squeeze through the cloth.
  • Clear holiday stock. Strain turkey or chicken stock for a smoother gravy or soup base.
  • Cut to size. Because it starts as a full yard, you can cut smaller squares for herb bundles or small jobs.
