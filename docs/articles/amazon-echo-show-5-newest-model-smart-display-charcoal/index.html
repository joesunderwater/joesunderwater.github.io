Who it's for

The Echo Show 5 suits anyone who wants a smart alarm clock and small display by the bed, or a compact helper in the kitchen. You can set alarms and timers by voice, check the weather before you get dressed, and play music or podcasts while you cook. If you already have compatible smart lights, a thermostat, or a video doorbell, it gives you one small screen to control and check them.

It's also a good fit for families who stay in touch with video calls, and for anyone who wants a rotating photo frame when the screen isn't in use. It makes an easy gift for a parent, a college student, or someone setting up a first smart home.

How to use it

On a nightstand, use it as your alarm clock. Ask Alexa to set an alarm, check the weather when you wake up, and set up a routine that starts your day, for example turning on compatible lights. When you're not using it, the photo slideshow turns it into a small picture frame. Close the camera shutter whenever you want extra privacy in the bedroom.

In the kitchen, set timers by voice, play a playlist or podcast while you cook, and glance at the screen for song titles or the news. If you have a compatible video doorbell, you can see who's at the front door without leaving the stove. For family check-ins, the 2 MP camera handles video calls with anyone who has the Alexa app or an Echo device with a screen.
