How to use it

Set the scale on a hard, flat floor and download the RENPHO Health app. Then follow the in-app setup to pair it over Bluetooth. The listing says setup takes minutes. Create a profile for each person who will use it, and connect Apple Health, Samsung Health, or Fitbit if you want the data shared there.

For the most consistent trends, weigh in at the same time of day under similar conditions, such as first thing in the morning. The electrodes need contact with your feet to read body composition, so step on barefoot. Then check the weekly and monthly views in the app. Single-day jumps matter less than the overall trend.

To weigh a baby or a pet, use Baby Mode. You weigh yourself, then weigh yourself holding them, and the app works out the difference.
