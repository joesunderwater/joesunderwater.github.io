How to use it

Drop one or more cloves into the chamber. You can leave the skins on if you want to save time, since the hole pattern is designed to push the garlic through and leave the skins behind. Squeeze the handles together over your pan, bowl, or cutting board, and the crushed garlic comes out the other side.

When you're done, flip the top handle over and use the built-in cleaner to push out the leftover pulp and skins. Give it a quick rinse, or put it in the dishwasher with the rest of the dinner dishes.

A few ideas for putting it to work:

  • Press garlic straight into warm olive oil for a quick pasta base.
  • Make a garlic butter for bread, steak, or roasted vegetables.
  • Add fresh garlic to vinaigrettes, marinades, and dips like hummus or tzatziki.
  • Press a big batch at once on meal-prep day.

Store it with your other prep tools so it's always within reach when a recipe calls for "two cloves, minced."
