How to use it

Start with clean, moisturized skin. L'Oreal says you can apply Lumi Glotion with your fingers, a makeup brush, or a beauty sponge. For the softest everyday look, warm a small amount between your fingertips and press it over the cheeks, forehead, and nose for a sheer tint.

If you already love your foundation but want more glow, mix a drop of Lumi Glotion into it on the back of your hand before applying. You can also wear it under foundation as a primer, or tap it over your base on the high points of the face: cheekbones, brow bones, the bridge of the nose, cupid's bow, and chin. For a summer-style glow, smooth a little over collarbones and shoulders. L'Oreal suggests setting it with a light dusting of translucent powder if you want the finish to last longer.
