How to use it

Pair it with your phone over Bluetooth, then set it in the middle of your group so the 360-degree sound reaches everyone. When you're outside, or the music is competing with wind and waves, tap the Outdoor Boost button. If you're listening to an audiobook or podcast on a drive or at camp, switch to Podcast Mode so voices come through more clearly.

At the pool or beach, the IP67 rating means you don't have to keep it away from the water, and it floats if it ends up in the pool. After a sandy beach day, the dustproof design helps. For a hike or a day trip, clip the loop to a backpack strap. Have a USB-C cable with you for charging, because one doesn't come in the box.

Have two? Press the Double Up button on both to link them. That's handy for spreading sound across a patio or for proper left/right stereo.
