Who it's for

Cabeau positions the Evolution S3 for airplane travel and road trips, especially long flights and overnight trips when you actually want to sleep. It's a good fit for travelers who struggle with neck strain in an upright seat and for passengers who tend to nod forward. Because it packs into a travel case, it also suits travelers who don't want a bulky pillow hanging off their bag.

How to use it

Before boarding, take the pillow out of its case and let it expand. Once you're seated, wrap it around your neck with the flat back against the seat and fasten the front clasps. Then loop the straps around the headrest wings and adjust them to the position that fits your height. That way, if you nod off, the pillow holds your head back against the seat instead of letting it tip forward.

On a road trip, you can use it as a regular neck pillow in the passenger seat. Between trips, remove the cover and wash it so it's fresh for next time, then pack the pillow back into its case and toss it in your carry-on.
