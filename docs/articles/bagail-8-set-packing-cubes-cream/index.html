How to use it

1. Sort by category or by person. Tops in one cube, bottoms in another, underwear and socks in a small one. For families, give each person a cube or two. 2. Roll or fold flat. Rolling soft clothing usually fits more into each cube and keeps creases down. 3. Zip and stack. Lay the larger cubes flat on the bottom of your suitcase and tuck smaller ones into gaps. 4. Unpack in seconds. At the hotel, move the cubes straight into a drawer or closet shelf so the suitcase stays tidy all week.

A few ideas beyond clothes:

  • Tech kit. Use a small cube for cables, chargers, and adapters.
  • Laundry cube. Keep one cube empty for worn clothes so they stay separate on the way home.
  • Day bag. Pull a single small cube out and toss it into a backpack or tote for a day trip.
  • Home storage. Between trips, the cubes can corral off-season clothes or kids' outfits in a closet.
