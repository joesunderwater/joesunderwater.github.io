Who it's for

This mouse is aimed at people who spend long stretches at a computer and want a shaped, thumb-rest mouse rather than a small travel or gaming model. Logitech presents the MX Master 4 as a comfort-first wireless mouse for work on Windows, macOS, Linux, Chrome OS, iPadOS, and Android, with deeper customization available through the Logi Options+ app.

It is a strong match if you work across more than one computer, edit long documents or timelines, or use a glass or glossy desk where a basic optical mouse loses tracking. This listing is the Graphite model. Other colors exist in the MX Master 4 family, including Mac-specific finishes, so confirm the exact variant on the Amazon page.

Key features

Details below come from Logitech’s official product and support specifications for the MX Master 4:

  • Ergonomic wireless mouse with a natural hand tilt, a thumb scroll wheel, and customizable haptic feedback on actions, shortcuts, and notifications. Vibration intensity can be adjusted in Logi Options+.
  • Actions Ring, a customizable on-screen overlay that places app-specific tools and shortcuts near the cursor.
  • MagSpeed electromagnetic scroll wheel that can move through long documents quickly or stop on a precise line, switching between ratchet and hyper-fast modes.
  • Darkfield sensor with 200–8,000 DPI, specified to track on a wide range of surfaces, including glass at least 4 mm thick.
  • Quiet clicks, which Logitech describes as 90% less click noise than the MX Master 3.
  • Wireless connection over Bluetooth Low Energy or a USB-C Logi Bolt receiver. You can pair and switch among up to three devices.
  • Rechargeable 650 mAh battery rated for up to 70 days of use, with about three hours of use from a one-minute charge. Logitech notes that a charging cable is not included in some packages, so check the current box contents.
  • Size is about 128.15 x 88.35 x 50.8 mm, and the mouse weighs 150 g. Logi Options+ is used for button customization and advanced features.

How to use it

Set up the Bolt receiver or Bluetooth pairing first, then install Logi Options+ if you want haptic patterns, Actions Ring shortcuts, and custom button maps. Pair a work laptop, a desktop, and a tablet if you switch among them during the day, and use the Easy-Switch control to move between those three connections.

The MagSpeed wheel is most useful in long pages, spreadsheets, and timelines: use hyper-fast mode to travel quickly, then let it settle into precise stepping when you need to stop on a line or frame. The thumb wheel is there for sideways scrolling or a shortcut you assign in the app. If your desk is glass, confirm the surface meets Logitech’s thickness note so the 8K sensor can track reliably.

Charge it over USB-C when the indicator shows a low battery. Because a one-minute charge is specified to cover a few hours, it can get you through a meeting, but a full charge is the better habit if you want to lean on the multi-week battery rating. Quick FAQ

Does it work on a glass desk?

Yes. Logitech specifies an 8,000 DPI Darkfield sensor that can track on glass, with a minimum glass thickness of 4 mm.

Can I use it with more than one computer?

Yes. It can pair with up to three devices over Bluetooth or the Logi Bolt receiver and switch among them.

How long does the battery last?

Logitech rates it at up to 70 days on a full charge, with about three hours of use from a one-minute charge. Actual life depends on use and features such as haptics.

Do I need Logi Options+?

Basic clicking and scrolling work without it. The app is required for advanced customization, including haptic settings and Actions Ring.
