How to use it

  • Charge it first. Fully charge the GB40 when it arrives and top it off regularly so it is ready when you need it.
  • Read the manual. NOCO's instructions are the final word on the exact steps for your vehicle. In general, you turn off the vehicle and accessories, connect the red clamp to the positive terminal and the black clamp to the negative terminal or a ground point, turn on the jump starter, and then start the engine.
  • Disconnect after starting. Once the engine runs, turn the unit off and remove the clamps, then let the vehicle run so its charging system can work.
  • Use the extras. Use the flashlight to find the battery terminals at night, and the SOS or strobe modes for roadside visibility. Plug in a phone if it is running low while you wait.
  • Store it sensibly. Keep it in its storage bag in the glovebox, under a seat, or in the trunk emergency kit.
