How to use it

Mielle's directions are straightforward: apply to the scalp or the ends, massage it in, then either leave it in or rinse after about 10 minutes with a cap on for deeper nourishment.

For a scalp routine, part your hair into sections and apply a few drops directly along each part, then use your fingertips to massage gently. A little goes a long way, so start small and add more only if you need it. For a pre-wash treatment, apply before shampooing, cover with a cap for around 10 minutes, then wash as usual. For ends, warm a drop or two between your palms and smooth it over the last few inches of hair.

Because the formula contains peppermint and menthol, expect a cool, tingly feeling. Keep it away from your eyes, and stop using it if you notice irritation.
