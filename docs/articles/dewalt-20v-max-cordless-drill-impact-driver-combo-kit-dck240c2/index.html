How to use it

Use each tool for what it does best:

  • Drill/driver for holes and careful driving. Use it for pilot holes, drilling through studs for shelf brackets, and driving smaller screws where you want more control, such as hinges or cabinet hardware.
  • Impact driver for long or stubborn screws. Deck screws, lag screws, and long construction screws are where the impact driver shines. The added torque helps sink fasteners without wearing out your wrist.
  • Use the LED. When you're working under a sink or inside a closet, the delayed LED stays on for a few seconds after you release the trigger, so you can line up the next screw.

Keep one battery on the charger while you work with the other so you always have a fresh pack ready. When the job is done, pack both tools, the charger, and the batteries in the bag so everything stays together.

For bits, check that your impact-rated driver bits fit the 1/4 in. hex chuck, and look at the Amazon listing for the full list of what is in the box.
