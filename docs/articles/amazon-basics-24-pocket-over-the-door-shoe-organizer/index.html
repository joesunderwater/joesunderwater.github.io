How to use it

Hang the three included metal hooks over the top of the door, then hook the organizer on and let it hang flat against the door. A closet door, bedroom door, or pantry door all work, as long as it is a standard residential door.

A few practical ideas:

1. Sort by how often you wear things. Put everyday shoes at eye or hand level and seasonal pairs at the top or bottom. 2. Use one pocket per shoe for bulky pairs. The listing's capacity assumes two pockets per pair of shoes, or one pocket per pair of sandals. 3. Go beyond shoes. On a pantry or laundry room door, the clear pockets can hold snacks, spray bottles, sponges, or gloves. 4. Give kids their own rows. Assigning a row per child makes it easier to find shoes on the way out the door.
