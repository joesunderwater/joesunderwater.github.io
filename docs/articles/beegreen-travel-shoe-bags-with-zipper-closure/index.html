How to use it

1. One pair per bag. Place shoes sole-to-sole, slide them in, and zip the bag closed. 2. Pack them at the edges. Lay the bags along the sides or bottom of your suitcase so they act as a base for folded clothes. 3. Tuck small items inside the shoes such as socks or a charger before bagging, which saves space. 4. Use a spare bag for laundry. On the return trip, a bag makes a handy place to keep worn clothes separate. 5. Wash after trips. Toss the bags in the washer when you get home so they are fresh for the next trip.

At home, the bags fit on closet shelves and shoe racks, so they can double as storage for formal shoes or seasonal boots.
