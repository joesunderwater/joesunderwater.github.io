The Ferrari FXX K is a track-focused machine most of us will only ever see in photos. The LEGO Technic Ferrari FXX K (set 42212) puts a buildable version on your desk instead. Who it's for

LEGO and Amazon list this set for builders ages 10 and up. It is a mid-size Technic build with real mechanical functions, so it suits:

  • Ferrari and supercar fans who want a model with authentic design details.
  • People who like how things work. You build the V12 engine with moving pistons and a working differential.
  • Display collectors who want an eye-catching red car on a shelf or desk.
  • Gift shoppers looking for a birthday or holiday gift for a husband, dad, or teen who loves cars.

If you already have a Technic car or two, this one adds a Ferrari to the lineup without taking over the whole room.

These details come from the Amazon listing and the official LEGO product page for set 42212:

  • 897 pieces in the set.
  • Opening butterfly doors, inspired by the real car.
  • Opening hood and an opening engine cover that reveals the V12 engine.
  • V12 engine with moving pistons.
  • Working differential you build and can test.
  • Ferrari red color scheme for a display piece that stands out.

How to use it

Treat it as a weekend project. Pour a coffee, open the bags in order, and follow the printed instructions or the LEGO Builder app's 3D view. The drivetrain steps are the fun part, because you can see how the differential and engine connect before the bodywork covers them.

When the build is done, try the functions. Swing the butterfly doors up, open the hood, lift the engine cover to show the V12, and roll the car to watch the pistons move. Then find it a home. It looks sharp on a desk, a bookshelf, a garage workbench shelf, or in a man cave next to other car memorabilia.

It also works as a shared project. Build it with a kid or a friend who loves cars, and talk through what the differential does as you go.
