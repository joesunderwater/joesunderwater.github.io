How to use it

Clip it in a front pocket or on a belt for everyday carry, or toss it in a glovebox, tackle box, or camping bin so it is always where you need it. The butterfly-style handles swing open to expose the needle nose pliers, which are useful for grabbing, bending, and pulling in tight spots. The wire stripper sits alongside them for quick electrical jobs.

With the handles closed, the outboard tools fold out one at a time. Use the flathead and cross drivers for loose screws on furniture, toys, and gear; the scissors for packaging and cord; and the blade for general cutting. Make sure each tool locks before you put pressure on it, and fold it back when you are done. The bottle and can openers make it a handy pick for tailgates and campsites.

Keep in mind that it includes a blade, so follow local carry laws and keep it out of reach of kids.
