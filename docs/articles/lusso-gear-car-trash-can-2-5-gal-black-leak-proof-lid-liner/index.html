How to use it

  • Pick a spot. Hang it on the back of the front seat for back-seat passengers, or on the front seat where the driver can reach it. Make sure it does not interfere with seat adjustment or passengers' legs.
  • Add a bag if you like. Clip a disposable bag onto the hooks for quick, no-touch emptying, or use the removable liner on its own.
  • Empty it often. Pull out the liner or bag when you fuel up or get home, so odors do not build up.
  • Use the pockets. Store napkins, wet wipes, spare bags, and hand sanitizer in the side and front pockets, so cleanup supplies are always next to the trash.
  • Rinse the liner. If a drink spills inside, lift out the waterproof liner and wipe or rinse it clean before putting it back.
