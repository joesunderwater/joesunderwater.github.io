How to use it

Flip the probe open and the thermometer powers on. Insert the tip into the thickest part of the meat, away from bone, and watch the display. If you are reaching over a hot grill, use the lock button to hold the reading so you can step back and check it.

When you are done, wipe the probe and rinse the body under running water (it is IP65 rated, so do not submerge it), then fold the probe closed to switch it off. Stick it to the side of the fridge or a metal grill surface with the magnetic back, or hang it on a hook so it is ready for the next cook.

Beyond the grill, use it for checking oil temperature when frying, candy and caramel stages, bread, and liquids. If readings ever seem off after long use, the calibration function lets you bring it back in line.
