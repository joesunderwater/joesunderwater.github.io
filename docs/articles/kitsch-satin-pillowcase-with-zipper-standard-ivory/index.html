Swapping a cotton pillowcase for a satin one is one of the simplest beauty-sleep tips you will see on TikTok. The idea is that a smoother surface creates less friction against your hair and face while you sleep. The Kitsch Satin Pillowcase with Zipper is a popular take on that swap: a silky polyester satin case in a soft ivory color with a zipper closure that keeps your pillow from slipping out overnight.

Who it's for

This pillowcase is for anyone who wakes up with frizzy, tangled hair or creases on their face and wants an easy change to their nighttime routine. Kitsch presents satin as a luxurious alternative to silk, so it is also a fit if you want that smooth feel in a polyester satin fabric. It works well for people with curly, textured, or color-treated hair who want to be gentler on strands, and for anyone who simply likes a cool, soft surface to sleep on.

How to use it

Using it is as easy as changing any pillowcase: slide your pillow in and close the zipper so it stays snug. Because the zipper keeps the case from shifting, you are less likely to wake up with the pillow half-exposed.

To get the most from a satin pillowcase, pair it with a simple nighttime hair routine. Loose styles such as a low braid, a pineapple for curls, or a soft scrunchie at the crown keep pressure off your hair. Take off makeup before bed so the satin stays clean, and wash it regularly according to the care instructions on the label.

If you like the feel, a second case makes it easy to rotate one while the other is in the wash. Check the listing for pack options, sizes, and colors.
