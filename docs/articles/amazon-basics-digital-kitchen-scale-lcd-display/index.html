How to use it

Set the scale on a flat, steady surface and turn it on. Put your empty bowl or container on the platform, press tare to zero it out, then add your ingredient until you reach the weight you need. To add a second ingredient to the same bowl, press tare again and keep going. This "one bowl" method means fewer measuring cups to wash.

A few everyday ways to use it:

  • Baking. Weigh flour, sugar, and butter in grams for more repeatable cakes, cookies, and bread.
  • Coffee. Weigh beans before grinding, or weigh water for pour-over brewing.
  • Meal prep. Divide cooked rice, chicken, or vegetables into even portions for the week.
  • Liquids. Switch to fluid ounces or milliliters when a recipe gives liquid amounts.

Because it is slim, you can store it in a drawer or on its side next to other appliances, then pull it out when you need it. Wipe the stainless platform clean after use.
