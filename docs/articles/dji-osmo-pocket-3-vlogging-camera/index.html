Who it's for

The Osmo Pocket 3 is aimed at vloggers and creators, but it works just as well for anyone who wants smooth video of trips, family events, or weekend outings without hauling a full camera kit. DJI's own examples include filming while dancing, chasing pets, or hiking, which tells you it is designed for movement.

It also suits people who want more control in editing. D-Log M and 10-bit color give you room to color-grade footage later, and the 4K/120fps mode lets you capture slow motion at high resolution. If you post vertical clips for social media, the rotating screen makes it easy to switch between horizontal and vertical shooting.

How to use it

For travel, keep the Osmo Pocket 3 in a jacket pocket or small bag so it is ready when something worth filming happens. Rotate the screen to vertical when you are shooting for Reels, Shorts, or TikTok, and back to horizontal for a longer travel video or a family edit.

For solo vlogs, set the camera on a tripod and turn on ActiveTrack 6.0. DJI says it keeps you center-frame while you move, so you can walk, talk, or demonstrate something without a camera operator.

For slow-motion shots, such as a golf swing, a wave breaking, or kids running, use the 4K/120fps mode. If you like to edit, record in D-Log M with 10-bit color and grade the footage later. It is especially useful for sunrise and sunset scenes, where DJI highlights the camera's strength.

Stereo recording handles everyday audio. For interviews or noisy places, check the Amazon listing for compatible accessories.
