Everybody knows the gap. It is that dark slot between the front seat and the center console where phones, keys, coins, pens, and stray fries go to disappear. Fishing something out of it at a red light is annoying at best and distracting at worst. Drop Stop is the original gap filler made famous on Shark Tank: a soft, flexible blocker that sits in that crevice so things land on the seat or console instead of under it. It earned a spot on the Car Accessories board because it is one of the smallest, simplest fixes you can make to a daily driver.

Who it's for

Drop Stop is for anyone who has ever dropped a phone or keys between the seat and console, which is to say nearly every driver. It makes particular sense for commuters who keep a phone close at hand, rideshare and delivery drivers who are in and out of the car all day, parents handing snacks around, and anyone who eats in the car. Drop Stop also pitches it as a way to reduce distracted driving, since you are not reaching down into the gap while the car is moving. The listing says one size fits most vehicles, from trucks to SUVs, but it is still worth a quick look at your own seat and console layout before ordering.

Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • Two gap fillers. The pack includes two Drop Stops, one for the driver side and one for the passenger side.
  • Attaches to the seat belt catch. Each filler has a built-in slot that slides over the seat belt catch, so it covers the gap in front of, around, and behind the buckle.
  • Moves with the seat. Because it is anchored to the seat belt catch, it moves when you slide the seat, so you do not need to readjust or reinstall it.
  • Neoprene casing. Drop Stop uses a high-grade neoprene casing that can contract or expand to fill different gap sizes.

  • Patented original. Drop Stop describes it as the original, patented seat gap filler as seen on Shark Tank.

How to use it

  • Install. Slide the filler down into the gap between the seat and the console, lining up the built-in slot with the seat belt catch so the catch pokes through. Push it down until it sits snugly along the gap.
  • Check the buckle. After installing, buckle and unbuckle the seat belt to make sure the buckle is easy to reach and latches normally.
  • Do both sides. Put the second filler on the passenger side, where bags, phones, and snacks tend to slip too.
  • Use the extras. Put the slide-free pad on the dash or console for sunglasses or a phone, and keep the LED card light in the glovebox or a door pocket for finding things in the dark.
  • Clean now and then. Pull the fillers out occasionally to wipe them down and vacuum the gap underneath.
