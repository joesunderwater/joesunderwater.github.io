How to use it

Select a 2-3/4-inch PTS tee and push it into the turf, a tee box, or a hitting mat that is designed for wooden tees. Use the color bar and printed marks as a guide so the exposed height stays consistent from shot to shot, especially when you are grooving driver or fairway-wood contact on a simulator. On the course, the same length and markings help you avoid teeing the ball much higher or lower than your usual setup.

Match the tee to the club. PTS exists so a 2-3/4-inch tee is easy to tell apart from shorter or longer sizes in the same family. If a mat does not accept wooden tees, use a rubber tee or a rubber tee holder instead of forcing a wood tee into a short-pile fairway mat.

Carry a few extras in the bag; wood tees break, and a 175-count pack is meant to last through a lot of range and course use.
