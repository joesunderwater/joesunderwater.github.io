If you mainly want to know how fast you swung and how far the ball should have flown, you may not need cameras, apps, or a membership. The PRGR HS-130A is a pocket-sized portable launch monitor with a built-in LCD. Turn it on, set it down, and it displays club head speed, ball speed, smash factor, and an estimated carry or total distance after the swing.

Who it's for

The HS-130A is aimed at golfers who want a simple, self-contained radar for the range, a speed-training session, or a quick check in the garage. It does not need Wi-Fi, an app, or calibration beyond placing it correctly. That makes it a practical companion to a hitting net or a SuperSpeed-style training routine, and it works for right- and left-handed swings.

It is not a full golf simulator. It does not measure spin, launch direction, or club path, and the distance figure is an estimate based on ball speed plus typical launch and spin for the selected club, not a complete ball-flight model.

Key features

Details below come from the official PRGR HS-130A user’s manual and PRGR product materials:

  • Portable launch monitor that uses 24 GHz K-band radar to measure club head speed and ball speed at the same time.
  • Built-in LCD shows club head speed, ball speed, smash factor, and estimated flight distance together. No app or phone is required.
  • Estimated distance can be switched between carry and total. Units can be switched between mph/yards and km/h/meters.
  • Club head speed can be measured on a practice swing without a ball. Ball speed, smash factor, and estimated distance appear when a ball is actually hit.
  • Estimated flight distance is calculated from measured ball speed together with an average launch angle and spin rate for the selected club. Actual carry can differ, and the device does not account for shot direction.
  • Up to 500 measurement records can be stored across modes.
  • Golf-mode measuring range for club head speed and ball speed is listed as 25 to 200 mph.
  • Powered by four AAA alkaline batteries (not included in the official box contents). PRGR estimates about one month of life at one hour of use per day, depending on conditions. Auto power-off is used to save batteries.
  • Dimensions are 55 x 116 x 48 mm (about 2.2 x 4.6 x 1.9 inches), excluding protrusions. Weight is 131 g including batteries. Operating temperature is 32°F to 100°F (0°C to 40°C).
  • Additional modes can measure baseball pitch and bat speed and other ball-sport speeds. Official box contents are the unit and the user’s manual.

How to use it

Install four AAA alkaline batteries, turn the unit on, and place it on the ground according to the manual so the radar can see the club and ball. Select golf mode, choose carry or total distance, and set yards or meters as you prefer. Hit a shot and read the four numbers on the screen. Use the history buttons when you want to scroll back through stored swings.

For speed training, make practice swings through the hitting zone without a ball to log club head speed only. When you are testing carry with each club, remember that the distance is an estimate, so use it to compare swings and clubs rather than as a guaranteed on-course number.

If you use it in a garage net, give the ball enough room to leave the club and pass the radar before it hits the net, following the placement notes in the manual.

Quick FAQ

Do I need an app?

No. Results show on the built-in LCD. There is no Wi-Fi or phone connection to set up.

Can it measure swing speed without hitting a ball?

Yes. Club head speed works on practice swings. Ball speed, smash factor, and estimated distance require an actual hit.

How accurate is the distance number?

It is an estimated carry or total calculated from ball speed and typical launch and spin for the club. PRGR notes that actual flight can differ and that direction is not included.

What batteries does it use?

Four AAA alkaline batteries, which are not included with the official package. PRGR estimates about a month of use at one hour per day.
