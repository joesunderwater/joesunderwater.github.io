How to use it

Clamping. Slide the movable jaw up against your workpiece, then squeeze the handle to tighten. Because each squeeze advances the jaw, you can hold the parts in place with one hand and clamp with the other. To release, press the quick-release lever.

Spreading. Convert the clamp to spreader mode when you need to push parts apart, like separating a stuck joint or holding a frame open. Follow the instructions included with the clamp to reverse the jaw.

Doubling up. Since this pack gives you two clamps, you can join them end to end for a workpiece wider than 6 inches.

A few tips for glue-ups: use just enough pressure to close the joint, since overtightening can squeeze out too much glue. Wipe away squeeze-out before it dries, and keep the pads clean so they don't mark the next project.
