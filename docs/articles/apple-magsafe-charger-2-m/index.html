Who it's for

This charger is aimed at iPhone owners with iPhone 12 or later, where the MagSafe magnets line the charger up automatically. If you have a newer iPhone and Apple's 30W USB-C Power Adapter, you get Apple's fastest wireless charging spec for this charger.

It also suits households with mixed Apple gear. Apple says it can wirelessly charge iPhone 8 or later and AirPods with a wireless charging case, the same way you would use any Qi2 or Qi-certified charger. And if your outlet is behind the bed or under a desk, the 2-meter cable is the reason to pick this version.

How to use it

Plug the USB-C end into a power adapter — Apple's 30W USB-C Power Adapter is the one referenced for the fastest charging figures — and bring the charger to the back of your iPhone. The magnets snap it into place, and charging begins.

On a nightstand, the 2-meter length gives you room to run the cable from a low outlet up to the bedside table and still pick up your phone to check messages without unplugging anything. At a desk, you can route the cable behind a monitor and keep the puck within arm's reach.

For AirPods, set a wireless charging case on the charger the way you would with any Qi charger. With older iPhones that support Qi but not MagSafe, the charger still works; you just will not get the magnetic snap or the MagSafe charging speeds.

Because the box does not include a power brick, check what you already own before you order. A USB-C adapter you already have may work, but charging speed depends on the adapter.

Quick FAQ

Which iPhones does it work with?

The magnetic MagSafe attachment works with iPhone 12 or later. Apple also says it can charge iPhone 8 or later as a standard Qi2 or Qi charger.

How fast does it charge?

Apple lists faster wireless charging up to 25W, with up to 50 percent in around 30 minutes for iPhone 16 and iPhone 16 Pro when used with the 30W USB-C Power Adapter. Results vary.

Does it include a power adapter?

No. The box includes only the charger with its integrated USB-C cable. The power adapter is sold separately.

Can I charge AirPods with it?

Yes. Apple says it works with AirPods models that have a wireless charging case.
