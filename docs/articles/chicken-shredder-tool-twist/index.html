How to use it

The listing is clear that this tool is for cooked meat only. Raw meat can damage the spikes. Cook the meat first and cut it into small pieces. The brand suggests dividing the pieces into about three batches rather than loading it all at once. Put a batch in, close the lid, and twist. Keep twisting until it looks the way you want through the clear lid, then open it and move the meat to a bowl.

A few practical ideas:

1. Weekly meal prep. Shred a batch of chicken breasts for salads, bowls, and wraps. 2. Pulled pork. Shred a cooked pork shoulder in batches for sandwiches. 3. Taco and enchilada fillings. Twist more for finer shreds and less for chunkier pieces. 4. Pet food. The brand mentions finely shredding chicken for pets.
