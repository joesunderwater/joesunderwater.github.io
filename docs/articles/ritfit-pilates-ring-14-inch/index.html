How to use it

For inner thighs, lie on your back with your knees bent and the ring between your knees or ankles. Squeeze slowly, hold for a moment, and release with control. For outer thighs, put your legs inside the ring and press outward against the pads.

For arms and chest, hold the ring in front of your chest with a palm on each pad. Press inward, then release. Try it overhead or low in front of your hips for variety. For core work, hold the ring between your hands or ankles during crunches, roll-ups, or leg lowers to keep your muscles engaged.

Start with slow, controlled reps rather than hard, fast squeezes. Use the included workout guide for ideas. Store the ring in its bag when you travel.
