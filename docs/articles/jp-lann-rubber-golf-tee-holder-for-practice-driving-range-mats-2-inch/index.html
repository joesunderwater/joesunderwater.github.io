Many hitting mats give you a rubber tee or a tight turf lie, but not a way to set a wooden tee at the height you use on the course. The JP Lann 2-inch rubber golf tee holder is a small adapter that sits in a practice or driving-range mat and accepts a wooden tee, so you can raise or lower the ball for driver and woods practice in a simulator bay or at the range.

Who it's for

This holder is for golfers whose practice mat has a standard tee hole, or can accept a rubber tee insert, and who want to use a real wooden tee at a chosen height. It is a useful add-on to a home golf simulator mat, a garage hitting strip, or a range mat that otherwise only offers a fixed rubber tee.

It is not a full tee or a hitting mat. The purchase includes one rubber holder only; the ball, mat, and wooden tee are not included. If your mat has no place for a rubber tee holder, this accessory will not have anywhere to sit.

How to use it

Press the 2-inch rubber holder into the tee hole on your practice or driving-range mat so it sits securely and roughly flush with the surrounding surface. Insert a wooden tee into the holder and adjust the exposed height to match the tee height you use on the course. Hit driver or woods off that tee, then reset the wooden tee as needed.

If the holder sits too high or too low for your mat, check whether the 1.5-inch version is a better depth rather than forcing this size. Do not use it as a stand-alone tee on bare floor or turf; it is meant to be seated in a mat. Keep a few wooden tees nearby, because the wood is still a consumable.

When you practice irons off the turf, remove the wooden tee or the holder if it is in the way of your hitting spot. The goal is to make mat practice feel more like a teed-up shot, not to leave an insert where you want to hit off the surface.
