How to use it

Unroll the mat and let it air out for a day or two if you notice a new-foam smell. Lay it on a flat floor. The brand says the thick foam is comfortable on wood, tile, or concrete.

Use it for gentle yoga flows, mat Pilates, stretching after a run, or bodyweight moves like planks, bridges, crunches, and bird dogs. The cushioning is especially nice for kneeling poses and anything that puts pressure on your knees or hips.

After a sweaty session, wipe it down with a damp cloth and let it dry before rolling it up. Then cinch the strap around it and stand it in a closet corner or slide it under the bed.
