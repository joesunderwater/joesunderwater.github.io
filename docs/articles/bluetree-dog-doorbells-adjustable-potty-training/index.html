How to use it

Hang the strap on the doorknob of the door you actually use for potty breaks, and adjust the snaps so the bells sit where your dog can reach them. For the first few trips, gently tap the bells yourself, say your cue, and take your dog straight outside. Reward a successful potty break. Over time, lure your dog to bump the bells before the door opens, then wait for them to offer the ring on their own.

Keep the training consistent. If the bells ring, go to the door. If it was a false alarm, still step outside briefly so the sound does not turn into a game for attention. Move the strap if you change doors, and make sure it cannot get pinched in the door itself.
