How to use it

  • Assign jobs. With twelve towels, you can keep a few for drying, a few for wax and sealant removal, a couple for glass, and the rest for interiors and door jambs. Keeping paint towels separate from dirty-job towels helps protect the finish.
  • Drying. After rinsing, lay a towel flat on the panel and pull it across gently instead of scrubbing.
  • Buffing wax or sealant. Once the product has hazed according to its own directions, fold the towel into quarters and buff with light pressure, flipping to a clean side as it loads up.
  • Glass and screens. Use a clean, dry towel with your glass cleaner for streak-free windows, mirrors, and infotainment screens.
  • Washing the towels. Toss them in the washing machine after use so they are ready for next time. Check the care guidance on the listing or packaging for any washing specifics.

Keeping one or two folded towels in the glovebox or trunk is also handy for wiping off bird droppings, smudges, or a foggy mirror while you are out.
