Marshall made its name on stage amplifiers, and the Emberton III carries that look into something you can toss in a bag. It made the White Collar Electronics board because it pairs the classic Marshall grille and script with practical features: 360° sound, a rugged IP67 build, and a battery Marshall rates at more than 32 hours. It is built for the patio, the garage, the beach, or the back seat on a road trip.

The photo on this page is a lifestyle image for mood and styling inspiration. It is not a photograph of the Emberton III. For official product photos, use the Amazon listing linked below. This ASIN is the Black & Brass colorway.

Who it's for

Marshall says the Emberton III is not for display, but meant to go wherever the party ends up. It suits anyone who wants a compact speaker that can handle dust, dirt, rain, and an accidental dunk, while still looking good on a shelf at home.

It is also a strong gift idea for someone who loves music and appreciates classic design, since the textured sleeve, metal grille, and Marshall script reference the brand’s amps. The built-in microphone makes it useful for hands-free calls too.

Key features

Details below come from Marshall’s official product page and the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • Marshall signature sound with Dynamic Loudness, which adjusts tonal balance so music stays balanced at different volumes.
  • True Stereophonic: Marshall’s multi-directional, 360° stereo sound designed to fill a space so there is no single sweet spot.
  • Drivers and amplification: Marshall lists two 2-inch 10 W full-range drivers, two passive radiators, and class D amplification.
  • 32+ hours of portable playtime on a single charge. Marshall says a 20-minute charge gives about 6 hours of playtime, and a full charge takes about 2 hours.
  • IP67 dust and waterproof rating: it can be submerged in 1 m (3 ft) of water for up to 30 minutes.
  • Built-in microphone for hands-free calls.
  • Bluetooth 5.3 LE, with Marshall listing range up to 100 m in free field, and Bluetooth LE Audio readiness for Auracast sharing.
  • Top-panel controls: a multi-directional control knob, power button, Bluetooth pairing button, and battery indicator.
  • Size and weight: about 6.3 x 2.7 x 3 in and 1.5 lb, per Marshall. A fastening strap is not included.

How to use it

Pair your phone with the Bluetooth button on top, then use the control knob for volume and playback. Because the sound is multi-directional, the speaker can sit in the middle of a table or patio rather than pointed at the listener.

For outdoor days, the IP67 rating means rain or a splash at the pool is not a problem. It is compact enough to fit in a backpack, golf bag pocket, or the car door. For longer trips, top it up before you leave; Marshall’s quick-charge figure means a short charge can still buy several hours of music.

At home, the Black & Brass finish fits a garage workshop, a desk, or a bookshelf. If someone calls while music is playing, the built-in mic lets you answer without grabbing your phone.

Quick FAQ

How long does the battery last?

Marshall rates it at 32+ hours of portable playtime per charge. Actual time depends on volume and usage.

Is it waterproof?

It carries an IP67 dust and waterproof rating. Marshall says it can be submerged in 1 m (3 ft) of water for up to 30 minutes.

Can I take calls on it?

Yes. The Emberton III has a built-in microphone for hands-free calls.
