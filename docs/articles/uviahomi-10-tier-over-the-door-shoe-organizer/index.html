How to use it

1. Measure your door. Make sure it's between 1.37 and 1.98 inches thick, and check that the door still closes with the hooks over the top. 2. Hang it. Place the four metal hooks over the top of the door and let the organizer hang flat. 3. Load from the bottom up. Put heavier boots and sneakers in the lower pockets and lighter flats, sandals, or kids' shoes up top. 4. Sort by person or season. Give each family member a few rows, or keep everyday pairs at eye level and off-season shoes higher.

On a pantry or bathroom door, the pockets can hold snacks, rolled towels, or toiletries instead.
