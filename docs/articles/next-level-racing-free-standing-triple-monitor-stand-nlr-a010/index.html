A single screen shows you the road ahead, but it hides most of what is happening beside you. Triple screens widen the view so you can judge corners, track cars alongside you, and feel more inside the cockpit. The Next Level Racing Free Standing Triple Monitor Stand (NLR-A010) is a floor-standing frame built to hold three displays around a racing or flight sim setup, rather than balancing them on a desk.

Who it's for

This stand is aimed at sim racers and flight simmers who already have, or plan to buy, a cockpit or wheel stand and want a triple-screen view. Next Level Racing describes it as the piece that finishes off a racing simulator setup, and it lists the stand as suitable for all Next Level Racing cockpits and stands.

Because it stands on its own feet, it is also a fit if you want to place your screens exactly where you like: directly behind the wheel or further back from the cockpit. Next Level Racing notes that the free-standing design with lockable wheels also suits events, trade shows, and sim racing centers where setups get moved around.

It is a large, heavy piece of equipment, so it makes the most sense if you have dedicated floor space for a sim rig. Check your monitor sizes and VESA pattern against the listing before you buy.

How to use it

Start with your seat and wheel (or yoke) in their final position. Assemble the stand, then roll it into place on the casters so the center screen sits directly in front of you at a comfortable distance. Lock the wheels, or swap to the height-adjustable feet if the stand is going to stay put.

Mount the center screen first using the bolts that fit your monitor's VESA pattern, then add the two side screens. Set the height so your eye line falls near the middle of the center display, and angle the side screens inward until the edges line up and the bezels sit as close together as possible. Small changes to tilt and angle make a big difference to how natural the wraparound view feels.

Route your display and power cables along the frame with the included Velcro ties so nothing hangs near the pedals or your seat rails. Once the screens are in place, set up your triple-screen field of view in your PC graphics settings and inside each game, since most sims need their own triple-screen or field-of-view adjustment.
