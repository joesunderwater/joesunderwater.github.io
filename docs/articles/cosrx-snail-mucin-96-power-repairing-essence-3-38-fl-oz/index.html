When TikTok talks about "glass skin," COSRX Snail Mucin 96% Power Repairing Essence tends to come up fast. It is a Korean skincare essence with a silky, slightly stretchy texture, made with 96.3% snail secretion filtrate. Who it's for

This essence is for anyone who wants an extra hydrating layer in their routine without a heavy feel. COSRX positions it for dehydrated and dry-feeling skin and for people looking to reduce the look of dullness. It also suits K-beauty fans who like layering thin products between toner and moisturizer.

COSRX says the essence is dermatologist-tested and that skin irritation and patch testing support its use on sensitive skin. Even so, the brand recommends doing your own patch test on the body first, then using it on your face only if no irritation occurs within 24 hours.

Key features

Details below come from the Amazon listing for this item:

  • 96.3% snail secretion filtrate: The key ingredient, which contains mucin and is described as an ingredient for enhanced moisturization.
  • Lightweight texture: Absorbs quickly and gives a natural, healthy glow, with long-lasting hydration and no heavy feeling, per the brand.
  • Soothing and hydrating: COSRX says it soothes dehydrated skin and improves skin vitality by reducing dullness.
  • Short ingredient list: Snail secretion filtrate, betaine, butylene glycol, 1,2-hexanediol, sodium polyacrylate, phenoxyethanol, sodium hyaluronate, allantoin, ethyl hexanediol, carbomer, panthenol, and arginine.
  • Ethically sourced mucin: COSRX says no snails are harmed and the mucin is collected in a safe, environmentally friendly way.
  • Size: 3.38 fl oz.

How to use it

COSRX's directions: after cleansing and toning, apply a small amount over your entire face, gently pat with your fingertips to help it absorb, then follow with your moisturizer.

In practice, that means it slots into the middle of your routine. Cleanse, use your toner, then press in the essence while your skin is still slightly damp. Let it sink in for a moment before applying your moisturizer and, in the morning, your sunscreen. Because the texture is thin, a small amount covers the whole face; you can add a second thin layer on drier areas if you like.

The listing notes it is for external use only and to avoid contact with eyes. Do the patch test first, and if irritation occurs, stop using it and follow the brand's advice to consult a doctor.
