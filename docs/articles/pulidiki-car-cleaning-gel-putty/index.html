How to use it

  • Press, don't wipe. Push the gel into vent slats, cup holders, and seams, hold a moment, then lift it out slowly. Fold it over on itself to bring a clean side to the surface.
  • Keyboards. Press it lightly on the keyboard surface and pull it away slowly to pick up dust between keys.
  • Skip screens. PULIDIKI says not to use it to wipe phone screens or computer monitors.
  • Check the surface. The surface must be water resistant, or the gel may stick to it. Try a small, hidden spot first if you are unsure.
  • Don't wash it. The listing says not to wash the gel with water. Return it to the jar after each use.
  • Know when to replace it. Once it turns black, it is done.

Gel works best on dust, crumbs, and loose debris. For sticky spills or stains, clean the spot with a cloth first, then use the gel on the seams.
