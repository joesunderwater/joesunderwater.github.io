How to use it

Drop the BlenderBall whisk into the bottle first. Pour in your liquid, using the embossed ounce or milliliter markings to measure. Then add your scoop of powder through the wide mouth. Putting the liquid in first usually keeps powder from sticking to the bottom.

Twist the lid on firmly, make sure the flip cap is closed, and slide the lock into place. Shake for several seconds while the whisk ball moves around inside. The rounded base helps the whisk reach every part of the bottle, so there are fewer clumps stuck in the corners. Press the button to flip the cap open and drink.

Rinse it soon after you finish so shake residue does not dry inside. When you are home, put it in the dishwasher, since the listing says it is dishwasher safe. Keep the sliding lock engaged whenever the bottle is in a gym bag.
