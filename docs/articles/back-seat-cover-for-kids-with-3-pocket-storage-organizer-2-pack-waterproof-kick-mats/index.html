If small kids ride in your back seat, you already know what the back of the front seats looks like: shoe prints, mud, juice drips, and scuffs from every pair of swinging legs. Kick mats are the easy fix. They hang over the backs of the front seats and take the dirt instead. This 2-pack from lebogner covers both the driver and passenger seats and adds three pockets on each for the small stuff kids want within reach. Who it's for

These are made for parents of young kids in car seats or boosters, grandparents who drive the grandkids, and anyone who carpools with little passengers. They also help if you want to protect leather or upholstered seat backs in general. lebogner says they adapt to all types of vehicles, but it is still worth checking that the 19 x 27 inch size suits your seat backs.

How to use it

  • Install on the front seats. Loop the top strap around the headrest and buckle it, then run the bottom strap around the base of the seat back and tighten until the mat sits snugly.
  • Check clearance. Make sure the mat and straps do not interfere with seat adjustments, seat-back controls, or how your child's car seat is installed. Follow your car seat manufacturer's instructions.
  • Load the pockets. Tablets, books, small toys, wipes, and snacks fit nicely in the pockets. Keep heavy or hard items out of the pockets so nothing becomes a projectile in a sudden stop.
  • Clean it regularly. Wipe off mud and spills, or remove the mat and wash it when needed, following any care tag.

They are also handy without kids, for example to keep dog paws or sports gear from scuffing the seat backs.
