How to use it

Fill the bottle through the wide opening with olive oil, avocado oil, or your usual cooking oil, then set it on the silicone pad by the stove. For a light, even coat, give the top a quick press to mist. For a measured pour, press slowly.

A few practical ideas:

1. Air fryer baskets. Mist the basket or food for even coverage without pooling. 2. Griddle and grill. The listing names griddle and BBQ use. Mist the surface before cooking. 3. Salads and roasted vegetables. A few sprays coat greens or sheet-pan vegetables lightly. 4. Track your oil. At 0.15g per full press, counting sprays is an easy way to keep portions consistent.
