How to use it

The listing's directions are simple: apply to damp skin in circular motions, then rinse thoroughly. In practice, that usually means cleansing first, then scooping out a small amount and massaging it over areas that tend to feel rough, like elbows, knees, and legs.

Because the listing calls out prepping skin before shaving, tanning, or moisturizing, a few easy routines follow:

  • Before shaving: exfoliate first, rinse, then shave as usual.
  • Before self-tanner: use the scrub a day or so ahead so you start with smoother skin, following your tanner's own directions.
  • Before moisturizer: pat skin dry after rinsing and follow with your favorite lotion or body oil to lock in that soft feel.

A practical tip for any sugar scrub: keep water out of the tub between uses by scooping with dry hands or a small spatula, and be careful on the shower floor, since oils from body scrubs can make surfaces slippery.
