How to use it

Heat guns get very hot, so read the user manual first; BLACK+DECKER points to it for safe operation. Then:

  • Stripping paint or varnish. Start on the lower setting, hold the gun a few inches from the surface, and move it steadily. When the finish bubbles and softens, scrape it away with a putty knife or scraper. Work in small sections.
  • Rusted nuts and bolts. Heat the nut briefly, then try your wrench. Heat can help break the grip of rust.
  • Frozen pipes. Warm the pipe gently and keep the gun moving rather than focusing on one spot.
  • Quick drying. Use the lower setting and keep a safe distance from the surface.
  • Cool down safely. Set the gun on its built-in stand after use and let it cool before storing it.

Keep the nozzle away from skin, cords, and flammable materials, work in a ventilated space, and don't leave it running unattended.
