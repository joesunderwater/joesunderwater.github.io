How to use it

  • Spread them around. Keep one where packages arrive, one in the garage or workshop, and one in the car, backpack, or toolbox.
  • Opening and locking. Flip the knife open with one hand and make sure the lock engages before you cut. Press the button to fold it closed when you are done.
  • Cutting. Cut away from your body and keep your other hand clear of the blade path, especially on thick cardboard or carpet.
  • Changing blades. When cuts start to drag, press the release, pull the old blade, and slide in a fresh standard utility blade. Dispose of used blades safely, in a blade bank or taped-up container.
  • Carrying. Close the knife fully before clipping it to a pocket or tossing it in a bag.

Because they take standard blades, you can pick up a pack of replacement utility blades and keep all three knives sharp for a long time.
