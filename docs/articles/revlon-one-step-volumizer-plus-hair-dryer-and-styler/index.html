The at-home blowout is one of the most rewatched categories on beauty TikTok, and hot air brushes are a big reason why. Instead of juggling a blow dryer in one hand and a round brush in the other, you use one tool that does both. The REVLON One-Step Volumizer Plus Hair Dryer and Styler is built around that idea: an oval brush that blows warm air so you can dry, smooth, and add volume in a single pass.

The photo on this page is a lifestyle image for mood and styling inspiration. It is not a photograph of the Revlon brush. For official product photos and current details, check the Amazon listing linked below.

Who it's for

Revlon positions this larger-barrel version for mid-length to long hair and for anyone who wants a classic, high-volume blowout. It suits people who find the two-tool round-brush method awkward, or who want a faster routine on busy mornings. Because you use it on damp hair, it replaces the separate dryer-plus-brush step rather than acting as a touch-up styler for dry hair.

If your hair is on the shorter side, note that the listing specifically calls this the larger-barrel option for longer lengths, so check whether a smaller barrel suits you better.

  • Dry and style in one step: Designed for damp hair, so you can dry, smooth, and volumize without a separate dryer and round brush.

How to use it

Start with damp, detangled hair rather than soaking-wet hair, and work in sections so the brush can grip each one. Place the brush at the root, give it a moment for lift, then glide it slowly down the length of the section. As you reach the ends, roll the brush slightly under or out to use the rounded edges for a curved, bouncy finish.

Use the High setting for the bulk of drying, Low for finer or more delicate sections, and Cool to help set the shape once a section is done. Clip finished sections out of the way so you do not re-wet them while working on the rest.

For a polished look, finish with a light smoothing product of your choice. Always follow Revlon's included instructions and safety guidance, and unplug the tool to cool before storing.
