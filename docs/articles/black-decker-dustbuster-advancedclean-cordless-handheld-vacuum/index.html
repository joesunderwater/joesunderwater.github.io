Who it's for

This hand vac suits anyone who wants quick, cordless cleanups: commuters who eat on the go, parents dealing with snack crumbs in car seats, pet owners fighting hair on upholstery, and people who want one small vacuum for both the car and quick messes at home. How to use it

  • Charge first. Set it on the charging station so it is charged before you need it.
  • Start with the floor mats. Pull the mats out, shake them, and run the vacuum over them, then do the carpet underneath.
  • Seats and seams. Pull out the crevice tool for the gaps between seats, the seat seams, and around the console. Rotate the nozzle to angle into tight spots.
  • Dust and upholstery. Flip up the brush for cloth seats, the dash, and door pockets.
  • Empty and clean. BLACK+DECKER recommends emptying the dust bowl after each use and cleaning the filter regularly to keep it performing well.
  • Keep it handy. Leave it on the charger near the door to the garage or driveway so quick car cleanups are easy.
