Who it's for

This kit is for pet owners who want a low-noise, cordless clipper and a full set of basic grooming tools in one box. It suits dogs and cats, including pets that get nervous around louder groomers. If your pet has very long or thick hair, the listing suggests a first pass with the included scissors before you clip, so the clipper is not fighting a heavy coat.

It is a home kit, not a substitute for a professional groom on a severely matted coat. Work in short sessions, keep the blade clean, and stop if you hit tangles that need to be worked out first.

How to use it

Charge the clipper before you start. Brush the coat out first so the clipper is not chewing through knots. Snap on a guide comb, hold the skin taut, and clip in the direction of the coat, moving slowly around legs, ears, and skin folds. For a long, thick coat, trim the bulk with the scissors first, as the listing suggests.

When a pass is done, pop the cutter head off and rinse it, then oil the blade as the manual directs. Do not soak or rinse the motor housing. Store the combs and scissors with the clipper so the next trim is ready to go.
