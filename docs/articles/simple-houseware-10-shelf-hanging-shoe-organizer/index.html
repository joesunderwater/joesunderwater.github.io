This organizer is aimed at people with narrow closets or limited floor space who still want shoes organized and easy to grab. How to use it

Unfold the organizer, then hang the metal dual-hook frame over your closet rod so the cubbies hang straight down. Because it is slim, it can sit at the end of the rod beside your hanging clothes.

Some practical ways to set it up:

1. One pair per cubby. Keep pairs together so you can grab and go. 2. Heavy items low. Put sturdier sneakers near the bottom and lighter flats or sandals higher up, and keep the total within the 20 lb limit. 3. Mix in accessories. Use a couple of top cubbies for beanies, gloves, or a small purse. 4. Fold it away off-season. Collapse it when you swap out your wardrobe or pack it for a move or trip.
