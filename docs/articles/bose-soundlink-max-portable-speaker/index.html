How to use it

Before people arrive, charge the speaker fully over USB-C and pair your phone over Bluetooth. Then download the Bose app so you can adjust settings without walking back to the speaker. Use the handle to carry it from the kitchen to the patio. With its IP67 rating, it can sit near the pool or on a boat deck.

If a guest wants to play something from a device without Bluetooth, use the AUX input and the included cable. If you already own a Bose smart soundbar or home speaker, try SimpleSync to play the same audio outside that you have playing indoors.

When you travel, pack the speaker in the bundled hard shell case to protect it. On long days, keep the battery spec in mind: Bose rates playtime at up to 20 hours, but volume and conditions affect how long it actually lasts.
