How to use it

PLANTIFIQUE's directions are simple. Clean the tools before the first use, then apply your favorite oil or serum to your face. Use the larger end of the roller on your neck, rolling outward and upward. Switch to the smaller end for the face, starting under the eyes and moving toward the hairline.

For the gua sha, scrape upward and outward, starting at the neck and moving to the jawline, cheeks, and forehead. Keep the pressure gentle and follow the contours of your face. The brand suggests a few minutes, once or twice a day.

A few practical notes from the listing: the tools are for external use only, should be cleaned after each use, and should not be used on broken or irritated skin. Keep them away from children.
