How to use it

Set the stand on a stable, level spot, place the fire pit on top, and keep a safe distance from your house, fences, overhangs, and anything flammable. Build the fire with dry, seasoned firewood, then let the airflow design do its work once the fire gets going.

When the evening is over, let the fire burn out and the pit cool completely. Solo Stove says the fire leaves only fine ash, and the removable ash pan and base plate make cleanup easier. For trips, pack it in the included carry case.

Solo Stove's safety guidance is simple: never leave a fire unattended, keep a safe distance from structures and flammable materials, and use appropriate fuel like seasoned firewood. Check local burn rules before you light up, too.
