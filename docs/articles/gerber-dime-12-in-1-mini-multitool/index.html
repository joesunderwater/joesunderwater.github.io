How to use it

Clip the Dime to your keys or a lanyard ring, or drop it in a pocket or pack. Because it weighs just 2.2 ounces, it disappears until you need it.

Use the spring-loaded pliers and tweezers for small, fiddly work like untangling fishing line, pulling a splinter, or bending a bit of wire. The scissors handle cord, thread, and tags. The retail package opener deals with stubborn clamshell packaging without reaching for a big knife. The flat head drivers take care of small screws, and the file helps smooth a rough edge. When the day is done, the bottle opener is right there on your keychain.

The Dime is meant for small tasks. For heavy prying or big jobs, use a full-size tool. Fold each tool back in after use so it carries neatly on your keys.
