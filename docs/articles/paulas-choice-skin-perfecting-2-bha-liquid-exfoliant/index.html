Who it's for

Paula's Choice describes this exfoliant as gentle enough for daily use on all skin types. The listing focuses on clogged and enlarged-looking pores, blackheads, uneven tone, rough or bumpy texture, and the look of fine lines.

It is a good fit if you:

  • Have been using a gritty scrub and want a non-abrasive alternative. The brand explains that a leave-on BHA is gentler on the face and neck than an abrasive scrub.
  • Deal with blackheads or congested-feeling pores and want a product that works inside the pore.
  • Prefer fragrance-free formulas.

If you have very sensitive skin or are using other active ingredients, start slowly and consider asking a dermatologist how to fit it into your routine.

How to use it

The listing's suggested use is simple: apply one to two times a day after cleansing, using clean fingers or a cotton pad, and do not rinse.

A practical routine might look like this:

1. Cleanse and pat skin dry. 2. Wet a cotton pad with the liquid and swipe it over your face, or press it on with clean fingertips. 3. Let it absorb, then continue with serum and moisturizer. 4. In the morning, finish with sunscreen, which is a smart habit any time you use an exfoliant.

If you are new to BHA, many people begin with a few evenings a week and build up from there as their skin adjusts.
