A dash cam is one of those things you hope you never need. When something does happen on the road, though, having video of what was in front of you and what came up from behind is a lot better than trying to remember it. The ROVE R2 DUAL records front and rear at the same time, uses a Sony STARVIS 2 sensor up front for low-light driving, and comes with a 128GB microSD card so you can get started right away. Who it's for

This setup fits daily commuters, new drivers, rideshare and delivery drivers, and anyone who wants a record of the road both ahead and behind. The rear camera is helpful for rear-end situations, and the GPS data adds location and speed context. If you want it to watch the car while parked, note that the 24-hour parking mode needs a hardwire kit, which is sold separately.

Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • Front and rear recording. The front camera records at 2.5K (QHD), and the rear camera records at Full HD 1080P. ROVE says front footage can be upscaled to 4K for playback, and is clear that this is upscaled, not native 4K.
  • Sony STARVIS 2 front sensor. The front camera uses a Sony STARVIS 2 IMX675 5MP sensor with an F1.5 aperture (F1.8 on the rear) and HDR for low-light and night driving.
  • Wide view. Amazon lists a 150-degree field of view for the front, and the listing mentions 140 degrees for the rear.
  • Loop recording, G-sensor, and emergency lock. Seamless loop recording keeps the card from filling up, and the G-sensor and emergency video lock help protect event clips from being overwritten.
  • Built-in GPS. It shows live speed and compass on the 3-inch IPS screen and stamps route, location, and speed data on the video. ROVE offers a free GPS Player for PC or Mac.
  • Dual-band WiFi and app. Built-in 5.8GHz and 2.4GHz WiFi works with the ROVE app on iPhone and Android to view, download (up to 20 MB/s, per ROVE), and share clips, change settings, and update firmware over the air.
  • Parking mode (hardwire kit required). Three options: 1 fps time-lapse, motion detection, and collision detection. The hardwire kit is not included.
  • Supercapacitor. ROVE uses a built-in supercapacitor for longer product life, plus thermal protection for high temperatures.
  • Storage. A ROVE 128GB microSD card is included, and the camera supports cards up to 1TB.
  • In the box. Front and rear cameras, 128GB card, dual USB fast car charger, power cable, data cable, rear cam cable, mounts, clips, trim tool, and cleaning wipe. The listing notes a 1-year warranty.

How to use it

  • Plan the cable route. Mount the front camera near the rearview mirror where it does not block your view, and tuck the power cable along the headliner and trim with the included trim tool.
  • Run the rear camera. Mount it at the top of the rear window and route the rear cable along the headliner to the front unit.
  • Use the included power parts. ROVE recommends its own power cable and a compliant 5V USB source, and says to inspect the cable and card for damage before use.
  • Set it up in the app. Connect over WiFi, set date and time, and check settings like loop length and GPS stamps. Check for firmware updates.
  • Save what matters. After an incident, lock or download the clip so loop recording does not overwrite it.
  • Want parking coverage? Add a compatible hardwire kit and set your preferred parking mode.

Check your local laws on windshield mounting and recording before you install.

Quick FAQ

Does it really record in 4K?

The front camera records at 2.5K and can be upscaled to 4K for playback. ROVE says plainly that it is not native 4K. The rear camera records at 1080P.

Is a memory card included?

Yes. The listing includes a ROVE 128GB microSD card, and the camera supports cards up to 1TB.

Does parking mode work out of the box?

No. The 24-hour parking mode requires a hardwire kit, which is not included.
