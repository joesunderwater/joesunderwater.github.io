Who it's for

The Go 2 is aimed at active guys who want handheld muscle relief without hauling a full-size massage gun around. Hyperice describes it as compact, lightweight, and travel-friendly, and the listing notes it is TSA approved for carry-on — useful if you train on the road or just want something to work out the kinks after a flight.

It is also a reasonable pick for anyone who shares a home or an office. Hyperice's QuietGlide technology is designed to keep noise down, so you can use it while music is playing or someone else is in the room.

How to use it

To power it on, press and hold the power button at the bottom of the handle and release once the LED lights illuminate. Then select your speed. Starting on the lowest setting and working up is a sensible approach, especially on sore or sensitive areas.

After lifting or running, use it on the big muscle groups — quads, hamstrings, calves, glutes, and upper back — moving slowly rather than parking it in one spot. On travel days, toss it in your carry-on and use it on your shoulders and legs after you land.

Because it will not run while plugged in, charge it between sessions. Keep the included USB-A to USB-C cable with it, and plan on using a standard 5V USB-A wall brick, since the box does not include one and USB-C to USB-C cables are not supported.

If you have an injury, a medical condition, or ongoing pain, check with a doctor or physical therapist before adding a massage gun to your routine.
