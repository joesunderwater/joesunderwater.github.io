Who it's for

These patches are for anyone who gets the occasional pimple, whitehead, or blemish and wants a simple spot step instead of a full routine change. Peach Slices lists them for dry, normal, oily, and combination skin, and describes them as hypoallergenic and safe for sensitive skin.

They are a good fit if you tend to pick at breakouts, since protecting the spot from picking is one of the main things the brand says they do. Because the patches are translucent, they also suit people who want to wear one during the day, on bare skin or under makeup, not just overnight.

How to use it

Peach Slices keeps the directions simple:

1. Cleanse and dry the area first, without applying other skincare products on that spot. 2. Choose a dot slightly larger than the blemish and press it on. 3. After about 8 hours, gently peel it off and throw it away. 4. Cleanse again and apply a fresh patch if needed.

The brand's suggested routine puts the patch right after cleansing, before toner, serum, and moisturizer, and says you can use them as often as you like. Many people put one on before bed so it can work overnight, but because the dots are clear, you can also wear one during the day and apply makeup over it.

Patches are not a substitute for a dermatologist if you have persistent or painful acne. If you have very sensitive skin, try one on a small area first.
