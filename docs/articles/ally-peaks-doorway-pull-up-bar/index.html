How to use it

Assemble the bar following the included instructions, then hook it over the top of your door frame so it sits against the trim. Before every workout, the listing says to tug down on the grips to make sure the bar is secure.

Start with what you can do. If full pull-ups are hard, try hanging holds, slow lowering (negatives), or chin-ups with palms facing you. Use the different grip positions to change the focus. You can also take the bar down and set it on the floor for push-ups and dips.

When you are done, lift it off the frame and store it in a closet or under the bed.
