How to wear it

For yoga or stretching, these are meant to be worn as a straightforward high-rise legging: close to the body, soft at the waist, with a hem that should land above the ankle. The gentle compression is the point if you do not like a very tight waistband. Because the waistband is described as seamless, it is intended to sit smoothly under a top.

For everyday wear, the same pair can go under an oversized hoodie, a long sweater, or a tucked tee — the usual cozy-fits and TikTok uniform. The 7/8 length leaves a little skin at the ankle, which works with sneakers, socks, or slides. If you are heading out rather than staying in, a longer tunic or oversized shirt can add coverage; if you are lounging, the high rise is what lets you wear a shorter top without the waistband folding down, provided the size is right.
