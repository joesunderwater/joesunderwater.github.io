How to use it

Commuting: switch on Active Noise Cancellation to quiet a train or bus, or let Adaptive Audio balance noise control for you as you move between environments. When someone starts talking to you, Conversation Awareness lowers your audio automatically.

At the gym: with the IP54 rating for dust, sweat, and water, they are suited to workouts. Try the different ear tip sizes to find the one that feels secure and creates a good seal, since the tips help keep out noise and hold the earbuds in place.

Calls and work: Voice Isolation is designed to make your voice clearer in loud places. Use Transparency mode when you need to stay aware of your surroundings.

Movies and music: set up Personalized Spatial Audio and play supported Dolby Atmos content for a more immersive listening experience.
