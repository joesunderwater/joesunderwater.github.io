Breville Bambino Plus Espresso Machine – Compact Stainless Home Barista

A full-size espresso machine can take over a counter. The Breville Bambino Plus is the company’s compact answer to that problem: a brushed-stainless home machine that is designed to reach extraction temperature in three seconds, pull a one- or two-shot espresso, and texture milk automatically for lattes and cappuccinos.

Who it's for

This machine is for people who want café-style espresso and milk drinks at home, but do not have space or patience for a large dual-boiler setup. Breville positions the Bambino Plus around its “4 keys formula”: a proper dose of coffee, precise temperature, the right water pressure, and true microfoam milk. The automatic steam wand is the feature that makes it friendlier than a fully manual machine, while a manual texturing option is still there if you want more control.

It is a good match for a small kitchen, an office nook, or any counter where width matters. Official dimensions are 11.9 x 7.4 x 14.3 inches. If you already grind coffee at home, you will still need a separate grinder; this machine does not include one.

Key features

Details below come from Breville’s official Bambino Plus (BES500) product page:

  • Compact espresso machine in brushed stainless steel, model BES500BSS in this listing.
  • ThermoJet heating system that reaches extraction temperature in 3 seconds. Breville also describes the digitally printed heating circuit as more energy efficient than a Thermoblock system in its internal testing.
  • 54 mm stainless steel portafilter, designed around an 18 g dose of coffee for a fuller shot. Single-wall and dual-wall filter baskets are included for 1-cup and 2-cup doses, so you can use it with freshly ground beans or a more forgiving dual-wall setup.
  • Low-pressure pre-infusion, followed by 9-bar extraction from a 15-bar Italian pump.
  • Automatic milk texturing with three milk temperature settings and three texture levels, plus hands-on manual texturing. Breville describes a powerful steam wand at 266°F for silky microfoam.
  • One-touch volumetric controls for 1-shot and 2-shot espresso, plus steam.
  • Included accessories: a 54 mm tamper, the Razor precision dosing tool, a 16 fl oz (480 ml) stainless steel milk jug, 1-cup and 2-cup single-wall and dual-wall filter baskets, a cleaning tool, and a cleaning disc.

How to use it

Fill the water tank, insert the portafilter basket that matches how you grind, and let the ThermoJet heat-up do its short warm-up. Dose about 18 g of coffee in the 54 mm portafilter for a standard double, level and tamp it, then use the 1-shot or 2-shot button for volumetric control. Dual-wall baskets are more forgiving if your grind is not yet consistent; single-wall baskets give more control once your grinder is dialed in.

For milk drinks, fill the included jug, choose automatic temperature and texture if you want the machine to build microfoam for you, or use manual texturing if you prefer to steam by hand. The automatic wand is the reason this model is called Plus: it can produce latte-art-ready foam without a long practice curve, while still leaving a manual option.

Keep the machine cleaner than you think you need to. Use the included cleaning disc and tool on Breville’s recommended schedule, and purge or wipe the steam wand after each milk drink. Because the footprint is compact, give it a little clearance for the jug and for emptying the drip tray. Check the Amazon listing for the current finish and accessory list before you buy.

Breville says the ThermoJet system reaches the optimum extraction temperature in 3 seconds, which is the main speed claim for this machine.

Yes. The Bambino Plus has an automatic steam wand with three temperature and three texture settings, plus a manual texturing option.

What size portafilter does it use?

A 54 mm stainless steel portafilter, designed around an 18 g dose, with both single-wall and dual-wall baskets included.

Official dimensions are 11.9 x 7.4 x 14.3 inches, which is why it is often chosen for smaller counters.
