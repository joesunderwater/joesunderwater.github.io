How to use it

1. Drop a liner into the basket or tray. No need to trim or fold it. 2. Add the food on top. The listing recommends putting enough food on the paper; a liner with nothing on it is light and can move around when the fan runs. 3. Cook as usual, staying within the 428°F temperature limit listed for the paper. 4. Lift out and toss. Grease and stains stay on the liner, so the basket usually just needs a quick wipe.
