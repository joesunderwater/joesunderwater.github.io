How to use it

Clear some space and stand on a non-slip surface. For a two-handed swing, set the kettlebell a little in front of you, hinge at your hips, grab the handle with both hands, hike it back between your legs, then drive your hips forward to swing it to about chest height. Let your hips do the work, not your arms.

For goblet squats, hold the bell by the sides of the handle at chest height and squat down between your heels. Single-arm rows and overhead presses are also good moves to add once you feel steady.

Amazon Basics recommends using it on rubber or padded surfaces and storing it apart from other weights to protect the finish. The listing also says to inspect the handle before each use and stop using it if coating damage exposes bare metal edges.
