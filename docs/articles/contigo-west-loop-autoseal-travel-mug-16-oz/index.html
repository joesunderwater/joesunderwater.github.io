How to use it

Brew straight into the mug. Since it fits under most single-serve brewers, you can skip pouring from a separate cup. Close the lid, lock the button if it's going into a bag, and head out. When you want a sip, unlock it and press the button. The lid reseals as soon as you let go.

For summer, fill it with iced coffee, cold brew, or iced tea to make use of the longer cold time. After use, open the lid for cleaning and put it on the top rack of the dishwasher. Wash the stainless steel body by hand.
