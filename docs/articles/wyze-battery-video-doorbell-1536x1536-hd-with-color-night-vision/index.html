Who it's for

Based on the listing, this doorbell is a good fit if you:

  • Rent, or have no doorbell wiring, and want a wire-free option.
  • Have existing doorbell wiring and prefer continuous power.
  • Get frequent package deliveries and want to see packages placed right against the door.
  • Want to talk to visitors from your phone when you are not home.
  • Would rather use local storage than pay for a cloud plan, or want the choice of either.

Key features

Details below come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • 1:1 head-to-toe view. A 150°×150° ultra-wide field of view captures visitors from head to toe and packages tucked against your door.
  • 1536x1536 HD+ video with color night vision. Wyze says its starlight sensor amplifies low light for color images after dark.
  • Wire-free or wired. Up to 6 months of battery life, or continuous power through AC hardwiring. The built-in battery recharges over USB-C using the included cable after you detach it.
  • Two-way audio and auto-responses. Press "talk" in the Wyze app to speak with visitors, or tap a quick auto-response.
  • Smart notifications and voice deterrence. Get alerts for visitors and package deliveries. The product title lists person, vehicle, and package monitoring. When enabled, the doorbell can tell visitors they are being recorded.
  • Local or cloud storage. Supports a microSD card up to 256GB (not included, FAT32 or exFAT only), or a Cam Plus subscription for 14 days of cloud video storage. In AC-wired mode, a Wyze microSD card enables 24/7 local recording.
  • Flexible chime options. Use a Wyze Chime Controller, Wyze Universal Wi-Fi Chime, or a Wyze Cam (all sold separately) to hear a chime anywhere in your home.

How to use it

1. Choose your power. Go wire-free on battery, or connect it to existing AC doorbell wiring for continuous power. 2. Set it up in the Wyze app. The listing describes a quick setup of about a minute. 3. Mount it at the door so the square view covers both faces and the doormat area where packages land. 4. Add a microSD card if you want free local storage, or subscribe to Cam Plus for cloud clips. 5. Pick a chime. Add a compatible Wyze chime or camera if you want to hear the doorbell inside. 6. Recharge as needed by detaching the doorbell and plugging in the included USB-C cable.
