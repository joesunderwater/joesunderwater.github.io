How to use it

Load 6 darts into the rotating drum. Pump the priming slide back and forward, aim, and pull the trigger to fire one dart. Repeat for each shot. When things get hectic, switch to slam fire: hold the trigger down and keep pumping the slide to send all 6 darts out quickly.

Keep the other 6 darts within reach so you can reload fast. If you want to customize it, the tactical rails and the stock and barrel attachment points work with NERF accessories, which are sold separately.

For a good game, set some ground rules before anyone starts firing: no shots to the face, clear the area of breakables if you are indoors, and pick up the darts when you are done. Hasbro recommends eyewear (not included), and the darts are a choking hazard for children under 3, so keep them away from little ones.
