How to use it

  • Vacuum first. Pull out the loose dirt so the brush can work on what is stuck in the fibers.
  • Brush dry to lift debris. On carpet and floor mats, short back-and-forth strokes help loosen sand and pet hair. Vacuum again afterward to pick up what you loosened.
  • Pair it with a cleaner for stains. Spray a carpet or upholstery cleaner that is made for your surface, let it sit as the cleaner's label directs, then agitate with the brush and blot or extract the residue. Test any cleaner on a hidden spot first.
  • Work in sections. Do one seat or one mat at a time so the cleaner does not dry before you agitate it.
  • Go easy on delicate fabric. Stiff bristles are great on carpet. On softer seat fabrics or headliners, use light pressure, or choose a softer brush.
  • Rinse and dry. Rinse the bristles after use and let the brush air dry before storing it.

Floor mats are the easiest place to start. Pull them out, brush them on the driveway, and you will see the difference quickly.
