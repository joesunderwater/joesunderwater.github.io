Who it's for

Based on the listing, this kit is a good fit if you:

  • Want to watch several spots at once, like the front door, driveway, backyard, and an indoor room.
  • Prefer not to drill holes. The magnetic base works on metal surfaces, or the cameras can sit on a flat surface.
  • Want to avoid monthly fees for storage or AI features.
  • Like the idea of facial recognition and tracking a person across cameras.

Key features

Details below come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • Magnetic, screw-free mounting. The magnetic base snaps onto metal surfaces, or you can set a camera on any flat surface, indoors or out.
  • Spotlight-free color night vision. eufy says its sensor shows true colors in darkness and can reveal details like faces and license plates without a spotlight.
  • HomeBase Mini included. It is the hub of the system and unlocks AI features like Facial Recognition and Cross-Camera Tracking.
  • No monthly fees. eufy says the one-time purchase includes everything, with no mandatory subscriptions for cloud storage or AI and no battery replacement costs.
  • Local storage. Footage is stored indoors on the HomeBase Mini, expandable up to 1TB. Each camera also has a microSD slot (up to 256GB, at least 32GB recommended, card not included).
  • Smart detection. AI detects people, vehicles, pets, and activity in zones, and recognizes faces, with instant alerts.
  • 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi for a stable connection.
  • Weather and power. The product title lists IP67 and Solar Panel Compatible.

Two compatibility notes from eufy: the cameras are not compatible with HomeBase 2, and you should update the eufy app to the latest version before setup.

How to use it

1. Update the eufy app on your phone first, as the listing recommends. 2. Set up the HomeBase Mini indoors and connect it to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. 3. Add each camera in the app. 4. Place the cameras. Snap them onto a metal surface or set them on a flat ledge, shelf, or window sill. Good starting spots are the front door, driveway, back door, and a main living area. 5. Set activity zones and detection types so you get alerts for what matters, like people and vehicles. 6. Turn on Facial Recognition if you want alerts that tell familiar faces from strangers.
