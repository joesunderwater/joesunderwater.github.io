How to use it

Charge the shaver fully before the first use, either in the SmartCare center or with the power supply. For a dry shave, move the head in the pattern that works for your face, and use the integrated ProTrimmer for longer hairs, sideburns, or a cleaner neckline. For a wet shave, you can use it in the shower with foam or gel, then rinse the head.

The ProComfort Head is a separate attachment for preparing the beard before the main shave. The SmartCare station is the daily maintenance step: park the shaver head-down so the contacts line up, and let the center run its clean, dry, and charge cycle. Replacement cleaning cartridges are sold separately and should be chosen to match this system.

For travel, use the case and the direct charger rather than packing the full SmartCare base unless you have the space. A 5-minute quick charge can cover a single shave if you forgot to charge overnight. Replace the shaving head on Braun’s recommended schedule, and read the manual for cleaning and wet-use limits.
