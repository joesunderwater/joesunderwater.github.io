Who it's for

AUVON says these lights are suited to bathrooms, hallways, stairs, bedrooms, children's rooms, and kitchens. They are a fit for families with kids, anyone who gets up during the night, and homes where a dark staircase or hallway is a trip hazard. With four in the box, you can cover several spots at once.

Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • Two sensor modes. The lights have a dusk-to-dawn light sensor and a motion sensor that work independently.
  • AUTO mode. The motion sensor detects movement over a range of 3 to 5 meters and 120 degrees. In the dark, the light turns on when it senses motion and turns off after 60 seconds without motion.
  • NIGHT mode. Only the light sensor is active. The light turns on when it's dark and off when it's bright, with no extra steps.
  • Adjustable brightness. Short-press for 5-step dimming, or long-press for stepless dimming from 1 to 50 lumens. If the light stays plugged in, it remembers your last brightness setting.
  • Warm white LED. The listing title describes them as mini warm white LED night lights.
  • Energy use. AUVON lists a maximum power consumption of 1.3W.
  • Safety design. The listing mentions a V-0 fire-resistant casing and over-current protection.
  • Compact size. The design leaves the second outlet free. The listing gives dimensions of about 2.17 x 2.17 x 1.5 inches.
  • In the box. 4 night lights, a user manual, and a 24-month warranty with technical support.

How to use it

Plug a light into an outlet where you need it: low in a hallway, near the bathroom door, at the top or bottom of the stairs, or in a child's room. Then choose a mode.

Use AUTO mode in spots you only pass through, like hallways and stairs. The light comes on when you walk by in the dark and turns off a minute later. Use NIGHT mode where you want a steady glow all night, such as a nursery or a bathroom.

Set the brightness to suit the room. A low setting is easier on sleepy eyes in a bedroom, while a brighter setting helps on stairs. Since the light remembers your setting while plugged in, you only need to set it once.

Quick FAQ

Do they stay on all night or only with motion?

Both options are available. NIGHT mode stays on from dusk to dawn, and AUTO mode turns on with motion in the dark and off after 60 seconds without motion.

Can I adjust the brightness?

Yes. The listing describes 5-step dimming with a short press and stepless dimming from 1 to 50 lumens with a long press.

Will it block my other outlet?

The listing says the compact design leaves the second outlet free for other devices.
