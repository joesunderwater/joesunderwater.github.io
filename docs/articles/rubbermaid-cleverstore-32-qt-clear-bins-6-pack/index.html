How to use it

A few ways to get the most from a matching set:

1. Sort before you fill. Group items by category (winter accessories, toys, crafts) so each bin has one job. 2. Label the front. Even with clear sides, a label on the short end makes a stack faster to scan. 3. Stack heavy on the bottom. Put the heaviest bins at the base and stay within the listed 18 lb capacity per bin. 4. Latch before stacking. Closing the lay-flat latches gives the next bin a flat surface to sit on. 5. Rotate seasonally. Keep off-season clothes in bins on a high shelf and swap them out when the weather changes.
