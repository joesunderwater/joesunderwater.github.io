A launch monitor is what turns a hitting net in the garage into something closer to a practice session with numbers attached. The Garmin Approach R10 is a compact, portable unit designed for the range, the backyard, or an indoor bay, and it pairs with the Garmin Golf app on a compatible smartphone to show club and ball data after each swing.

Who it's for

The R10 is a fit if you want a portable launch monitor you can move between a home simulator space and the driving range, without committing to a ceiling-mounted or cart-based unit. Garmin positions it for golfers who want more than a dozen metrics, a practice range in the app, and the option to play virtual rounds through Home Tee Hero when an active Garmin Golf subscription is in place.

It also suits people who like swing video with their numbers. The app can automatically record clips of each shot with stats overlaid, and the included phone mount clips to a golf bag or stands on the ground so you can check the Garmin Golf app between swings. You will need a compatible smartphone with the app installed; the device is not a standalone simulator computer.

How to use it

Charge the unit, attach the tripod, and place the R10 according to Garmin’s setup instructions, using the alignment line on the device to match the ball and your target. Pair it with the Garmin Golf app, then choose a practice range session or, if you have an active membership, a Home Tee Hero round.

Indoors, give yourself the ball-to-screen or ball-to-net distance your bay allows, and confirm the latest placement notes in the owner’s manual. Outdoors, the same portable setup works at the range; the phone mount keeps the app visible on your bag between swings. After each shot, review the metrics and, if video is enabled, the recorded clip.

Virtual rounds are a way to play a course you already know, or one you plan to visit, without leaving home. Check current Garmin Golf membership terms for Home Tee Hero, and confirm E6 Connect requirements separately if you want that software.
