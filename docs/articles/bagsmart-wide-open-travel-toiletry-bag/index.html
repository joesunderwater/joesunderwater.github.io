How to use it

Start by sorting your kit by how often you reach for it. Put taller bottles such as shampoo, body wash, and toner in the middle compartment, where they can stand upright. Use the side sections for makeup, brushes, and small tools. Save the waterproof front pocket for anything that may be damp when you pack up, like a toothbrush, a washcloth, or a razor after your morning shave.

When you arrive, unzip the bag and set it open on the counter. You see everything at a glance, so there's no need to unpack it all. At home, it can double as a tidy storage spot for backup products or a gym kit, so it's always packed and ready.
