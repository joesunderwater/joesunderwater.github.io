Who it's for

Cetaphil makes this cream for dry to very dry, sensitive skin. It suits people who want a single moisturizer for the face and body, anyone who deals with dry patches on the hands, feet, elbows, and knees, and households that like a big tub that lasts. How to use it

Cetaphil suggests using it daily and as often as needed. Gently massage it into the skin until it is absorbed. The brand points out that the best moment is right after your morning shower or evening bath, when skin is still slightly damp, to help lock in hydration.

For the face, a small amount goes a long way. Apply it after cleansing and before sunscreen in the morning, or as your last step at night. For hands and feet, a thicker layer at bedtime works well, and cotton socks or gloves can help keep it where you put it.

The tub is easy to share in a busy bathroom. Scoop with clean hands or a small spatula and close the lid after each use. If your skin is reactive, patch test first, and read the full ingredient list on the package. It contains sweet almond oil, which matters if you avoid nut-derived ingredients.
