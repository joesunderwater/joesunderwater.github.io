The Apple Watch SE 3 is Apple's entry point into its smartwatch lineup, and it keeps showing up on gift lists for a simple reason: it covers the everyday features most people actually use. It tracks workouts and sleep, keeps notifications and calls on your wrist when paired with an iPhone, and includes safety features such as fall and crash detection. This listing is the GPS model with a 40mm Starlight aluminum case and a Sand Sport Band in size S/M.

Who it's for

The SE 3 suits first-time Apple Watch owners, iPhone users who want a fitness and sleep tracker without stepping up to a pricier model, and anyone shopping for a practical holiday gift. The 40mm case is the smaller of the two SE 3 sizes. Apple lists it as fitting wrists of roughly 130–200mm, and this listing comes with the S/M band, so check your wrist size against the band options on Amazon before you order.

Keep in mind that this is the GPS model. Apple describes it as working with your iPhone or Wi-Fi to stay connected, so you'll want an iPhone to set it up and use it day to day.

Key features

  • Health insights. The listing says temperature sensing feeds the Vitals and Cycle Tracking apps. Apple also lists a Sleep app with sleep stages, sleep score, a Heart Rate app, and high and low heart rate notifications. Some health features depend on your region and software, so check Apple's notes.
  • Workout tracking. Track everything from running and strength training to a daily step goal, with real-time metrics. Apple's spec page lists many workout types, including pool and open-water swimming, yoga, HIIT, and Pilates.
  • Safety features. The listing says SE 3 can detect a hard fall or severe car crash, connect you with emergency services, and notify your emergency contacts. Emergency SOS and Backtrack are also on Apple's spec list.
  • Battery and fast charging. Up to 18 hours of normal use, according to Apple, and up to 32 hours in Low Power Mode. Apple says 15 minutes of charging gives up to 8 hours of normal use.
  • Display. An Always-On Retina display with up to 1000 nits peak brightness, plus Ion-X front glass.
  • Swimproof. Water resistant to 50m, per Apple.
  • Stay connected. Send texts, take calls, play music and podcasts, use Siri, and get notifications from your wrist.
  • Personalize it. Lots of customizable watch faces, and the band swaps out for other colors and styles.

How to use it

Pair the watch with your iPhone, then set your activity goals and a sleep schedule in the Health and Watch apps. Wear it to bed a few nights so sleep tracking and sleep score have data to work with. Charge it during a morning shower or while you get ready. Apple's fast-charging numbers make a short top-up enough for a full day.

Before you head out on a run or a bike ride, set up Emergency SOS and add your emergency contacts so the safety features are ready. The Starlight case and Sand band are a soft, neutral pairing that works with work clothes and gym gear alike. Swap in another band if you want a dressier look.
