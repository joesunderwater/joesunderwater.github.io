Some of the best work gadgets help you focus by doing less. The Kindle Scribe Colorsoft is on the White Collar Electronics board for that reason. It is a reading and writing device with an 11-inch paper-like color display, not a full tablet full of apps and alerts. Amazon calls it its most advanced Kindle Scribe, and it comes with a pen for notes, markups, and highlights.

Who it's for

This device is for people who think better with a pen in hand: anyone who takes notes in meetings, marks up contracts and reports, plans their week on paper, or reads a lot of books. Amazon positions it as purpose-built for reading and writing. Its glare-free display is easy on the eyes, even in bright sunlight, and there are no distracting apps or notifications.

It also suits anyone who wants to cut down on printing. You can bring documents in from cloud storage, write on them, and send them back out as PDFs. This listing is the 32GB model in Graphite.

Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • 11-inch Colorsoft display with a front light, custom-built for high-contrast, paper-like color without distracting flashes when you write.
  • Pen included. It never needs charging and has a built-in eraser and a shortcut button for tools like the highlighter. The textured screen surface is designed to feel like pen on paper.
  • Thin and light: 5.4mm thin and 400g.
  • Built-in notebook with AI tools. Search your notes, ask questions about what you have written, generate summaries, clean up your handwriting, or convert it to text.
  • Document workflow. Import files from Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive, add handwritten notes and markups, and export them as PDFs. You can also send notebooks to Microsoft OneNote.
  • Write in books. Jot notes right on the page of Kindle Store titles. Text shifts to make room as you write between the lines, and margins expand or collapse as needed.
  • Battery life: Amazon says one charge lasts weeks of reading and writing.
  • 32GB of storage.

How to use it

Use the built-in notebook for meetings and keep one notebook per project. Afterward, have the AI tools summarize what you wrote, or convert your handwriting to text so you can paste action items into email. Color comes in handy here. You can use one highlight color for decisions and another for follow-ups.

To review documents, pull the deck or contract from Google Drive or OneDrive, mark it up with the pen, and export a PDF to send back. If your team uses OneNote, send the notebook there so you can edit it on your laptop.

In the evening, it works as a large Kindle. The front-lit, glare-free screen is comfortable for long reading sessions, and you can write notes in the margins of books you are studying.

Yes. You can import files from Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive, add handwritten notes and markups, and export them as PDFs.
