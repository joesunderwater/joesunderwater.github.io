How to use it

Setting them up takes a few minutes:

1. Empty the drawer and measure its inside width or depth, wherever you want the dividers to run. 2. Compress a divider, place it in the drawer, and let it expand until the foam-padded ends press against both sides. 3. Space the lanes to suit what you're storing: narrow lanes for socks or utensils, wider ones for folded T-shirts or bigger tools. 4. Readjust any time. Because nothing is screwed in, you can move or remove dividers as your needs change.

In a dresser, try one lane each for socks, underwear, leggings, and accessories like belts and scarves. In the kitchen, separate everyday utensils from serving pieces and gadgets.
