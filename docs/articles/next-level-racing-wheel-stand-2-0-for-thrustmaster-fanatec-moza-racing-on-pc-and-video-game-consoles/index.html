A desk clamp is a reasonable way to start, and a poor way to hold a stronger wheel or a firm load-cell brake. The Next Level Racing Wheel Stand 2.0 is a dedicated, foldable stand for that next step: a carbon-steel frame with adjustable wheel and pedal plates, a shifter and handbrake mount, and a chair cradle, without committing a whole room to a permanent cockpit.

Who it's for

The Wheel Stand 2.0 (model NLR-S023) is for people who want a rigid place to mount a wheel and pedals, still want to sit in their own chair, and need to fold the stand away afterward. Next Level Racing also built in a path to a full cockpit: the GTSeat add-on, sold separately, can turn the stand into a seated rig and, in that combined form, work with certain Next Level Racing motion platforms.

It is a fit for entry- to mid-range wheels and pedals, including specific direct-drive bases that Next Level Racing lists as compatible, such as the Fanatec Gran Turismo DD Pro, Fanatec CSL DD, and several MOZA and Thrustmaster models. It is not automatically a match for every high-torque base; official troubleshooting notes that higher-torque wheels can introduce vibration, especially with the wheel plate set high. The published user-height range is 120–210 cm (4 ft to 6 ft 9 in).

How to use it

Unfold the stand, lock the quick-release points, and hard-mount or clamp the wheel and pedals to the plates. Set wheel height and angle first, then bring the pedal plate to your feet so you can apply firm brake pressure without lifting out of the chair. Slide your chair into the cradle so it cannot roll backward on a hard stop.

Mount a shifter or handbrake on the side you use, then adjust arm height and handbrake angle before you tighten everything down. If you use a stronger direct-drive base, start with a lower wheel-plate height and a moderate force-feedback setting, then raise either one only if the stand stays planted. Official notes warn that vibration can increase at higher heights or with stiffer bases.

When you are done, fold the stand as the manual shows. If you later want a dedicated seat, look at the official GTSeat add-on rather than improvising a cockpit around the stand. Retighten folding-axis bolts after assembly or a move, because those fasteners can loosen in shipping or with use.
