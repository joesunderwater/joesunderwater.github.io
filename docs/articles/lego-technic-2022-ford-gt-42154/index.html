How to use it

Clear a table and give yourself time. This build rewards a slow pace. LEGO points out that you can take your time on details like the differential, the opening doors, and the adjustable spoiler. Sort pieces as you go and follow the steps in the printed instructions or in the LEGO Builder app, which offers digital instructions.

Once it is finished, try the mechanics: steer the front axle, press on the suspension, and watch the pistons move in the V6. Then pick a display spot. At about 15 inches long, it fits on a shelf, a desk, or a cabinet in the den. LEGO notes that the dark blue body with white stripes makes it a striking display piece.

If you are giving it as a gift, it pairs well with other LEGO Technic car sets for someone building a collection.
