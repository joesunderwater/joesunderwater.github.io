How to use it

Place the strip on a level surface next to your stance mat, or set it into a platform cutout so the hitting surface is flush and stable. Stand on the adjacent mat, not only on the narrow strip, so your footing stays consistent. Tee the ball with a wooden or plastic tee for driver and woods, or hit irons and wedges directly off the turf.

Move the ball to different spots along the 3-foot length so one area does not wear out first. Use the way the club travels through the fibers as feedback: a heavy strike should feel different from a clean one. Keep the strip and the stance mat at the same height if you can, so you are not standing below or above the ball.

When the insert eventually wears, you can replace just the strip instead of the whole stance area. Follow any care and warranty notes on the listing.
