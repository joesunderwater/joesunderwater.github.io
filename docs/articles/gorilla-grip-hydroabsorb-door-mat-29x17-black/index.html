How to use it

Lay the mat flat on a clean, dry floor just inside or outside the door, with the rubber backing down. If you put it outside, a covered porch or entry is best, as the brand recommends. Check that the door clears it, though the low profile should make that easy for most doors.

Wipe your feet across the raised squares to scrape off mud and grit, then step onto the softer topside to soak up water. For pets, let them walk across the full length of the mat before they head into the house.

Shake out loose dirt regularly. When the mat gets saturated or muddy, clean it following the care instructions on the listing and let it dry fully before putting it back.
