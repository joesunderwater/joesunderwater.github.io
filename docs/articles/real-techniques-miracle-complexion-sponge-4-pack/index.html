How to use it

Run the sponge under water, squeeze out the excess, and you will notice it gets bigger and softer. That damp state is what the brand recommends for a natural, dewy finish. Dot foundation or skin tint on your face (or on the back of your hand), then use the flat edge to spread product over larger areas like the cheeks and forehead.

Switch to the rounded side and use a light bouncing motion rather than wiping, which helps blur edges and avoid streaks. Finish with the precision tip under the eyes, around the nostrils, and over any spots where you want concealer. If you want more coverage, use the sponge dry or build thin layers.

Wash sponges regularly and let them air-dry fully between uses. Having four in the pack makes it easy to rotate while one dries. As with any beauty product, stop using it if irritation occurs.
