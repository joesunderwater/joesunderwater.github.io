How to use it

Sort by category or by day. Roll or fold shirts into the larger cubes and buckle the straps. Put underwear and socks in the small cube, and keep chargers and cables in the accessories bag. Shoes go in the shoe bag so they don't touch your clothes, and the toiletry bag can hang on a hook in the hotel bathroom.

Packing tips:

  • Give each traveler a different cube for easy family packing.
  • Pack one cube with a full outfit and swimsuit so you can grab it first on arrival.
  • Move worn clothes into the laundry bag as you go, then toss it straight into the wash at home.
  • Keep your passport and travel documents in the passport wallet so they're easy to find.
