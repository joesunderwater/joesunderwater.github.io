How to use it

1. Empty and measure the drawer. Note its inside width, depth, and height so you can plan a layout. 2. Sort what goes back in. Group like with like: pens, sticky notes, batteries, measuring spoons, makeup brushes. 3. Lay out the trays. Put the longest trays along the sides for long items like utensils, spatulas, or pens, and use the small square tray for little things like clips, hair ties, or rings. 4. Fill and adjust. Move trays around until the layout fits the way you use the drawer.

The open trays also work on top of a desk or vanity as catchalls. To keep the bamboo looking good, wipe up spills promptly and skip the dishwasher.
