How to use it

Fill it with ice and water in the morning, twist the lid to the straw position, and keep it in your car cup holder or on your desk all day. When you are tossing it into a tote or gym bag, rotate the cover to the full-cover position for added leak resistance. Stanley describes the lid as leak resistant, not leakproof, so keep it upright in your bag when you can.

The drink opening is handy for iced coffee or tea when you would rather sip without the straw. Because it is dishwasher safe, rinsing between flavors is simple. Lilac pairs well with neutral desk setups and pastel accessories, and it is an easy item to add to a gift basket with a favorite drink mix.
