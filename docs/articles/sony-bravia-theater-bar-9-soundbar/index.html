Who it's for

This soundbar is for people who want immersive, room-filling sound from one bar under the TV. It suits movie fans, sports watchers, and gamers. Its 360 Spatial Sound Mapping and automatic room calibration aim for a surround experience without a full receiver-and-speaker setup.

It is a particularly strong fit if you own a compatible Sony BRAVIA TV or a PS5. Sony lists TV-menu control, Acoustic Center Sync with compatible BRAVIA TVs, and support for BRAVIA TV features for PS5. If you want more bass or true rear channels later, the system can grow with optional Sony subwoofers and rear speakers.

How to use it

Once the bar is in place under the TV, run Sound Field Optimization so it can calibrate to your room's characteristics. Then open the BRAVIA Connect app and choose a sound profile for what you are watching. If you have a compatible BRAVIA TV, control the soundbar from the TV menu and turn on Acoustic Center Sync.

For gaming, confirm that your console and TV support the HDMI 2.1 features you want, such as 4K120, VRR, and ALLM. PS5 owners with a compatible BRAVIA TV can also use Auto HDR Tone Mapping and Auto Genre Picture Mode.

When the TV is off, the bar works as a music system with Spotify Connect, AirPlay 2, or Bluetooth. If you want more impact on game day later, add one of the listed optional subwoofers or rear speakers instead of replacing the whole setup.
