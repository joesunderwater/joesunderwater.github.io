Who it's for

The C120 is for anyone who wants an affordable, flexible camera for keeping an eye on pets, kids, or the house. Because it's weatherproof (IP66), it works inside and out. You could use it as a pet cam in the living room, a baby monitor in the nursery, or a camera on a porch or patio. Its magnetic base makes it useful for renters and anyone who'd rather not drill: stick it to a metal railing, frame, or even the fridge.

It's a wired camera that connects over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, so plan to place it within reach of power and your home Wi-Fi.

How to use it

As a pet cam, set it on a shelf facing the dog's favorite spot, or stick the magnetic base to the fridge. Turn on pet detection so you get alerts when your pet moves, and use two-way audio to talk to them. In a nursery, place it out of reach with a clear view of the crib. At night, switch on invisible IR mode so no red light shows.

Outdoors, mount it on a metal railing or frame by a door or patio, within reach of a power outlet. Set up person and vehicle detection and customize the alerts so your phone only buzzes for what matters. For recording, put in a compatible microSD card (up to 512GB, sold separately), or subscribe to Tapo Care if you'd rather use cloud storage.
