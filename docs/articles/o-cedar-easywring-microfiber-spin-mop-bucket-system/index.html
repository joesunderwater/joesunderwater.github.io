Mopping is one of those chores nobody looks forward to, mostly because of the wringing. Twisting a soggy mop head by hand is messy, and a mop that is too wet leaves floors streaky and slow to dry. The O-Cedar EasyWring Microfiber Spin Mop & Bucket System solves that with a foot pedal. You press it to spin the mop head inside the bucket, so you never have to touch the dirty mop. It is on the Home Essentials board as a practical upgrade for anyone with hard floors.

Who it's for

The EasyWring is made for homes with hard floors. O-Cedar says it is safe for all hard floor surfaces, including hardwood, sealed wood, tile, laminate, and vinyl. It is a good fit for:

  • Homes with a mix of floor types, like tile in the kitchen and laminate in the hallway.
  • People who want to control how wet the mop is.
  • Anyone who prefers a reusable, washable mop head over disposable pads.
  • Busy households that mop often and want a quicker routine.

Key features

These details come from the O-Cedar brand page and the product details for ASIN B00WSWGVZQ:

  • Foot pedal wringing. The bucket has a built-in wringer, and the foot pedal spins the mop for hands-free wringing and controlled water release.
  • Moisture control. Because you choose how long to spin, you control how damp the mop is, which O-Cedar ties to quicker-drying floors.
  • Splash guard. A splash guard keeps splash and spray inside the bucket while you wring.
  • Microfiber mop head. O-Cedar says the microfiber head removes tough dirt and grime and cleans with just water.
  • Triangle head shape. The brand describes a triangle-shaped mop head for easy maneuvering and corner cleaning.
  • Telescopic handle. O-Cedar lists a telescopic handle that adjusts to 4.25 ft (51 inches), plus an integrated C-clip on the handle for hanging it up.
  • Machine washable head. The microfiber mop head can be machine or hand washed and is compatible with the EasyWring Mop Refill.

How to use it

Fill the bucket with water, or water plus a hard floor cleaner such as O-Cedar PACS if you like. Dip the mop head in, set it in the wringer basket, and press the foot pedal a few times. More pumps make for a drier mop, which works well for wood and laminate. Fewer pumps leave it a bit wetter for sticky spots on tile.

Mop in sections, using the pointed edge of the triangle head to get into corners and along baseboards. Rinse and spin again whenever the head looks dirty. When you finish, machine or hand wash the mop head without fabric softener or bleach, lay it flat to dry, and rinse and dry the bucket before storing it. O-Cedar also advises storing the bucket in dry conditions out of excessive sunlight.
