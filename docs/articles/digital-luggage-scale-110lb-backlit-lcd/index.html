How to use it

Loop the nylon strap through your suitcase handle and clip it to the steel hook. Hold the rubberized handle and lift the bag off the floor in one steady motion. Once the reading locks, set the bag down and check the screen. Switch to kg if your airline lists its limit that way.

Do a weigh-in at home before you head to the airport, then pack the scale in your suitcase. At the end of your trip, weigh the bag again after souvenir shopping. If you're close to the limit, you can move a few heavy items into your carry-on before you get to the counter. Always double-check your airline's own baggage rules, since limits vary.
