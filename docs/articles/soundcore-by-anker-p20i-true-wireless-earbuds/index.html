How to use it

Charge the case, pair the earbuds with your phone over Bluetooth, and install the soundcore app to set up the controls the way you like. Try a few of the 22 EQ presets. A bass-forward preset works well for workouts, while a more balanced one may suit podcasts and calls better.

For the gym or a commute, clip the case to your bag or keys with the attached lanyard so it doesn't get lost at the bottom of your bag. If you're running low before you leave the house, a 10-minute charge gives you about 2 hours of listening. If an earbud goes missing around the house, use Find My Earbuds in the app to make it play a sound.
