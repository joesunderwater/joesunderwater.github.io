A good running watch makes training simpler: it tracks pace and distance, tells you when to push and when to rest, and stays out of the way the rest of the day. The Garmin Forerunner 165 is built around that idea. Garmin describes it as an easy-to-use running smartwatch with built-in GPS, wrist-based heart rate, and a bright AMOLED touchscreen with traditional buttons. This version is the 43mm size in the Whitestone color.

Who it's for

The Forerunner 165 fits new runners who want guidance without a complicated setup, and regular runners training for a specific race. Daily suggested workouts and Garmin Coach plans help structure training, while recovery insights help you avoid overdoing it. Because it also includes smart notifications and Garmin Pay, it works as an everyday watch, not just a race-day tool. The light Whitestone finish makes it an easy gift for a runner in your life.

Key features

  • Built-in GPS and wrist heart rate. Track pace and distance outdoors and monitor heart rate from your wrist.
  • AMOLED touchscreen with buttons. A bright display plus traditional button controls, in a lightweight 43mm design.
  • Battery life. Garmin lists up to 11 days in smartwatch mode and up to 19 hours in GPS mode.
  • Adaptive daily suggested workouts. Suggestions adjust based on your performance and recovery. Garmin Coach and race adaptive training plans give workout suggestions for specific events.
  • Morning report. Wake up to an overview of your sleep, recovery, and training outlook, along with weather and HRV status. Garmin notes this data is a close estimate.
  • Training effect and recovery time. See how each workout affects your fitness and how long to recover before your next hard session.
  • 25+ activity profiles. Running, cycling, HIIT, strength, and more.
  • Safety features. Incident detection (during select outdoor activities) and Assistance can send your live location to emergency contacts when paired with a compatible smartphone that has coverage. Setup is required.
  • Smart notifications and Garmin Pay. Get calls and texts from a paired Android or Apple phone, and pay contactless in supported countries and payment networks.
  • Garmin Connect app. Review your stats, connect with friends, and join challenges.

How to use it

Pair the watch with the Garmin Connect app and set up your profile. Each morning, check the morning report to see how you slept and whether your body is ready for a hard day. Then follow the daily suggested workout, or pick a Garmin Coach or race plan if you have an event on the calendar.

On the run, start the run profile and let GPS track pace and distance. Afterward, look at training effect to see what the session did for your fitness and recovery time to plan your next hard effort. If you run alone, set up incident detection and Assistance before heading out.
