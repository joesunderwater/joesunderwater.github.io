Amazon Echo Studio – Smart Speaker With Immersive Dolby Atmos Sound & Alexa

If you want better sound at home without running wires for a full stereo system, a single smart speaker is an easy upgrade. The Amazon Echo Studio (2025 release) is Amazon's high-end Echo speaker. Amazon says it delivers powerful bass and clear vocals with spatial audio and Dolby Atmos, tunes itself to your room, and doubles as a smart home hub. This listing is the Glacier White version.

The photo on this page is a lifestyle image for mood and inspiration. It is not a photograph of the Echo Studio. For official product photos, use the Amazon listing linked below.

Who it's for

Echo Studio suits anyone who listens to a lot of music at home and wants a fuller sound from one speaker. It is also a good fit for smart home households, since it has a built-in hub that can control lights, locks, blinds, and more. Fire TV owners can use it as part of a simple home theater setup with compatible devices. And Prime members get Alexa+ at no extra cost, according to Amazon.

Key features

  • Spatial audio with Dolby Atmos. Amazon highlights powerful bass and crystal-clear vocals.
  • Room adaptation. Echo Studio analyzes your room's acoustics and fine-tunes playback for where it is placed.
  • Adjustable EQ. Tune bass, mid, and treble in the Alexa app or by voice.
  • Built-in smart home hub. The listing names Zigbee plus Matter and Thread Border Router support, with Omnisense technology. Connect and control tens of thousands of compatible devices.
  • eero built-in. It can help extend your wifi.
  • Designed for Alexa+. Chat naturally and move between topics and requests. Amazon says Alexa+ is free with Prime.
  • Expand your setup. Play music across other Echo devices for multi-room audio, create a stereo pair with a second Echo Studio, or add an Echo Sub.
  • Home theater with Fire TV. Amazon says the 2025 Echo Studio works with select Fire TV devices, such as Fire TV Stick 4K (2nd Gen) and Fire TV Stick 4K Max (2nd Gen). Older Echo and Fire TV models do not support this experience.
  • Privacy controls. A mic and camera disable button, plus camera status indicators on the device and in the app.
  • In the box: Echo Studio (2025 release), a 50W power adapter, and a quick start guide.

How to use it

Plug it in, connect it to wifi with the Alexa app, and place it where you listen most. Let room adaptation tune the sound, then adjust bass, mid, and treble to taste. Ask Alexa to play a playlist, set timers, or control your lights and locks.

For movie nights, set up your Echo Studio, then link it from your compatible Fire TV device. If you want a bigger sound later, add a second Echo Studio as a stereo pair or add an Echo Sub. For background music in several rooms, group it with other Echo devices.
