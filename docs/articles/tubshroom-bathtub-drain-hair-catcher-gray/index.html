A slow bathtub drain usually comes down to one thing: hair. It slips past flat drain covers, tangles around the stopper, and turns into a clog. Then you're stuck with a drain snake or a bottle of harsh cleaner. The TubShroom tries to stop that before it starts. It sits inside the drain and catches hair around a small cylinder, so you can lift it out and wipe it clean. It is on the Home Essentials board as a small bathroom fix for anyone with long hair or pets.

Who it's for

TubShroom is aimed at households where hair regularly goes down the tub drain. That includes people with long hair, families sharing one bathroom, and anyone who bathes pets in the tub. It suits people who would rather do quick, regular cleanups than deal with clogs later.

Fit matters here. The listing says it works with most bathtub drains that are 1.4" to 1.75" wide. It is compatible with removable pop-up, lift-and-turn, and toe-touch stoppers. It is not compatible with non-removable built-in stoppers or flat 4" shower stall drains. The brand also suggests checking the space under the drain opening, since internal crossbars can get in the way.

Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for ASIN B01M0H7YC2:

  • In-drain design. Unlike flat covers, TubShroom fits inside the drain to catch human and pet hair before it causes slow drainage.
  • Hair wraps around the cylinder. Hair collects around the in-drain cylinder, so you can lift, wipe, and rinse it with no tools needed.
  • Water flow. It sits ½" to 1" above the drain floor so water can flow around the sides. The brand says drainage is barely affected when you clean it regularly.
  • Silicone, in gray. The listing gives silicone as the material and gray as the color for this version.
  • Dishwasher safe. You can rinse it under warm water with a bleach-free household cleaner, and the brand says it is dishwasher safe for periodic cleaning.
  • Filter, not a stopper. TubShroom is a drain filter, not a plug. To take a bath, remove it and use a compatible stopper, then put it back.

How to use it

Start with a clear drain. The brand says TubShroom is for prevention, not for removing an existing clog, so clear out any blockage first. Then remove the existing stopper if it's a removable type, and press TubShroom down into the drain so it sits snugly with the cap above the drain floor.

Shower or drain the tub as usual. Every so often, pull it out, slide or wipe the hair off the cylinder into the trash, rinse it, and drop it back in. How often you clean it depends on how much hair goes down the drain. Regular cleaning keeps the water flowing well. Soap residue can build up over time, so give it a wash with a bleach-free cleaner or run it through the dishwasher now and then.
