This set is designed for adults ages 18 and up and is part of LEGO's range of creative projects for adults. It is a good fit for:

  • Classic car lovers and fans of Ford collectibles.
  • Adult builders who want a slower, detail-rich build to unwind with.

How to use it

This is a build to enjoy at a slow pace. Spread it over a few evenings, put on some music or a game in the background, and work through the bags one at a time. If you like a screen, the LEGO Builder app shows the same steps in 3D.

When it is finished, take a minute to try every function. Fold back the roof, drop the split windshield, lift the hood panels to show the engine, and turn the crank to watch the fan spin. Lift the seat to find the gas tank. Then pick a display spot. A bookshelf, a home office credenza, a den side table, or a clean shelf in the garage all suit a vintage car.

As a gift, it pairs nicely with an interest in automotive history. It is a thoughtful pick for someone who already has modern supercars on the shelf and wants a classic to round out the collection.
