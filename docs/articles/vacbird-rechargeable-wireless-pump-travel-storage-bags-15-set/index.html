How to use it

1. Charge the pump before your trip using the Type-C cable or wireless charging. 2. Fill a bag with folded clothes. Leave room at the top so it seals cleanly. 3. Seal the bag along the closure. 4. Place the pump on the valve and let it pull the air out until the bag is flat. 5. Pack the flat bags into your suitcase, then pack the pump in its pouch so you can reseal for the trip home.

A few practical ideas:

  • Group by outfit or by person so each bag holds one day's layers or one family member's clothes.
  • Use the large bags for coats and blankets and the smaller ones for sweaters and kids' clothes.
  • Separate dirty laundry on the way home in its own compressed bag.
  • Store off-season clothes under the bed or on a closet shelf between trips.

One packing tip: compression saves space but not weight, so keep your airline's weight limit in mind when you fill a suitcase.
