How to use it

Wash every piece before first use. Umite Chef notes that any new-silicone smell should fade after soaking the pieces in water and letting them air out for about 3 hours.

Stand the everyday tools, like the spatulas, turners, ladle, and tongs, in the holder next to the stove so they are always within reach. Use the hooks to hang extra tools or the measuring cups and spoons on a rail or inside a cabinet door. The silicone mat gives you a spot to rest a messy spoon while you cook.

Reach for the silicone tools with nonstick pans, where metal utensils could scratch the coating. The whisk and spoonula handle batters and sauces, the pasta server and slotted spoon are made for draining and serving, and the basting brush works for glazes and butter.

When cleaning, keep in mind that the listing does not recommend long soaks or the dishwasher because of the wooden handles. Wash by hand and dry the handles promptly.
