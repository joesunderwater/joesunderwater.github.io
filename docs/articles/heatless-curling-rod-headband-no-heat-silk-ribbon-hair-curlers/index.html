How to use it

The listing's directions are straightforward:

1. Wrap sections of damp or dry hair around the silk ribbon curling rod. 2. Secure the hair in place with the attached hair clip or other hair accessories. 3. Wear the headband overnight or while you go about your day. 4. Gently release the curls in the morning or after the time you want. 5. Style as you like: brush out for softer waves, or separate the curls for more texture.

A practical tip is to center the rod on top of your head like a headband, clip the middle at your crown, then wrap one side at a time, adding a small section of hair with each twist around the rod. Tie the ends with scrunchies or hair ties so the wraps stay snug. Slightly damp hair tends to hold a set better than soaking wet hair, which may still be damp by morning.
