How to use it

Rinse the mat, press it firmly onto a clean, smooth surface so the suction cups grip, and spread a thin layer of a pet-safe treat with the spatula. During a bath, stick it to the tub wall at a comfortable height. On the floor, make sure the surface is smooth enough for the cups to hold. Let your pet lick it clean, then pop the mat in the dishwasher or wash it by hand.

For a longer session, you can spread the treat and chill the mat in the freezer before you use it, since the listing says it is freezer safe. Always supervise, and take the mat up when you are done so it does not become a chew toy.
