How to use it

Kitsch's directions are three steps. Store the roller in the freezer, take it out when you are ready, and gently roll it over your face and neck.

For a morning routine, roll after cleansing and before serum or moisturizer. Work from the center of the face outward, along the jawline, across the cheeks, and lightly under the eyes. A minute or two is plenty, and you can put it back in the freezer right after. After shaving or a long day outside, a few slow passes over the area can feel calming.

Wipe it clean after each use and return it to the freezer so it is ready the next morning. If any area of skin is broken or very irritated, skip it.
