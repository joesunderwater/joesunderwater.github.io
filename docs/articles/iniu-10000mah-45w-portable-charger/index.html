How to use it

Charge the power bank fully before you leave, then toss it in your bag with the attached USB-C cable. When your phone gets low, plug it in. For devices that use a different connector, bring your own cable for the USB-A or second USB-C output.

Practical ideas:

  • Pack it in your carry-on, not your checked bag, since airlines generally require power banks in the cabin. Check your airline's rules before you fly.
  • Use it to top up a tablet or handheld game console on long flights or road trips.
  • Keep it charged in a drawer as part of your power outage kit.
  • Share the extra port with a travel companion so you're both charged up.
