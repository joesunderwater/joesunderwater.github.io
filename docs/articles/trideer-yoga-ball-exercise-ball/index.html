How to use it

Follow the inflation instructions that come with the ball, and check the Amazon listing to see what is included with your order. Use the ball on a clean, flat surface with the area around it clear.

For core work, try seated marches, gentle seated balance holds, or lying back over the ball for crunches. For stretching, drape your back over it for a chest-opening stretch, or sit and roll your hips in slow circles. To use it as a desk seat, choose the right size so your hips sit level with or slightly above your knees. Start with short stretches of time and switch back to a chair when you need to.

Trideer's own safety tips: keep the ball away from sharp objects like furniture corners, toys, pet claws, or equipment with sharp edges. Keep it away from pets when you are not using it.
