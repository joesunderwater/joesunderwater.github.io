Who it's for

This adapter makes sense for:

  • International travelers heading to Europe, the UK, Asia, Australia, or South America.
  • Families and couples who need to charge several phones and tablets from one outlet.
  • Business travelers who carry a laptop, a phone, and earbuds and want one adapter for all of them.
  • Study-abroad students and cruise passengers who visit several countries on one trip.

One important note from the listing: this is not a voltage converter. Use it only with devices rated for 100–240V. Check the label on your charger before you plug in. It is not meant for 110V-only devices like many hair dryers, curling irons, and straighteners.

How to use it

1. Check your devices. Look for "100–240V" on each charger or device label. Most phone, tablet, and laptop chargers are dual voltage, but always confirm. 2. Select the plug type for your destination, using the country list above. 3. Plug the adapter into the wall, then plug your device's own charger into the universal input or connect cables to the USB ports. 4. Use the Power Delivery USB-C port for the device that needs the fastest charge, such as a laptop or a newer phone.
