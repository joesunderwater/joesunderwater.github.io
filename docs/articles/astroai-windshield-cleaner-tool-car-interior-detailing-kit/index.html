How to use it

  • Start with a clean pad. Fit a dry microfiber pad onto the head.
  • Spray lightly. Fill the included spray bottle with your preferred glass cleaner and mist the pad or the glass. A light mist is enough; too much liquid can leave streaks and drip onto the dash.
  • Work in sections. Extend the handle, angle the head, and wipe the windshield in overlapping strokes, paying extra attention to the lower corners where the glass meets the dashboard.
  • Buff dry. Swap to a fresh, dry pad and go over the glass again to pick up any remaining film. With four pads, you can keep one for cleaning and one for buffing.
  • Wash and store. Wash the pads without fabric softener or bleach, let them dry, then split the handle and pack everything in the storage bag under a seat or in the trunk.

It works on side windows, the rear glass, and mirrors too, and AstroAI mentions household windows that are hard to reach.
