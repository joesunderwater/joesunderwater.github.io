Who it's for

This lead is for dogs that ride in the car and already have, or will wear, a vest-style harness with a D-ring. The listing specifically says to use it with a vest harness for better comfort and safety, not as a neck collar tie-down. It suits everyday errands, longer highway trips, and anyone who wants their dog to sit, stand, or lie down within a limited range instead of climbing the seats.

It is a restraint strap, not a crash-tested child-seat equivalent. No car pet belt replaces good harness fit, a well-secured setup, and common-sense travel habits.

How to use it

Put a well-fitted vest harness on your dog. Clip the swivel hook to the harness D-ring, then buckle the metal tongue into the car's seat-belt receiver until it clicks. Adjust the strap so your dog can sit or lie down comfortably but cannot climb into the front, reach the driver, or tumble off the seat.

Check that the 0.79-inch tab fits your vehicle's buckle, and never attach this lead to a collar alone. For the first rides, keep trips short and reward calm settling. Afterward, inspect the stitching, clip, and bungee for wear.
