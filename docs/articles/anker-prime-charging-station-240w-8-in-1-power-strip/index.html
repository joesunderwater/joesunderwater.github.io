Anker Prime 240W Charging Station – 8-in-1 USB-C Desk Charger

Anker Prime Charging Station, 240W 8-in-1 Power Strip · Posted 2026-11-11

A home-office desk collects chargers quickly: a laptop brick, a phone cable, a watch puck, and a spare outlet for a lamp. The Anker Prime Charging Station is meant to collapse that pile into one compact hub, with 240W of total USB power, four USB-C ports, two USB-A ports, and two AC outlets.

The photo on this page is a lifestyle image for mood and styling inspiration. It is not a photograph of this charging station. For official compatibility notes, product photos, and package contents, use the Amazon listing linked below.

Who it's for

This is a desk or home charger, not a travel power strip. Anker’s Amazon listing flags it as “Not for Travel,” which is worth taking at face value: it is designed to sit at a workstation and replace several wall warts. It is a fit if you charge a USB-C laptop, a phone, and smaller accessories from one place, or if you want two residual AC outlets for a lamp, a monitor, or a speaker.

It is also useful if you want app-level visibility. The station is Wi-Fi enabled, so you can monitor charging and receive alerts from your phone. If you only need a simple USB brick for a suitcase, a smaller Anker charger is a better match than this 8-in-1 hub.

Key features

Details below come from Anker’s official product page for the Prime Charging Station (8-in-1, 240W), model A91B2:

  • Eight ports in total: four USB-C, two USB-A, and two AC outlets.
  • Up to 240W total USB output. Each USB-C port can deliver up to 140W (28V/5A). USB-A ports are rated at 5V/2.4A each. When several USB ports are used together, the station shares that 240W budget; Anker lists slightly lower combined maxima when five or six USB ports are active.
  • A compact footprint. The charging station alone is 3.07 x 5.43 x 0.71 inches and 8.11 oz; with the wall adapter it is 4.06 x 3.07 x 1.25 inches.
  • A 5-foot power cord, including the plug. Anker describes the cord as detachable on the Amazon listing.
  • Input is 100–240V, 50–60 Hz. AC output is listed as 125V/13A.
  • 300J surge protection, plus Anker’s ActiveShield 3.0 monitoring, which the company describes as continuous temperature checks.
  • Wi-Fi app control for real-time status, error alerts, and power management.
  • cTUVus and other safety certifications listed on the official page. Anker notes that a 5A E-Marker USB-C cable is needed to reach charging speeds of 60W or above.

How to use it

Place the station on a desk or shelf with some airflow, plug in the adapter, and use the highest-demand USB-C port for your laptop if that device supports high-wattage USB-C charging. A single USB-C port can supply up to 140W, which is enough for many USB-C laptops that request a high-power profile. Phones, tablets, earbuds, and other accessories can share the remaining USB-C and USB-A ports. Keep the two AC outlets for devices that still need a wall plug.

If you want the app features, connect the station to Wi-Fi and use Anker’s software to watch status and alerts. For fast laptop charging, use a USB-C cable rated for 5A / E-Marker current, as Anker specifies. Do not treat this as a packing cube for travel, and do not cover the adapter while it is under heavy load. High-power multi-port chargers can run warm; Anker says that is expected and that the product includes multiple protections.

Always check the live listing for current device compatibility. Anker publishes a list of tested phones and tablets, and laptop results can depend on the cable and the computer’s USB-C power profile.

Quick FAQ

Can one USB-C port charge a laptop?

Yes. Each USB-C port is rated up to 140W, which is intended for high-power USB-C laptops as well as phones and tablets. Use a 5A E-Marker cable for higher-wattage charging, and confirm your laptop’s requirements.

Is this meant for travel?

No. The Amazon listing describes it as a home-office charging station and not for travel.

How many devices can it charge at once?

There are eight ports: four USB-C, two USB-A, and two AC outlets. Total USB power is up to 240W and is shared when several USB ports are used at the same time.

Does it have surge protection?

Yes. Anker lists 300J surge protection, along with ActiveShield 3.0 temperature monitoring.
