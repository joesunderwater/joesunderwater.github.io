How to use it

Pick the brush that fits the job: a larger brush for broad surfaces like tub walls and floors, and a smaller one for grout lines and detail work. Fit it into your cordless drill's chuck and tighten it securely. For corners or recessed spots, put the extension rod in the drill first, then attach the brush to the rod.

Spray your usual cleaner on the surface, then start the drill at a low speed with the brush already touching the surface to keep splashing down. Let the spinning bristles do the work with light pressure, and move slowly across the area. Rinse or wipe the surface afterward. Keep the drill and its battery away from standing water.

When you finish, rinse the brushes, shake out the water, and let them air dry before storing them.
