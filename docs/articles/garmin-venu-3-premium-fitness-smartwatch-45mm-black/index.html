Garmin Venu 3 Smartwatch – 45mm AMOLED Fitness Watch, 14-Day Battery

A useful fitness watch has to survive a full week of wear, not just a single workout. The Garmin Venu 3 is the larger 45 mm model in Garmin’s Venu 3 series, built around an AMOLED touchscreen, built-in GPS, and a battery rating of up to 14 days in smartwatch mode, so you can track sleep and recovery without a nightly charge.

Who it's for

The Venu 3 is aimed at people who want a daily-wear GPS smartwatch with strong health tracking and a bright display, rather than a minimalist sport watch or a phone-first smartwatch. Garmin presents it as a coach on the wrist: sleep scoring and coaching, Body Battery energy monitoring, nap detection, and a morning report that summarizes overnight data.

It is a fit if you want to make and take calls from the watch when it is paired with a compatible smartphone, or if you want more than 30 built-in sports apps plus animated strength, HIIT, Pilates, and yoga workouts. This listing is the 45 mm black model. A smaller Venu 3S is a separate size.

Key features

Details below come from Garmin’s official Venu 3 announcement and product materials:

  • 45 mm GPS smartwatch with an AMOLED touchscreen, a stainless steel bezel, and a silicone band. This black/slate colorway is one of the official Venu 3 finishes.
  • Battery life of up to 14 days in smartwatch mode, which is the reason Garmin emphasizes all-day and overnight tracking without charging every night.
  • Built-in GPS, with support for additional satellite systems described in Garmin’s specifications, so outdoor runs and rides can be recorded without carrying a phone the entire time.
  • Health and recovery tools include Body Battery energy monitoring, a sleep score with personalized sleep coaching, nap detection, HRV status, Pulse Ox, heart rate, respiration, and stress tracking. Pulse Ox is not available in all countries and is not a medical device.
  • A built-in speaker and microphone for making and taking phone calls, plus voice-assistant replies to texts, when the watch is paired with a compatible Apple or Android phone. Android users can also use an on-watch keyboard for some replies.
  • More than 30 built-in sports apps, including walking, running, cycling, pool swimming, and wheelchair-specific activities. Wheelchair mode can track pushes and offer related workouts and alerts.
  • Workout benefit and recovery time estimates, rate of perceived exertion, and interval creation for running and cycling. Preloaded animated workouts and Garmin Coach training plans are available through the watch and Garmin Connect.
  • Other daily tools include smart notifications, Garmin Pay contactless payments where supported, and offline music from services such as Spotify, Amazon Music, and Deezer (subscriptions required).

How to use it

Pair the watch with the Garmin Connect app, wear it overnight so sleep, HRV, and Body Battery have a full picture, and glance at the morning report when you wake up. That report is the easiest way to see how sleep, recovery, and stress are trending before you decide how hard to train.

For workouts, pick a built-in sport app or start a preloaded strength, HIIT, Pilates, or yoga session and follow the animations on the display. Outdoor runs and rides can use the built-in GPS. If you are training for a race, Garmin Coach plans can be sent to the watch from the app. After a session, review workout benefit and recovery time rather than stacking hard days back to back.

For everyday use, enable calls and notifications only if you want the watch to act as a reach extender for your phone. Download music for phone-free listening if your service is supported, and set up Garmin Pay if your bank is on Garmin’s current list. Charge it before the battery gets low rather than on a nightly schedule.

Quick FAQ

How is this different from the Venu 3S?

This listing is the 45 mm Venu 3. The Venu 3S is a smaller model in the same series, with its own display and band sizes. Confirm the exact model name on the Amazon page.

How long does the battery last?

Garmin rates the Venu 3 at up to 14 days in smartwatch mode. GPS use, music, calls, and brighter display settings will shorten that.

Can I take calls on the watch?

Yes, when it is paired with a compatible smartphone. The Venu 3 has a built-in speaker and microphone for calls and some voice-assistant text replies.

Does it have built-in GPS?

Yes. You can record outdoor activities with the watch’s own GNSS, without keeping a phone in hand the entire time.
