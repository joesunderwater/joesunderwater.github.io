How to use it

Assign each cube a job. For example, use the larger cubes for tops and pants, mid-size cubes for sleepwear or workout clothes, and the smallest cubes for socks, underwear, and accessories. Rolling or folding clothes neatly before you zip makes compression easier. Close the main zipper, then zip the compression zipper to squeeze the cube down before it goes into the suitcase.

When you arrive, follow the brand's tip and move the cubes straight into the hotel drawers or closet. You can find what you need through the mesh top, and the rest of your clothes stay folded. On the way home, a spare cube makes a handy spot for worn clothes so they stay apart from clean ones.

For family trips, give each person their own cube or two. Everyone's clothes stay together, and repacking goes much faster.
