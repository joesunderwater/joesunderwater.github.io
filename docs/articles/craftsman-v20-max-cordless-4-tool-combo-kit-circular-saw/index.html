How to use it

Think of the four tools as a team. Use the circular saw to cut boards to length or rip plywood to width. Its tool-free bevel adjustment lets you make angled cuts, and the spindle lock makes blade swaps simpler. Always clamp your work, keep both hands on the saw, and wear eye and hearing protection.

Use the drill/driver to drill pilot holes and bore larger holes, choosing low speed for control and high speed for drilling. Then switch to the impact driver to sink long screws and lag bolts. Its high torque makes driving deck screws or structural screws much easier on your wrist than a standard drill.

The LED task light pivots, so you can aim it into a cabinet, under a sink, or at your cut line in a dim garage. Because everything shares V20 batteries, keep one battery on the charger while you work with the other.
