How to use it

The listing gives step-by-step directions. Place the roller under the muscle group you want to work, such as your calves, hamstrings, quads, glutes, or upper back. Use different parts of the GRID surface to change the intensity. Roll slowly and evenly over the muscle, adjusting pressure with your arms and legs. When you reach a tender spot, hold for 30 to 60 seconds or until the discomfort eases, then move on.

TriggerPoint's safety notes say to use gentle to moderate pressure, avoid rolling on bones, joints, or sensitive areas, and not hold pressure on one spot for more than 60 seconds. If anything hurts, stop and talk to a healthcare professional.

After use, wipe the roller with a damp cloth and let it air dry. Its small size makes it easy to bring to the gym or on trips.
