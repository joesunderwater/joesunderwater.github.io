How to use it

Choose the blade you need and attach it to the grater. Press the base onto a clean, smooth countertop so the suction holds. Cut the cheese into pieces that fit the chute, put them in, press down with the food pusher, and turn the crank. Set a bowl or plate under the drum to catch the shreds.

A few practical ideas:

1. Taco and pizza night. Shred a block of cheese in a few turns. 2. Parmesan for pasta. Use the grinding blade for finer results on hard cheeses. 3. Nuts and toppings. The listing mentions peanuts, almonds, and cookies for salads, smoothies, and desserts. 4. Hash browns. Use the shredding blade on potatoes.

When you're done, take it apart and rinse it, or put it on the top rack of the dishwasher.
