How to use it

Measuring. Hook the end on the edge of your workpiece and pull the blade out to the length you need. The blade locks automatically, so you can let go, read the fraction printed on the blade, and mark your cut.

Retracting. Press the button to release the blade. Thanks to the controlled retraction, it comes back without the hook smacking into the case, but it's still a good habit to guide it in with your hand.

Reading fractions. Instead of counting the small lines between inch marks, look for the printed fraction next to the line. This is handy for cutting trim, spacing hardware, or anything where 1/8 inch makes a difference.

To keep the tape in good shape, wipe off sawdust, paint, or grit before you retract it, keep the blade from kinking, and avoid dropping it from height. Clip it to a belt or pocket when you're working so it's always close.
