How to use it

A few practical ways to set it up:

1. Assign a pocket to each item. Put long cables in the larger compartments and small things such as USB drives, SD cards, and earbuds in the mesh pockets, so the layout stays the same every trip. 2. Use one layer for "daily" gear and the other for "trip" gear. For example, keep your phone charger and earbuds on one side, and international adapters or a spare cable on the other. 3. Coil cables loosely before tucking them in, which keeps them tangle-free and makes the pouch easier to close. 4. Keep it packed. Many travelers keep a duplicate set of chargers in the pouch full-time, so packing becomes "grab the pouch" instead of unplugging things around the house. 5. Grab it by the strap when you only need your tech, such as heading to a café or a meeting room.

It also works at home or in a desk drawer as a single spot for spare cords, a mouse, and chargers.
