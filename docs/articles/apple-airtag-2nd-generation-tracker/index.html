Who it's for

AirTag is made for iPhone and iPad users. Setup is described as a simple one-tap process with iPhone or iPad. It is a good fit if you:

  • Regularly misplace keys, a wallet, a backpack, or a purse.
  • Travel and want an easier way to keep track of luggage. Apple says you can share an AirTag's location with over 50 airline partners if you lose something important.
  • Already use Apple Watch. The 2nd generation adds Precision Finding on Apple Watch, not only iPhone.
  • Want a small, practical gift for someone in the Apple ecosystem.

Key features

Details below come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

  • Works with Find My. Attach AirTag to an item and track it with the Find My app.
  • Expanded Precision Finding. Get step-by-step directions to a lost item on iPhone and now on Apple Watch. The product title describes up to 1.5x the Precision Finding range.
  • Louder speaker. Apple says the speaker is 50% louder, with a new, distinctive chime, so it is easier to hear.
  • Longer range. Upgraded Ultra Wideband and Bluetooth chips let you find items from farther away than before.
  • Share item location. Share AirTag location access temporarily and securely with trusted contacts, third parties, or over 50 airline partners.
  • Replaceable battery. Apple says it works for more than a year on a standard battery that you can replace when your iPhone alerts you.
  • Privacy. Only you or authorized users can see the AirTag's location, and location data and history are never stored on AirTag itself.

Apple marks several of these features with footnotes on the listing, so check the full Amazon page for compatibility requirements.

How to use it

1. Set it up. Bring AirTag near your iPhone or iPad and follow the one-tap setup. 2. Attach it. Add it to a keychain, wallet, bag, or suitcase using a holder or keyring, or tuck it into a pocket of the item you want to track. 3. Find it. Open Find My to see the item, play a sound, or use Precision Finding for step-by-step directions when you are nearby. 4. When traveling, put one in each checked bag. If a bag goes missing, you can share its location with a participating airline. 5. Replace the battery when your iPhone tells you it is low.

Quick FAQ

Does AirTag work with Android phones?

The listing describes setup with iPhone or iPad and tracking through Apple's Find My app, so it is designed for Apple device owners.

No. Apple says it runs for more than a year on a standard replaceable battery, and your iPhone alerts you when it is time to replace it.

Only you or people you authorize. Apple says location data and history are never stored on AirTag itself.
