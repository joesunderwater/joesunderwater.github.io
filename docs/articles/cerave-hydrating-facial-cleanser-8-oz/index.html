Who it's for

CeraVe makes this cleanser for normal to dry skin. It suits anyone who wants a gentle daily wash rather than a foaming, deep-cleaning gel, and people who are building a short, no-fuss routine around a cleanser and a moisturizer. Because it is free of added fragrance and carries the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance, it is also a common pick for people who prefer gentle, low-fuss formulas.

If your skin is very oily or you like a rich lather, this may not be the feel you are after. It is a creamy, non-foaming texture. Check the brand's other cleansers if you want something made for oily skin.

How to use it

CeraVe's directions are simple. Wet your skin with lukewarm water, work a small amount of cleanser between your palms, and massage it onto your face in gentle circular motions. Rinse well and pat dry with a clean towel instead of rubbing.

Because the texture is creamy rather than foamy, you will not see much lather. That is normal for this formula. Many people use it morning and night, and it works as a gentle second step after an oil cleanser or makeup remover on heavy-makeup days. CeraVe suggests following it with a moisturizer, such as CeraVe Moisturizing Cream, while skin is still slightly damp.

A practical tip: keep one bottle by the sink and one in the shower, since it doubles as a body wash for dry patches. As with any new skincare product, try it on a small area first if your skin is reactive, and check the ingredient list on the package for anything you avoid.
