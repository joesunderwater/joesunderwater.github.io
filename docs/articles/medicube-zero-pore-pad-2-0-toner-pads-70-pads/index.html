  • Prefer a pad you can toss in a routine after cleansing, morning or night, as your skin tolerates it.

How to use it

medicube's directions on the listing are straightforward:

1. Cleanse your face first. 2. Swipe the embossed side over your skin, focusing on areas where you notice texture or visible pores. 3. Flip the pad and follow with the silky side. 4. Finish by lightly patting your skin so the remaining essence can absorb.

After that, continue with your usual serum and moisturizer. Since the pads contain AHA and BHA, it is sensible to wear sunscreen during the day and avoid stacking several other strong exfoliants in the same routine. If your skin feels irritated, cut back on how often you use them.
