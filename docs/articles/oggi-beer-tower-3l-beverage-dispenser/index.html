How to use it

Before the party, fill the ice tube and put it in the tower. Then pour in your drink of choice, whether that is beer, a batch of margaritas, sangria, or lemonade for the kids. Set the tower on a stable, level surface where guests can reach the spigot, and let everyone pour their own.

At about 30 inches tall, the tower makes a good centerpiece on a bar, a kitchen island, or a sturdy folding table. For a long party, keep a backup ice tube ready in the freezer if you have one, or refill and refreeze between rounds. Refill the tower as needed so the drinks keep flowing.

When the party is over, empty the tower and take it apart to clean the tube, the ice tube, and the spigot. Check the product instructions for care and cleaning details.
