How to use it

Place it on the floor under your desk or in front of your sofa. Sit in a stable chair at a comfortable distance so your feet rest flat on the pedals and your knees are not jammed up. Start in Manual mode at a low speed and let your legs settle into the motion.

Use the remote to raise or lower the speed without leaning down. Once you are used to it, try the Auto programs (P1 to P3) for variety, or switch the direction to work your legs a little differently. Short sessions spread through the workday are an easy way to start.

When you are done, use the built-in handle to move it out of the way or into a closet.
