How to use it

  • Dry dusting inside. Use a soft synthetic brush to sweep dust out of air vents, around buttons and screens, and along the seams of the dash and console. Work with a vacuum nozzle nearby to catch what you loosen.
  • With interior cleaner. Spray a little interior cleaner on the brush (not directly on electronics), agitate textured plastic or seat seams, then wipe with a microfiber towel.
  • Wheels and emblems. Use a boar's hair brush with soapy water or wheel cleaner around lug nuts, between spokes, and around badges, then rinse.
  • Keep sets separate. Use the same brushes for wheels every time and keep different ones for the interior so brake dust and grit do not end up on your dash.
  • Rinse and dry. Rinse the brushes after each use and let them dry bristles-down or flat.
