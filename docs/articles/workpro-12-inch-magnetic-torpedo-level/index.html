Who it's for

This level suits homeowners hanging shelves, art, and curtain rods; DIYers installing cabinets, brackets, and appliances; and anyone working with metal pipe, conduit, or steel studs, where the magnetic edge helps. The multi-pitch vial is useful for jobs that need a set slope rather than dead level. How to use it

  • Leveling a shelf or bracket. Set the level on the shelf or bracket and adjust until the horizontal vial's bubble sits centered between the lines.
  • Checking plumb. Hold the level against a post, door jamb, or upright and read the vertical vial.
  • 45-degree work. Use the 45-degree vial for braces and angled cuts or installs.
  • Pipe and conduit. Rest the V-groove on round stock so the level stays put while you read it. On steel pipe, the magnetic edge can hold the level in place, freeing up both hands.
  • Setting a slope. For drains or outdoor surfaces that need a slight pitch, use the multi-pitch vial and its 1/8 inch per foot increments.

Use the top-view holes when the level sits below eye height, such as on a low shelf or the top of an appliance. When you're done, hang it from its hole on a pegboard so it doesn't get knocked around in a toolbox.
