## How to use it

Slide the bracket over the top edge of the door so the hooks hang on the side you want, then close the door to make sure it shuts smoothly.

Ideas for where it helps most:

1. **Bathroom door.** Give each person a hook for a towel and a robe.
2. **Entry or mudroom.** Hang coats, bags, and the dog's leash by the door.
3. **Bedroom.** Use it for tomorrow's outfit, a bathrobe, or a purse.
4. **Laundry room or closet.** Hang reusable bags, aprons, or items waiting to be ironed.

Because each hook is a double hook, you can hang two lighter items on one hook, such as a hat above a scarf.
