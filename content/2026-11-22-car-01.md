## How to use it

- **Install.** Slide the filler down into the gap between the seat and the console, lining up the built-in slot with the seat belt catch so the catch pokes through. Push it down until it sits snugly along the gap.
- **Check the buckle.** After installing, buckle and unbuckle the seat belt to make sure the buckle is easy to reach and latches normally.
- **Do both sides.** Put the second filler on the passenger side, where bags, phones, and snacks tend to slip too.
- **Use the extras.** Put the slide-free pad on the dash or console for sunglasses or a phone, and keep the LED card light in the glovebox or a door pocket for finding things in the dark.
- **Clean now and then.** Pull the fillers out occasionally to wipe them down and vacuum the gap underneath.
