## How to use it

1. **Measure first.** Check your freezer's inside length, width, and depth to see how many bins fit side by side and stacked.
2. **Sort by category.** Give each bin a category, such as chicken, beef, vegetables, leftovers, or desserts.
3. **Stack smart.** Put the items you use least in the bottom layer and everyday items in the top bins.
4. **Lift, don't dig.** Use the side handles to lift the top bin out, grab what you need from below, and set it back.

A label or clip tag on each handle saves time when the lid is open and cold air is escaping. When it's time to defrost, you can lift all the bins out quickly.
