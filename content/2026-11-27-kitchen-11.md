## How to use it

1. **Drop a liner into the basket or tray.** No need to trim or fold it.
2. **Add the food on top.** The listing recommends putting enough food on the paper; a liner with nothing on it is light and can move around when the fan runs.
3. **Cook as usual,** staying within the 428°F temperature limit listed for the paper.
4. **Lift out and toss.** Grease and stains stay on the liner, so the basket usually just needs a quick wipe.

A few practical ideas:

- **Saucy wings and glazed vegetables.** These are the foods most likely to bake onto a bare basket.
- **Reheating leftovers.** A liner keeps pizza, fries, or breaded foods from leaving crumbs behind.
- **Party serving.** The bowl shape doubles as a disposable tray for snacks, as the listing suggests.
