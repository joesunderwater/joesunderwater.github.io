## Who it's for

This Elite net is for golfers who want a more robust home or backyard cage than a basic pop-up or lightweight practice net, and who may add a projector screen later. It is designed for full swings with real golf balls, indoors or outdoors, using drivers, irons, and wedges.

The 10-foot size needs a fairly wide practice space. GoSports’ materials for the 10-foot Elite describe an assembled size of about 10 ft wide by 7.5 ft tall by 3.5 ft deep, with 10 x 7.5 ft netting. Measure your garage, basement, or patio, and leave room to stand, swing, and miss a little left or right. If you only have a tight corner, look at a smaller Elite size instead of forcing this one.

## How to use it

Assemble the steel frame on a level surface and attach the netting so it hangs evenly, then set the net so a mishit still lands in the hitting area, not on a window or a parked car. Leave enough depth in front of the net for a full swing and, if you use a launch monitor, for that device’s ball-to-net distance. A hitting mat is not part of this listing; add one if you are practicing on a hard floor.

When you are ready for a simulator picture, add the matching GoSports impact screen rather than projecting onto the open net. Take the net down with the same short process and store it in the carrying case if you need the garage back. Inspect the netting and frame fittings before heavy sessions, especially after outdoor use.
