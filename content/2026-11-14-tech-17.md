## Key features

- **100W max on a USB-C port.** Anker says the GaN design delivers 100W output on a USB-C port and can sustain peak performance longer than an average 100W charger.
- **Three ports.** The listing's specs show two USB-C ports (each up to 100W max on its own) and one USB-A port (up to 33W max). When several devices are plugged in, the power is shared between ports, and the listing details the splits.
- **Smart display.** The high-definition display shows real-time charging status, temperature, and port performance. The listing title also mentions touch control.
- **Travel-sized.** Anker lists the size as 2.72 x 2.17 x 1.34 inches with a foldable plug, small enough to slip into a backpack, shoulder bag, or coat pocket.
- **Low-current mode.** The USB-C2 or USB-A port automatically switches to a low-current mode for devices like earbuds when output stays at or below 5W for one minute.
- **ActiveShield 4.0.** Anker says it monitors temperature over 1,000,000 times a day.
- **In the box:** the charger, a 4.9 ft USB-C to USB-C cable, a welcome guide, and an 18-month warranty.

## How to use it

Fold out the prongs, plug it into the wall, and connect your laptop to a USB-C port with the included cable. Use the second USB-C port for a phone or tablet and the USB-A port for an older cable or accessory. Glance at the display to see each port's status and temperature.

For travel, fold the plug back in and toss it in your bag in place of two or three separate bricks. At a desk, keep it behind your monitor or on a power strip so one outlet handles your whole setup. If you are charging earbuds or a smartwatch overnight, plug them into USB-C2 or USB-A so the low-current mode can kick in.
