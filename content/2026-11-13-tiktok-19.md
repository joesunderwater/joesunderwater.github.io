## How to use it

**On a nightstand:** set alarms by voice and tap the Echo Dot to snooze in the morning. Ask for the weather while you get ready, or play a podcast or calming playlist at night.

**In the kitchen:** hands-free timers are one of the most practical uses. Ask Alexa to set a timer while your hands are busy, add music while you cook, or ask quick questions without picking up your phone.

**In a home office:** play background music over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, and use routines to make your setup feel more automatic. Because the listing mentions built-in motion and indoor temperature sensors, you can create routines that react to those, such as turning on a compatible smart plug when you walk in, depending on your devices.

If you have an eero mesh network, placing the Dot in a room with weaker coverage can also help fill a gap, since eero Built-in lets it work as an extender.

Whenever you want the microphone off, use the mic off button.
