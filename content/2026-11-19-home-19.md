Sometimes you don't need the big vacuum. You need something you can grab from a closet to clean up crumbs under the table, a trail of dirt by the door, or the stairs. The BISSELL Featherweight 2033 is a small, light stick vacuum built for those quick jobs. It is on the Home Essentials board for small spaces and fast daily pickups.

## Who it's for

BISSELL pitches the Featherweight for quick pickups on sealed hard floors and area rugs, and for tight spaces like dorm rooms and apartments. It suits anyone who wants a light, easy-to-store vacuum as a main cleaner for a small place, or as a grab-and-go helper next to a full-size vacuum.

It is a corded model. That means no battery to charge, but you will need an outlet nearby. If you want a cordless stick vacuum, this isn't it.

## How to use it

Plug it in and use it in stick mode for kitchen floors, entryways, and around furniture. Its light weight makes it easy to reach under chairs and into corners.

To clean stairs, counters, or furniture, convert it to a hand vacuum as described in the manual. Use the crevice tool for baseboards and between cushions, and the floor nozzle for stair treads.

Watch the clear dirt cup and empty it when it fills up. Clean the filter regularly. The listing's troubleshooting notes say a clogged, wet, or dirty filter or a clogged dirt container can reduce suction, and that dust blowing out of the vent can mean the filter isn't secured properly. If it won't run, check that the cord is firmly plugged in and that a breaker hasn't tripped.
