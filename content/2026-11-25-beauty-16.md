## How to use it

Step out of the shower and gently squeeze excess water from your hair. Bend forward and place the wide end of the towel at the nape of your neck, with the rest hanging down over your head. Gather your hair into the towel, twist it from the base toward the end until it forms a firm roll, then flip the twist back over the top of your head. Slip the loop over the button at the back to lock it in place.

Leave it on while you do skincare or makeup, then let your hair finish drying as usual. Rotate between the two towels and machine wash them regularly so each one is fresh and absorbent for the next wash day.
