## How to use it

- **Pick the spot first.** Sit in the driver's seat and choose a flat area on the dash where the phone will be easy to glance at without blocking your view of the road or any airbag area.
- **Prep the surface.** Clean and dry the spot so the adhesive has the best chance to bond, then press the base down firmly.
- **Add a ring if needed.** For a phone or case without MagSafe, attach one of the included metal rings to the back of the phone or case.
- **Set your angle.** Adjust the ball joint for portrait navigation or landscape, then just snap the phone on each time you get in.
- **Set up before you go.** Start your route or call before pulling out, and follow local laws on handheld phone use while driving.
