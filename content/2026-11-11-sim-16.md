## How to use it

Place the pedals on the floor under your desk, centered on your seat, at a distance where you can push either pedal fully forward with a slight bend in your knee. A hard floor or a mat under the base helps them stay in position. Adjust the footrests to fit your shoes, and attach the heel rests if you like a more planted feel.

Plug the USB cable into your PC, then open your sim's controls menu. Assign the rudder axis to the pedals and the two toe brake axes to the left and right brakes. If your sim lets you, add a small dead zone or sensitivity adjustment to suit your feet.

Turn the tension dial to set how much effort the pedals take. A lighter setting is easier for long sessions; a firmer setting can make small rudder corrections easier to control. Practice taxiing with differential braking first, using one toe brake to tighten a turn, then work on keeping the nose straight on takeoff and on crosswind landings.
