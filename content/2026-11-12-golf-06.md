## How to use it

If you own the GoSports ELITE net, attach the screen according to the included instructions so it sits evenly across the hitting area. If you are hanging it on its own, use the four corner grommets and the shock cords to keep some give in the surface, which helps the screen absorb impact. Pull it taut enough for a clear picture, but do not stretch it like a rigid wall.

Place the projector so the image fills the usable screen without spilling heavily onto the frame or floor. Short-throw models are often easier in a garage, but any compatible projector can work if the throw distance is right. Keep the hitting area clear, use a proper mat, and inspect the polyester surface for wear. When you want a movie night, leave the clubs in the bag and use the same screen as a large backdrop.

This screen is the projection surface only. You still need a net or enclosure behind or around it if you want help containing mishits, plus a launch monitor if you want simulated golf data.
