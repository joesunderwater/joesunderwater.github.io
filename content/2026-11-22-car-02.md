## How to use it

- **Set it up.** Unfold it to one or two compartments depending on what you carry, and add the extra divider if you want smaller sections.
- **Anchor it.** Press it onto the trunk carpet so the hook and loop strips grab, then attach the cargo straps to the trunk interior for more stability.
- **Zone your stuff.** One side for groceries, one side for sports gear or tools, and the mesh pockets for things like cleaning cloths, a tire gauge, or an umbrella.
- **Build a car kit.** Keep jumper cables, a first aid kit, a flashlight, and a blanket in one compartment so they are always in the car.
- **Fold it away.** When you need the whole trunk, buckle it closed and fold it flat.
