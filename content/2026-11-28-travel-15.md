## How to use it

1. **Wear it with the opening in front** for all-around support, so your chin is less likely to drop when you doze off.
2. **Turn it if you lean.** If you tend to sleep against the window, shift the thicker part toward that side.
3. **Clip it on the outside of your bag** with the snap strap while you walk through the airport, keeping space free in your carry-on.
4. **Pair it with an eye mask and earplugs** for a better shot at real sleep on overnight flights.
5. **Wash the cover after trips.** Remove the velvet cover and wash it so the pillow stays fresh.

At home, it can be used while reading in bed or relaxing on the couch.
