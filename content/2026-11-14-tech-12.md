## How to use it

Follow Meta's included instructions to swap the stock strap for the Elite Strap. Then put on the headset and twist the dial at the back until it feels snug but not tight. Next, adjust the top strap. That strap carries some of the weight, so getting it right can take pressure off your cheeks and forehead. Find the position where the image looks sharp and the headset stays in place when you turn your head.

For fitness games, tighten the dial a little more before you start so the headset stays steady. For movies or slower games, loosen it slightly. If you store the headset in Meta's Carrying Case or use one of the listed docks, the strap still works with them. Just remember it won't fit the Compact Carrying Case.
