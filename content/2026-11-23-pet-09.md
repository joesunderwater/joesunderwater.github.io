## Who it's for

This brush is for dogs and cats with long or short hair, including puppies and small pets the listing mentions, such as rabbits. It is a good everyday tool if your pet blows coat in the spring and fall, or if you want a quick tidy between baths. The rounded plastic tips on the pins are meant to be gentler on skin than sharp, unfinished wire.

It is a deshedding and grooming brush, not a dematting rake for tight, neglected mats. Work through tangles carefully, and keep sessions short on thin-skinned or short-coated pets so you are not over-brushing.

## How to use it

Brush in the direction of the coat with smooth, light strokes, starting at the back and working toward the legs, belly, and tail. Do not jam the pins into the skin. When the head fills with fur, press the release button, peel the hair off, and keep going. A few minutes a day during heavy shed is often more comfortable for your pet than one long session.

Afterward, retract the pins, remove leftover fur, and wipe the head clean. Store it with the pins retracted so they stay in good shape.
