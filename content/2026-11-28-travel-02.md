## How to use it

1. **Match the cube to the clothing.** Follow Veken's guide: small items in S, tops in M, jeans in L, sweaters and jackets in XL.
2. **Fill, then zip the main zipper.** Lay clothes flat or rolled and close the cube first.
3. **Zip the compression zipper.** This second zipper pulls the cube down to a slimmer profile.
4. **Stack in your suitcase.** Place the larger compressed cubes on the bottom and fill gaps with the smaller ones.

A few packing ideas:

- **Outfit cubes.** Pack each day's outfit together in an M cube for easy mornings.
- **Ski or winter trips.** Use the XL cube for a puffy layer and the L cubes for base layers and fleece.
- **Shared suitcase.** Two M and two L cubes make it easy to split a suitcase between two people.
- **Dirty laundry.** Save one cube for worn clothes so they stay apart from clean ones.

Compression packs more clothing into less space, but it doesn't shrink weight. Keep an eye on airline weight limits if you fill every cube.
