## How to use it

Adjust the strap before you lie down so the mask feels snug but not tight. It should rest over your eyes without pressing on them. Position it so the edges cover the bridge of your nose and the corners of your eyes, where light tends to sneak in.

For travel, keep one mask in your carry-on or bedside bag so it is ready for flights, hotel rooms, or naps in the car. Because it is a 2-pack, you can also rotate the masks between washes. Hand wash gently, as the care label directs, and let it air dry.
