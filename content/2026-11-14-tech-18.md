## How to use it

Pair the watch with the Garmin Connect app and set up your profile. Each morning, check the morning report to see how you slept and whether your body is ready for a hard day. Then follow the daily suggested workout, or pick a Garmin Coach or race plan if you have an event on the calendar.

On the run, start the run profile and let GPS track pace and distance. Afterward, look at training effect to see what the session did for your fitness and recovery time to plan your next hard effort. If you run alone, set up incident detection and Assistance before heading out.
