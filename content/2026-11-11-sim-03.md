Direct-drive wheels used to sit at the expensive end of sim racing, because the motor is mounted straight to the steering shaft instead of driving the rim through belts or gears. The MOZA R5 bundle is one of the compact kits that made that layout more approachable: a 5.5 Nm direct-drive base, a quick-release steering wheel, SR-P Lite pedals, and a desk clamp in one PC-focused package.

## Who it's for

The R5 bundle is for PC sim racers who want their first direct-drive wheel, or who are moving up from a belt- or gear-driven starter set. It is PC only; it is not a PlayStation-licensed wheel. MOZA positions it for enthusiasts who still need a compact setup, including a desk clamp for a table or a light rig, and who race titles such as iRacing, Assetto Corsa Competizione, and F1.

It is a good match if you want a complete ready-to-run kit rather than mixing a base, wheel, and pedals from different brands on day one. The ecosystem is expandable later with other MOZA wheels and pedals, but the bundle itself is designed to get you on track with the parts in the box.

## Key features

Details below come from MOZA’s official R5 Racing Bundle page:

- Direct-drive wheel base with 5.5 Nm of peak torque, a 15-bit encoder, a 1000 Hz USB refresh rate, and a maximum steering angle of 2700°. The housing is aviation-grade aluminum alloy.
- ES steering wheel: an 11-inch rim with a microfiber-leather wrap, an aluminum-alloy frame and front plate, 22 buttons, 10 LED indicators, and MOZA’s quick-release system.
- SR-P Lite pedals in high-strength steel, with Hall-effect sensors, adjustable pedal spacing and plate height, and support for input and output-curve adjustments in software. This bundle’s pedal set is a two-pedal throttle-and-brake design as listed by MOZA; confirm current contents on the listing if a clutch is important to you.
- A standard desktop mounting clamp, plus four-hole bottom mounting if you later move the base to a cockpit.
- MOZA Pit House software for Windows, used to tune force feedback, check device status, apply presets, adjust pedals, and update firmware. A mobile app can also change base, wheel, and pedal settings.
- Force-feedback features include MOZA’s NexGen 4.0 algorithm, FFB decoupling, an equalizer, custom curves, an interpolation mode, and an iRacing 360 Hz mode.
- Official compatibility with a long list of PC racing games, including Assetto Corsa, ACC, iRacing, F1 titles, Forza Horizon, rFactor 2, and others published on MOZA’s site.

## How to use it

Clamp the base to a solid desk or bolt it to a rig, fit the ES wheel on the quick release, and place the SR-P Lite pedals so the brake can take firm pressure. Connect power and USB, then install Pit House and update firmware before the first serious session. Use a beginner or game-specific preset if you want a safe starting point, then raise torque and detail as you get used to direct-drive response, which feels more immediate than a belt-driven wheel.

Tune the pedal curves in Pit House once the physical spacing is comfortable. A linear throttle and a slightly delayed or progressive brake curve are common starting points, but the useful work is making the brake consistent for your foot, not copying someone else’s file blindly. If you race iRacing, look at the 360 Hz force-feedback mode after the basics feel stable.

Because the R5 is compact, a light table can still flex under 5.5 Nm. A sturdy desk or a dedicated stand will make the feedback cleaner. If you later add a shifter, a different MOZA wheel, or upgraded pedals, check MOZA’s official compatibility chart for the R5 base.

## Quick FAQ

### Does the R5 work on PlayStation?

No. Official specifications list the platform as PC. If you need a console-licensed wheel, look at a different bundle.

### How strong is the force feedback?

The base is rated at 5.5 Nm of peak torque, delivered through a direct-drive motor rather than belts or gears.

### What is included in the bundle?

MOZA lists the R5 base, the ES 11-inch quick-release wheel, SR-P Lite pedals, a desk clamp, a power supply, a USB cable, and a toolkit. Confirm the exact packing list on the Amazon page.

### Can I change settings in software?

Yes. Pit House on PC is the main control app for force feedback, pedals, presets, and firmware. A companion app can also adjust settings.
