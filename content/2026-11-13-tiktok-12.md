## How to use it

The routine is short:

1. Cleanse your face and pat the area completely dry. Hydrocolloid sticks best to clean, dry skin, so skip heavy creams on that spot right before applying.
2. Peel one dot from the sheet and press it over the blemish, smoothing the edges.
3. Leave it on overnight. In the morning, peel it off gently.

A few practical tips: keep a sheet in your bathroom drawer so it is there when you need it, and apply the patch as the last step of your night routine. Because the patch is thin and translucent, some people also wear one during the day around the house.
