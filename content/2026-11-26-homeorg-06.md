Spice jars lined up in a single row at the back of a cabinet are hard to see and harder to reach. A tiered shelf fixes that by lifting each row a step higher than the one in front, so every label is visible at a glance. The Zri Bamboo Expandable 3-Tier Spice Rack does that in bamboo and stretches to fit your cabinet, which is why it made the Home Organization / Practical Finds board.

The photo on this page is an illustrative image of the product, not Amazon's photo. Check the Amazon listing for exact dimensions and details before you order.

## Who it's for

This rack is for home cooks who are tired of digging for the cumin, and for anyone with a cabinet or pantry shelf full of small bottles. Zri Bamboo says it also works for vitamin bottles, essential oils, and cosmetics like lipstick and nail polish, so it can be just as useful in a bathroom cabinet.

Because it expands, it fits a range of cabinet widths. Measure your shelf first: the rack is listed at 12.70 to 22.20 inches long, 7.70 inches deep, and 4.30 inches high.

## How to use it

Zri Bamboo is specific about one thing: set the rack **horizontally** in the cabinet, not on its end. Then:

1. **Measure your cabinet or shelf** and extend the rack to fit.
2. **Put the tallest bottles at the back** and the shortest in front so every label shows.
3. **Group by how you cook.** Baking spices on one tier, everyday seasonings on another, and specialty blends on the third.
4. **Try it elsewhere.** On a bathroom shelf it can hold vitamins, nail polish, or lipstick; on a desk it can corral small supplies.

To clean it, wipe it down with a damp cloth and let it air dry completely. Keep it out of the dishwasher.

No. Zri Bamboo says to wipe it with a damp cloth and air dry thoroughly, and not to soak it or put it in the dishwasher.

No. The brand suggests it for vitamin bottles, essential oils, and cosmetics like lipstick and nail polish, too.
