## How to use it

1. **Sort by size.** Put bulky items like a power bank, wall charger, or small hard drive in the divided compartment.
2. **Loop cables under the elastic bands** so each cord stays coiled and separate.
3. **Use the mesh pockets** for small items like SD cards, flash drives, and earbuds, or slip a phone or small tablet into the larger mesh layer.
4. **Adjust the dividers** as your kit changes, for example when you add a camera for a trip.
