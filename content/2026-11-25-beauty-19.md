The Pure Daily Care NanoSteamer is a countertop facial steamer built for exactly that, and it earned a place on the Beauty and Skincare Finds board because it does more than one job: facial steamer, room humidifier, and towel warmer in one device.

## Who it's for

The NanoSteamer is for people who like a regular self-care routine at home and want a dedicated steamer instead of leaning over a bowl of hot water. It suits anyone who steams before a face mask or skincare, or who wants a warm towel for a quick spa-style moment.

It also fits people who would like a small humidifier for a bedroom or desk, since that is one of its three modes. If you already do your own blackhead and blemish care, the included stainless steel kit is meant to be the follow-up step after steaming.

## How to use it

Start with a clean face. Following the listing's directions, fill the NanoSteamer with water, turn it on, and let it begin producing steam. Sit comfortably with your face a safe distance from the nozzle and steam for a few minutes. Afterward, you can follow up with the included skin kit if you use extraction tools, then go on to your usual mask, serum, or moisturizer.

Switch modes when you want the humidifier for a room or the towel warmer for a warm compress. Pure Daily Care's safety notes are worth following: always unplug the device before refilling the tank, keep the device and steam away from children and pets, do not leave it running unattended, let it cool completely before handling or storing, and keep it clean and dry between uses. Do not use the humidifier mode near an open flame or flammable materials.
