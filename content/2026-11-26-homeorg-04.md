## How to use it

Assemble the bench following the included instructions, then place it against a wall near your door.

Practical setup ideas:

1. **Everyday shoes on the top shelf, extras below.** Keep the pairs you wear most within easy reach.
2. **Use the top as a drop zone.** Set a small tray on one end for keys and mail, and leave the rest clear for sitting.
3. **Add a basket.** A small basket on a shelf can corral umbrellas, dog leashes, or reusable bags.
4. **Try it in other rooms.** In a bathroom it can hold towels and baskets; in a living room it can serve as a plant stand or low side table.

Stay within the brand's weight ratings: up to 22 lb per shelf and up to 286 lb on the top.
