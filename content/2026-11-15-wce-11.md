A good portable speaker earns its spot on the White Collar Electronics board by being the one piece of gear that goes everywhere: the tailgate, the boat, the garage workbench, and the backyard grill. The JBL Charge 6 is the latest version of JBL's long-running Charge line, and it is built around two ideas — fuller sound than you would expect from something you carry by a strap, and a body tough enough to survive water, dust, and the occasional drop.

## Who it's for

The Charge 6 fits guys who want one speaker that can handle both indoor and outdoor duty without babying it. JBL rates it IP68 waterproof and dustproof, and says it is built to survive a 1-meter drop onto concrete, so it makes sense for poolside afternoons, fishing trips, job sites, and campouts.

It also suits anyone who already owns, or plans to own, other newer JBL speakers. Auracast support lets you stereo pair two Charge 6 units or link multiple Auracast-enabled JBL speakers to the same playlist. And if your phone tends to die before the party does, the built-in powerbank is a practical bonus.

## Key features

These details come from the official Amazon listing for this ASIN (B0DN2ZCZX6):

- **JBL Pro Sound with AI Sound Boost.** JBL says AI Sound Boost analyzes your music in real time to deliver maximum acoustic performance with less distortion, with richer bass and crisp highs.
- **Up to 28 hours of playtime.** JBL lists up to 24 hours on a single charge, plus up to 4 more hours using the Playtime Boost feature.
- **Fast charge.** About 10 minutes on the charger gets you up to 150 minutes of playtime, according to JBL.
- **IP68 waterproof and dustproof.** Based on JBL's lab test conditions for submersion in up to 1.5 meters of freshwater for up to 30 minutes.
- **Dropproof.** Built to keep working after a 1-meter drop onto concrete.
- **Auracast multi-speaker connection.** Stereo pair two Charge 6 speakers or connect multiple Auracast-enabled JBL speakers.
- **Removable handle strap** for carrying it from the car to the campsite.
- **Built-in powerbank** to charge your phone or other mobile device. JBL notes it does not support laptop charging.
- **Lossless audio over USB-C** from a laptop or other compatible source. JBL says this feature arrives through an over-the-air update in the JBL Portable app and requires compatible lossless content.
- **JBL Portable app** for support, updates, and customizable audio controls.

One practical note from the listing: JBL does not include a USB-C charging cable in the box to cut down on e-waste. Any standard USB-C cable will work.

## How to use it

Charge it fully before the first big outing, then pair it with your phone over Bluetooth and download the JBL Portable app to check for updates and set your EQ. If you are short on time before heading out, the fast-charge spec means even a quick top-off on the way out the door buys you a couple of hours of music.

At the tailgate or on the boat, clip the removable strap on and set the speaker wherever it is convenient — the IP68 rating and drop resistance mean spray, sand, and the odd knock are part of the design brief. Keep your phone topped up by plugging it into the speaker's built-in powerbank.

For a bigger backyard setup, pair two Charge 6 speakers in stereo through Auracast, or add other Auracast-enabled JBL speakers to spread the same playlist across the patio and the garage. At a desk, plug into a laptop via USB-C for lossless playback once the update is installed.

JBL lists up to 24 hours on a charge, plus up to 4 extra hours with Playtime Boost, for up to 28 hours total. Real-world time depends on volume and settings.

Yes. It has a built-in powerbank for mobile devices. It does not support laptop charging.

### Does it come with a charging cable?

No. JBL leaves the USB-C cable out of the box to reduce e-waste; any standard USB-C cable works.
