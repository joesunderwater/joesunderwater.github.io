## How to use it

Pack your gym clothes, towel, and toiletries in the main compartment. Use the small inner pouch for things like earbuds or a lock. Put your shoes in the ventilated shoe pocket so they stay away from your clothes. After the workout or swim, put damp clothes or swimwear in the waterproof wet pocket.

The medium front pocket is good for keys, a wallet, or snacks. The side mesh pocket fits small items you want to reach quickly. For weekend trips, the same layout works: clothes in the main compartment, shoes in their own pocket, and a damp swimsuit or laundry in the wet pocket.

Do not leave wet gear zipped inside for days. Empty the wet and shoe pockets when you get home and let them air out.
