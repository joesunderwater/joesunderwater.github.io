Most tape measures make you pull the blade out and then hit a lock button before it snaps back. The Komelon Self Lock flips that around: pull the blade out, let go, and it stays put. When you're done, press a button and the blade comes back under control instead of whipping into the case. It's a small change that makes measuring alone a lot easier, which is why it's on the Power Tools and DIY board.

## How to use it

**Taking a measurement.** Hook the end on your workpiece and pull the blade out. When you let go, the blade locks where it is, so you can read the number, mark it, or walk to the other end without it retracting.

**Retracting.** Press the top button to let the blade return. Komelon designed it so you control the speed, which helps protect the hook and the blade from slamming into the case.

**Laying out walls.** Use the stud markings every 16 inches to find typical stud spacing when you're planning shelves, hanging cabinets, or framing.

A few habits keep any tape measure working longer: wipe dust and grit off the blade before it goes back in, avoid kinking the blade, and keep the hook straight. Since the case has a rubber overmold, it's built to handle the occasional drop, but it still lasts longer on a belt than bouncing around a toolbox.
