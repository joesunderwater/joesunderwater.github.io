## How to use it

The basic routine is simple: place the crisper plate in the basket, add your food in an even layer, choose a program and temperature, and let the hot air do the work. For fries, wings, and frozen snacks, the Air Fry program is the go-to. Roast works for vegetables, chicken pieces, and other things you would normally put in the oven, while Reheat is handy for leftover pizza or takeout that tends to go soggy in a microwave.

The Dehydrate program runs at the low end of the temperature range, which is how you get chip-style dried fruit or vegetable snacks. The included recipe booklet is a good starting point if you are new to air frying.

A few practical habits help: avoid overcrowding the basket so air can circulate, shake or flip food partway through for even browning, and pop the basket and crisper plate in the dishwasher when you are done. Always follow the timing and safety guidance in Ninja's instructions.
