## How to use it

The listing gives clear directions. Start after your usual skincare has absorbed. Apply the primer evenly to your face with your fingertips, patting it into the skin rather than rubbing. The warmth of your fingers helps it absorb for an even application. Then give it about 30 seconds to set before you apply makeup. e.l.f. suggests starting with a small, pea-sized amount, since you can always add more.

Once the primer feels tacky but no longer wet, go in with foundation, skin tint, or concealer as normal. On busy mornings it slots easily into a short routine: skincare, primer, base, and done. On days you skip foundation, some people simply like the hydrated feel on its own, though the listing positions it as a prep step for makeup.

As with any new face product, the listing advises you to stop use if irritation occurs. A patch test on a small area is a sensible first step if your skin is sensitive.
