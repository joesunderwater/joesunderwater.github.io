## Who it's for

These organizers are for anyone with a cluttered kitchen sink base or bathroom vanity, especially if pipes or a garbage disposal get in the way. They suit households that keep tall bottles like dish soap refills and cleaning sprays under the sink, since the bottom tier leaves room for them.

The adjustable height is useful if you're not sure how much vertical space you have, or if you want to move one unit to a different cabinet later. Measure your cabinet's height, width, and depth and compare them with the listing before ordering.

## How to use it

1. **Measure the cabinet.** Note the inside height and where the pipes and disposal sit.
2. **Set the height.** Choose one of the five height levels between 14 and 17 inches so the top drawer clears your cabinet frame.
3. **Position each unit.** Turn the narrow top tier toward the plumbing, then secure the base with the included suction cups and nano tapes.
4. **Load by height.** Tall bottles go on the bottom drawer, and sponges, gloves, brushes, and small bottles go in the top drawer.

With two units, a common setup is one on each side of the sink pipes. You could also split the pack between the kitchen and a bathroom vanity, or use one in a laundry cabinet.
