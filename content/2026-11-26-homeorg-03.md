This organizer is for anyone whose closet has plenty of hanging rod but not enough shelving: renters, people in older homes with a single rod, and anyone sharing a closet. The roomy shelves fit folded clothing like sweaters and jeans, plus handbags, linens, or towels.

## How to use it

Hang the two hooks over your closet rod or wire rack and let the organizer drop down. Position it where it won't block clothes you reach for every day, such as the end of the rod or between two sections of hanging clothes.

Ideas for filling it:

1. **Sweaters and knits.** Folding sweaters instead of hanging them helps avoid stretched shoulders, and a 12-inch-square shelf fits a neat stack.
2. **Jeans and casual pants.** One shelf per category keeps outfits easy to put together.
3. **Handbags and clutches.** Stand bags upright on a shelf so they keep their shape.
4. **Small things in the side pockets.** Use the mesh pockets for socks, belts, sunglasses, or chargers.
5. **Linens.** In a hall or guest closet, use it for sheet sets and towels.
