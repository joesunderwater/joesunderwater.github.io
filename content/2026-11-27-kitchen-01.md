## How to use it

Open the probe, insert it into the thickest part of the meat (away from bone), and wait for the reading to settle. Compare it with the meat temperature chart that comes in the box or with your recipe.

A few practical ideas:

1. **Check early, not late.** Start checking a few minutes before the recipe says food will be done, so you don't overshoot.
2. **Use it beyond meat.** The listing calls out liquids, deep-frying oil, and candy making, so it can double as your oil and sugar thermometer.
3. **Rinse after every use.** The IP67 rating means you can wash the probe under running water, which helps keep raw and cooked foods separate.
4. **Recalibrate when needed.** If readings ever seem off, follow the included instructions to recalibrate.
