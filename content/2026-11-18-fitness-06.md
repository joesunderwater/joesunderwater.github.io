## How to use it

Give yourself plenty of room overhead and around you, and jump on a flat, non-slip surface. A rubber floor, a mat, or flat outdoor pavement works well. Wear supportive shoes.

Because the rope is heavy, the rhythm is slower than a speed rope. Keep your elbows close to your sides and turn the rope with your wrists and forearms. Start with short rounds, such as 30 seconds on and 30 seconds off, and build up as your shoulders and grip get used to the weight.

Weighted ropes fit well into HIIT circuits and boxing-style conditioning. Pair rounds of jumping with squats, push-ups, or core work. When you are done, coil the rope and put it back in its bag so it is ready for your next trip.
