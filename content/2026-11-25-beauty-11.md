## How to use it

Start with a clean face and no makeup. If you want the cooling effect, chill the tools in the fridge first. Apply your usual facial oil, serum, or moisturizer, or use the roller over a sheet mask, so the stone glides instead of dragging.

Roll slowly, working outward and upward across the cheeks, forehead, and jaw, and use lighter pressure around the eyes. For the gua sha, the brand's directions suggest starting at the neck and working upward in sections with slow, mindful strokes, then moving along the jawline and cheeks. BAIMEI also notes the gua sha can be used on larger muscle areas like the shoulders.

The brand recommends washing the set before and after each use. Clean it with warm water and dry it afterward, and store it in its box so the stones do not knock against anything hard.
