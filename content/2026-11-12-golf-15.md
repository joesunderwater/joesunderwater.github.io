The Perfect Practice Standard putting mat is a 9-foot-6-inch indoor green with a crystal-velvet surface, two hole sizes, printed alignment guides, and a magnetic roll-back track that returns the ball after a made putt. It is a compact way to practice stroke and start line in a living room, office, or next to a simulator bay.

## Who it's for

The Standard 9'6" mat is Perfect Practice’s most common size, aimed at golfers who want a dedicated putting surface that still fits in a typical indoor space. The company says all of its products work for right-handed and left-handed golfers, and that the mats can be used indoors or outdoors, then stored out of long-term UV exposure.

It is a putting trainer, not a full-swing mat. If you want breaking putts or a very long lag, this length offers an 8-foot putting distance, which is enough for short and mid-range work but not a 20-foot putt. Perfect Practice also makes shorter Compact and longer XL mats if your room is smaller or larger.

## Key features

Details below come from Perfect Practice’s official specifications and key-features pages for the Standard mat:

- Standard mat length of 9 feet 6 inches (2.9 m), with a putting distance of 8 feet (2.4 m) and a thickness of 5 mm.
- Two hole sizes: a smaller cup at 6.5 cm (2.56 in) and a larger cup at 8.5 cm (3.35 in).

- Printed alignment markings, distance guides, and track lines to help you see start line and path.
- Automatic ball return using a magnetically connected roll-back track and a pine wood backboard.
- Rubber base designed to grip the floor and help the mat stay in place.
- Intended for indoor or outdoor use on a flat surface; the company recommends bringing it inside after outdoor sessions and vacuuming it if it gets dirty.

## How to use it

Unroll the mat on a flat, level surface and pull it tight up the ramp so the ball does not hitch at the transition. Use the larger cup while you groove a straight-back, straight-through stroke, then switch to the smaller cup when you want a tighter target. The alignment lines are there to show whether the ball started on line; if it rolls off the track, adjust aim or face angle rather than only stroking harder.

The automatic return keeps made putts coming back so you can work in sets. Because speed changes with the floor underneath, try the mat on the surface you will actually use, and do not compare a carpet session directly to a hard-floor session. Perfect Practice also offers instructional videos for its training system if you want a structured practice routine.

When you are finished, store the mat out of harsh sun. It is fine next to a simulator, in an office, or in a living room, as long as the surface underneath is reasonably flat.
