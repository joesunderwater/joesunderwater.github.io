## Who it's for

Garmin describes the fenix 8 as an advanced multisport GPS smartwatch for athletes and adventurers. It is aimed at people who train seriously, whether that means running, strength work, or outdoor sports, and who want a single watch that tracks it all.

It also suits the person who spends time off-grid. Multi-band GPS, a built-in compass, gyroscope, and barometric altimeter, plus a built-in LED flashlight, are tools for hiking, travel, and early or late outings. And because it can take calls from the wrist when paired to a phone, it covers the everyday smartwatch basics too.

## How to use it

Start each day with the training readiness score. If it suggests you are well recovered, that is a good day for an interval session or a heavy strength workout. If it is low, take an easier day. Use the built-in sports apps and strength plans to track sessions, and keep an eye on real-time stamina during longer efforts.

During the workday, the fenix 8 handles smartwatch tasks: pair it to your phone to take a quick call from your wrist, or use your phone's voice assistant to reply to a text when your hands are full.

On the weekend, use multi-band GPS and the compass and altimeter for hikes and outdoor routes. The built-in flashlight is useful for early starts, late finishes, or finding things in a dark tent or hotel room. If you head somewhere without a signal, off-grid voice commands still give you access to select watch controls.
