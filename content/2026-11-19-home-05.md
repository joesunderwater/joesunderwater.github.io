Dust on the shelves, pet dander on the couch, and pollen that drifts in every spring all end up in the air you breathe at home. A small air purifier is one of the easier ways to clean up a single room. The LEVOIT Core 300-P is a compact cylinder made for bedrooms, home offices, and living spaces. It is on the Home Essentials board for anyone who wants cleaner air without a big machine in the corner.

## Who it's for

LEVOIT pitches the Core 300-P for homes with pets and allergens, and as a fit for the bedroom or office. It is a good match for:

- Pet owners who want to cut down on dander and hair in the air.
- People bothered by dust and pollen indoors.
- Light sleepers who want a purifier that can run quietly overnight.
- Apartment and small-space dwellers who need something compact.

One note from the listing: the purifier runs on 120V and is meant for use only in the United States. The Core 300-P does not include Auto Mode or smart connectivity, which are only on the Core 300S-P. You set the fan speed by hand.

## Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for ASIN B07VVK39F7:

- **AHAM Verifide performance.** The listing gives CADR ratings of 143 CFM for smoke, 153 CFM for dust, and 167 CFM for pollen.
- **Room coverage.** LEVOIT says it refreshes the air in a 222 ft² room 4.8 times per hour, or a 1,073 ft² room once per hour.
- **Filtration.** The brand says its filter has 99.97% filtration efficiency for 0.1 to 0.3μm airborne particles, including pollen, dust, and animal dander, based on independent lab testing.
- **Quiet Sleep Mode.** LEVOIT's QuietKEAP technology brings noise down to a stated 24dB in Sleep Mode.
- **Display off and timer.** You can turn the display lights off, and there's a timer for 2, 4, 6, or 8 hours plus a check filter indicator.
- **56W motor.** The listing describes a 56W high-torque motor.
- **Certifications.** LEVOIT lists AHAM Verified, FCC Certified, ETL Listed, and CARB Compliant.
- **Size.** The listing gives dimensions of 8.7"D x 8.7"W x 14.2"H and a weight of 7.9 pounds.

## How to use it

Place the purifier on the floor or a stable surface with some open space around it so air can flow in and out. Pick a fan speed for daytime and switch to Sleep Mode at night. If the display light bothers you, turn it off and set the timer for the hours you want it running.

When the check filter light comes on, swap in a new filter. LEVOIT recommends replacing filters every 6 to 8 months and using genuine LEVOIT filters, since its CADR and coverage figures are based on its original filters. The Core 300-P also has specialty filters for pet allergies, toxins, and smoke. Keep the power cord away from pets that might chew it.
