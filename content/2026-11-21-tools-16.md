## How to use it

For most measurements, hook the end over the edge of a board or wall, pull the blade out, and slide the thumb lock to hold it in place while you mark. When you're working with steel studs, metal framing, appliances, or pipe, let the magnetic hook stick to the surface so you have both hands free.

For inside measurements, like the width of a window opening or a cabinet, press the case against one side and read the blade where it meets the case. The sliding hook is designed to account for its own thickness, so you don't need to add or subtract anything for the hook.

The triangle marks every 19.2 inches are useful if you lay out joists or trusses on that spacing. For everyday jobs like hanging shelves, cutting trim, or checking furniture sizes before you buy, the printed fractions make it quick to read and write down measurements.

Clip it to your belt or pocket with the metal buckle, or hang it from a pegboard hook by the lanyard so it's always easy to find.
