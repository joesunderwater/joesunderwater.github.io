## How to use it

- **Activate it.** Push the vent clip firmly until you hear it click. That starts the fragrance.
- **Clip it on.** Attach it to a vent slat where it sits securely and does not block the airflow you need, like defrost or a vent you aim at your face.
- **Set the intensity.** Start low in a small car or if you are sensitive to fragrance, and turn it up if the scent fades or the car has a stronger smell.
- **Let the air do the work.** Scent moves through the cabin when the fan is running, so you will notice it most with the heat or AC on.
- **Swap on schedule.** Each clip lasts up to 40 days, so a 4-count pack covers several months for one car, or one clip each for a couple of vehicles.

A vent clip works best alongside the basics. Clear out trash, shake out floor mats, and air the car out now and then, and the freshener has less to fight.
