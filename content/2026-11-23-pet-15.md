## Who it's for

This case is for people who already use, or plan to use, an Apple AirTag on a dog or cat collar. The listing also mentions backpacks and luggage, but the everyday use here is a pet collar. It is a holder only: the AirTag is not included.

The listing offers size options such as Small, Regular, and Large, because collar widths vary. Measure the flat width of your collar and pick the size the listing recommends. A holder that is too loose can slide or rotate; one that is too tight may not go on at all.

## How to use it

Measure your collar's width and choose the matching size on the Amazon page. Unbuckle the collar, slide the holder on, and seat the AirTag in the case until it is fully enclosed, following the included directions. Put the collar back on and check that the holder sits flat, cannot slip off an open buckle, and does not rub uncomfortably.

Set up the AirTag in Apple's Find My app before you rely on it, and remember that an AirTag is a helpful locator, not a substitute for a secure collar, ID tags, or supervision. Inspect the case now and then for wear, and replace it if the seal or the slide-on opening gets loose.
