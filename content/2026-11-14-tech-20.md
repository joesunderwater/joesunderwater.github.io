## How to use it

Plug it in, connect it to wifi with the Alexa app, and place it where you listen most. Let room adaptation tune the sound, then adjust bass, mid, and treble to taste. Ask Alexa to play a playlist, set timers, or control your lights and locks.

For movie nights, set up your Echo Studio, then link it from your compatible Fire TV device. If you want a bigger sound later, add a second Echo Studio as a stereo pair or add an Echo Sub. For background music in several rooms, group it with other Echo devices.
