## How to use it

Wear the MP600 in its sheath on your belt or keep it in the truck console or a toolbox. When you need pliers, hold the tool in one hand and flick your wrist to slide the needlenose pliers out. That leaves your other hand free to hold a wire, a fishing line, or a part.

The other tools fold out from the handles. Use the screwdrivers for quick fixes around the house, the wire cutter and crimper for light electrical or automotive jobs, the serrated knife for rope and cord, and the fine-edge knife for packages and general cutting. The ruler helps with quick measurements, and the bottle and can openers make it handy at the campsite or tailgate.

Lock each tool in place before you put force on it, and fold everything away before the tool goes back in the sheath. Wipe it down after wet or dirty jobs to keep the action smooth.
