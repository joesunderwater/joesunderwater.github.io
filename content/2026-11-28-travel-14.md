## How to use it

1. **Wash before first use** and let the bottles dry.
2. **Fill through the wide mouth.** Leave a little room at the top rather than filling to the brim.
3. **Label each bottle** with a strip of tape or a marker so you can tell shampoo from conditioner at a glance.
4. **Close the lids firmly** and stand the bottles in the clear bag.
5. **Keep the bag on top of your carry-on** so it is easy to pull out at security.
6. **Squeeze gently.** The no-drip valve lets you control the amount.

Between trips, rinse the bottles with warm water and let them air-dry with the caps off.
