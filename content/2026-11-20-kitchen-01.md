## How to use it

Cut larger vegetables into pieces that fit the grid, such as halving or quartering an onion. Place a piece on the blades, close the lid, and press down firmly. The dice falls into the 5-cup container. Repeat until you have what you need.

A few ideas to get more out of it:

- **Prep base vegetables for the week.** Dice onions, peppers, carrots, and celery on Sunday and store them in the container or in separate tubs for soups, stir-fries, and omelets.
- **Salsa and pico de gallo.** Dice the onion and peppers in seconds, then mix in the rest.
- **Holiday cooking.** Fullstar mentions it for holiday meals from weeknight dinners to Thanksgiving and Christmas, when stuffing and side dishes need a lot of diced vegetables.
- **Clean up right away.** Use the included brush and scraper to clear the grid, rinse, and put the parts on the top rack of the dishwasher.
