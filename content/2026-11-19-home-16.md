## How to use it

Unplug the dryer first, and shut off the gas supply if you have a gas dryer. Pull the dryer away from the wall and disconnect the vent hose from the back.

Start with the lint brush. Remove the lint screen and slide the brush into the trap housing to pull out lint that has slipped past the screen.

For the duct, screw together as many rods as you need, attach the vent brush, and feed it into the pipe. Connect a drill using the drill attachment, and follow the kit instructions for drill direction and speed so the rods stay connected. Work in and out slowly to loosen lint, then pull the brush back and clear what comes out. It also helps to clean from the outside vent opening if you can reach it.

Have a vacuum and a trash bag ready for the lint you pull out. Wear the included gloves. When you're done, reconnect the vent hose securely, push the dryer back, and run a short cycle to make sure air flows out of the outside vent.
