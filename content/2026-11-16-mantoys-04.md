## Who it's for

The Cryo is aimed at people who want a compact, all-steel folder for daily carry. It also works for anyone who just wants a dependable blade for boxes, cord, and small tasks at work or around the house.

If you prefer quick, one-handed opening and a clip you can set up for either hand, the Cryo fits that bill.

## How to use it

Set the pocket clip for your preferred hand and carry position, then clip the Cryo in your pocket. When you need it, open the blade with one hand and let the assisted opening finish the job. The lock holds the blade in place while you cut.

Use it for everyday jobs: opening packages and mail, breaking down boxes, cutting rope and zip ties, and small tasks at the campsite or on the job. To close, release the lockbar and fold the blade back into the handle, keeping your fingers clear of the edge.

The steel handle gives the Cryo some heft for its size, which many people like for control. Wipe it clean after use and touch up the edge now and then to keep it cutting well. Check local laws on carrying assisted-opening knives before you carry it.
