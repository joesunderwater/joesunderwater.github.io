## How to use it

Pull on a pair for the jobs where bare hands get scraped or slip: moving lumber, assembling furniture, swapping wiper blades, weeding, or carrying boxes. The thin shell keeps enough feel that you can still pick up screws and work small fasteners without taking the gloves off.

Since the coating is on the palms and fingers, the backs of your hands stay ventilated, which helps on warm days. Keep in mind the listing says these are for dry or slightly damp and oily conditions. For heavy oil, standing water, or heavy-duty demolition, use a glove made for those conditions.

When a pair gets dirty, toss it in the wash. With 12 pairs in the box, you can stash some in the shed, a few in the car, and keep a spare pair by the back door. Different colors also make it easy to give each family member their own pairs.

Check the size chart on the listing before ordering so the gloves fit snugly without pinching.
