## How to use it

Fill the bottle, make sure the filter is in place if you are using it, and fill the top container with kibble or treats if you want them. Keep the lock turned to the right when the bottle is in a bag, or it may leak. When your dog needs a drink, turn the lock left, press and hold the button, and let water fill the bowl. When they are done, let leftover water run back in and lock it again.

The bottle is listed as dishwasher safe, but follow the manual for the filter and any parts that should be washed by hand. Pack it for walks, car days, and travel so you are not hunting for a cup at every stop.
