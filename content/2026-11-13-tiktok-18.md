## How to use it

Think of the Restore 3 in two halves: evening and morning.

**At night,** choose a light and sound pairing that feels calming and start it as part of a consistent bedtime routine, ideally around the same time each night. Reading, stretching, or simply putting your phone in another room pairs well with this. Once you are ready to sleep, let a sleep sound play to help mask household noise or street sounds, and dim the clock so it does not light up the room.

**In the morning,** set the sunrise alarm for your wake-up time. Instead of a sudden beep, the light brightens gradually so you wake more naturally.

For best results, keep the unit on a nightstand where the light can reach you, and give the new routine a week or two to feel normal.
