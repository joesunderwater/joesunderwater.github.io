## How to use it

Clip the Leek in your front pocket in whichever carry position you prefer, tip-up or tip-down. When you need it, slide the tip-lock off and open the blade with one hand. The assisted opening takes it the rest of the way, and the frame lock holds it in place while you work.

Use it for the everyday stuff it is built for: breaking down cardboard, opening packages, cutting rope or twine, and light camp chores. When you are done, close the blade and slide the tip-lock back on before it goes back in your pocket. That small habit keeps the blade closed while you carry it.

Because 14C28N steel is described as easy to resharpen, a few passes on a sharpener every so often will keep it working well. Keep it clean and dry after messy jobs. As with any knife, check your local laws on carrying a folding knife with assisted opening.
