Pick a spot with room to stand and play, near an outlet. If you want to use the leaderboards, place it within reach of your Wi-Fi. Once it is set up, connect the cabinet to Wi-Fi so your high scores can post to the global leaderboards.

From there, it is all about the games. Start with Ms. Pac-Man, then try the shooters like Galaga and Galaxian, or the digging and maze games like Dig Dug and Mappy. Rolling Thunder and Rally-X add variety. The light-up marquee and faux coin door make it look the part even when nobody is playing.

It also works as a party piece. Set up a high-score challenge on game night and let guests take turns.
