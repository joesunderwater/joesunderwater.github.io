## How to use it

The brand's directions differ for the two bag types.

**Vacuum storage bags (fill, seal, vacuum):**

1. Place clean, dry items inside.
2. Close the double zipper securely.
3. Remove the air with the included hand pump or a standard vacuum cleaner.
4. Tightly close the valve cap.

**Travel roll-up bags (fill, seal, roll):**

1. Place items inside.
2. Close the double zipper securely.
3. Roll from the zipper end toward the bottom to push the air out. No pump needed.

Make sure everything you pack is completely clean and dry first. Use the jumbo bags for comforters and pillows, the medium and small bags for sweaters and seasonal clothes, and the roll-up bags for suitcases.
