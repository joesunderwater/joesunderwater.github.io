## How to use it

1. **Assign a passport pocket** to each traveler so you always know whose is whose.
2. **Put boarding passes in the boarding sleeve** right after check-in.
3. **Load the card slots** with the cards you will use on the trip.
4. **Use the cash pockets** to keep home and foreign currency separate.
5. **Keep the pen in its holder** for customs and arrival forms.
6. **Use the exterior zip pocket** for your phone or the next ticket you need.

A few practical ideas:

- **Put printed reservations in the transparent pocket** so confirmation numbers are easy to see.
- **Clip keys to the detachable key chain** so house or car keys do not get lost while you are away.
- **Pair it with a crossbody bag or personal item** so the organizer stays within easy reach.
