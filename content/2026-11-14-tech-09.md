## Who it's for

The Pill is a grab-and-go speaker for backyard hangouts, beach and pool days, hikes, and weekend trips. Those are the uses Beats points to in the listing. It works for households with a mix of Apple and Android phones, since Beats supports one-touch pairing and Find My or Find My Device on both. It's also an easy gift because it doesn't lock anyone into one platform.

## Key features

- **Bigger sound.** Beats says the racetrack woofer moves 90% more air than the previous Pill, for deeper, fuller bass. The redesigned tweeter handles crisp highs and mid-range tones.
- **Up to 24 hours of battery.** Beats lists up to 24 hours of playback on a single charge.
- **Doubles as a power bank.** Use the included USB-C cable to charge your phone or another device from the speaker.
- **IP67 dust and water resistance.** Beats says enhanced internal seals help keep sand, dirt, and water out at the pool, at the beach, or in the rain.
- **USB-C audio.** Connect to a laptop or another compatible device over USB-C for high-resolution lossless audio.
- **Portable design.** A removable lanyard and soft-grip silicone body. Beats lists it at about 8.6 in. long and 24 oz.
- **On-device controls.** Buttons on top handle playback, calls, volume, power, and pairing. You can answer calls and activate Siri or Google Assistant from the speaker.
- **Two-speaker modes.** Pair two Pills in Amplify mode for more volume or Stereo mode for separate left and right channels.
- **Class 1 Bluetooth.** Apple's spec page lists Bluetooth 5.3.

## How to use it

Pair it with your phone using one-touch pairing, then clip on the lanyard so it's easy to carry. On a pool or beach day, the IP67 rating means splashes and sand aren't a worry. Rinse it off and let it dry before charging. If your phone runs low, plug it into the Pill with the USB-C cable and keep the music going.

At home, plug it into a laptop with USB-C for wired, lossless playback at a desk. If you have friends with Pills, pair two in Stereo mode for a wider sound at a party. A quick press of the power button shows battery status: green means you're good, and red means it's time to charge.
