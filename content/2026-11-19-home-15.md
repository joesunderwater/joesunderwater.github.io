## How to use it

Mount the extinguisher with the included bracket in a spot that is easy to reach and near an exit path, not right next to the stove or another likely source of fire. Make sure everyone in the house knows where it is.

Check the gauge regularly. If the needle is outside the ready range, or after any use, have it recharged by a certified professional as the listing describes.

Read the label before you ever need it. A common way to remember the steps is PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, and Sweep side to side. Always follow the instructions printed on the unit.

A home extinguisher is for small, contained fires. If a fire is spreading, the room is filling with smoke, or you are unsure, get everyone out and call 911. Your safety comes first.
