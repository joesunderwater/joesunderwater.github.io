## How to use it

- **Put it on the night before.** Lay the cover across the windshield with the soft lining facing the glass and let the magnets settle onto the hood and body.
- **Cover the mirrors.** Slip the side mirror protectors over the mirrors to help keep them clear too.
- **Lift it off in the morning.** Pull the cover off carefully so the snow and ice fall away from the car instead of onto the glass. Tevlaphee says you can lift it off in seconds to reveal a clear windshield.
- **Shake, fold, and store.** Shake off loose snow, fold it up, and put it in the pouch. Let it dry out at home if it is soaked.

If it is very windy or icy, give the magnets a quick check after you install it, and remove the cover before driving.
