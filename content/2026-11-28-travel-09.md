## How to use it

1. **Turn the scale on** and pick pounds or kilograms with the unit button.
2. **Thread the nylon strap through the suitcase handle** and clip it to the metal hook.
3. **Lift by the ergonomic handle** until the bag hangs freely, and hold it still for a moment.
4. **Read the locked weight** on the backlit screen after you lower the bag.
5. **Adjust as needed.** Move dense items like shoes or toiletries to your carry-on or another bag.

A few ideas:

- **Use tare** when weighing items inside a bag or box, so you get the weight of the contents only.
- **Switch to kg** when flying with airlines that list limits in kilograms.
- **Toss it in your carry-on** so it is on hand for the return trip, when bags tend to be heaviest.
