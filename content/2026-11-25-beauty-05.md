## Who it's for

Aquaphor makes this ointment for dry, compromised skin. It suits anyone with chapped lips, cracked hands or cuticles, or rough heels, and people who want one tube that can stand in for a lip balm, hand cream, and foot cream. Aquaphor calls the 7 oz tube an ideal size for the whole family.

It has also become popular for "slugging," a skincare trend where people put a thin layer of ointment over their nighttime routine. Aquaphor's own listing mentions using it as a facial moisturizer and a hydrating mask. Because it is an ointment rather than a lotion, it feels thicker and more occlusive than a cream. That is the point, but it is good to know if you prefer a light finish.

## How to use it

The directions are simply to apply as needed. A little goes a long way, so start with a small dab. For lips, smooth a thin layer on through the day and before bed. For hands and cuticles, rub some in after washing or at night. For cracked heels, apply a thicker layer at bedtime and pull on cotton socks.

If you want to try slugging, use your usual cleanser and moisturizer at night, then press a very thin layer of ointment over the top as the last step.

Follow the label warnings: it is for external use only, so keep it out of your eyes. Do not use it on deep or puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns. Stop using it and ask a doctor if the condition gets worse or symptoms last more than 7 days. The formula contains lanolin alcohol, so check the ingredients if you are sensitive to lanolin.
