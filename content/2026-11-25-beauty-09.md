## Who it's for

COSRX says this cream is suitable for all skin types, including sensitive skin. It is a good fit for anyone who wants moisture without a heavy, sticky finish, people with combination skin who find rich creams too much, and anyone building a "glass skin" style routine. The gel-cream texture also makes it easy to wear under sunscreen and makeup in the morning.

Because skin reacts differently to new ingredients, COSRX recommends a patch test first: try it on your body and wait 24 hours before using it on your face.

## How to use it

Use it as the moisturizing step after cleansing and toning. COSRX's directions are to gently apply an appropriate amount to the face, avoiding the eye and mouth area, then tap the area lightly so the cream absorbs into the skin.

In the morning, let it settle for a minute before sunscreen and makeup. At night, it can be your last step. COSRX suggests pairing it with its Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence for best results, so if you already use that essence, this cream is a natural follow-up. If irritation occurs, stop using it and check with your doctor, as the brand advises.
