## How to use it

Place the roller on the fabric and roll it back and forth in short strokes. The back-and-forth motion is what pulls hair into the chamber, so don't just roll in one direction. Work across a couch cushion in sections, then move to the arms, pillows, and back.

When the chamber fills up or you finish the job, hold the roller over a trash can and press the release button to open it. Empty out the fur, close it, and it is ready for the next round. Keep it near the couch or in the car so it is easy to grab between vacuum sessions.

For best results on furniture, use it often rather than waiting for hair to build up into a thick layer. On delicate fabrics, test a small area first.
