## How to use it

1. **Remove the battery isolation tag** before first use (use the included screwdriver to open the battery compartment).
2. **Turn the scale on** and loop the strap through your suitcase handle, then secure the hook.
3. **Lift the bag by the scale's rubber handle** until it hangs freely off the floor, and hold it steady.
4. **Set the bag down and read the locked weight** on the backlit display.
5. **Compare it to your airline's limit** and move heavy items to your carry-on if you need to.

- **Weigh twice.** Once before you leave home and once before the return flight, after shopping.
