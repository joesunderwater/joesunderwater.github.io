## How to use it

Fill the clean water tank with water and BISSELL formula following the directions in the manual, then plug it in near the stain. Spray the spot, scrub the area with the tool, then pull the tool slowly back over the spot to suction up the dirty water. Repeat a few passes on tougher stains, finishing with a few suction-only passes so the fabric is not left soaking wet.

Treat fresh spills as soon as you can, and blot up any solids before you start. On upholstery and car seats, test a hidden area first to make sure the fabric handles moisture well. After each use, empty and rinse the dirty water tank and let the tool dry before you store the machine.
