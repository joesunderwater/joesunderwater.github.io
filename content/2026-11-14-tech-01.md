## Who it's for

According to Anker's listing, this power bank is aimed at people who need real laptop-level power away from an outlet. It is a good fit if you:

- Work from coffee shops, coworking spaces, or airport gates and want to keep a laptop going without hunting for a plug.
- Fly often. The listing describes it as TSA-approved, flight-ready power, and its capacity is listed at 72.36Wh.
- Carry several devices at once, like a laptop, a phone, and a tablet or earbuds.
- Use both iPhone and Android devices. The listing calls out compatibility with MacBook and iPhone 17 / 16 series devices.

## Key features

Details below come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

- **20,100mAh (72.36Wh) capacity.** Anker says that is enough for up to 4.2 days of video streaming on an iPhone 17 Pro Max, based on its internal lab testing.
- **220W total output across three ports.** There are two USB-C ports and one USB-A port, so you can charge several devices at once.
- **Up to 140W from a single port.** With a 5A cable, Anker says it can charge a 16" MacBook Pro (M4 Pro) to 50% in 28 minutes.
- **100W max recharging.** Using the USB-C input, the listing says the power bank itself reaches 50% in 25 minutes.
- **Two-way charging and app control.** Both are named in the Amazon product title.
- **In the box.** The power bank, a 3.3 ft USB-C to USB-C cable (240W), a travel pouch, a welcome guide, and a 24-month warranty. The listing notes that the charging base is not included.

Anker notes that its performance figures come from internal lab testing and actual results can vary with conditions of use.

## How to use it

A few practical ways to fit it into your routine:

1. **Charge it up before you leave.** Plug it into a USB-C wall charger that can supply up to 100W to take advantage of the fast recharging.
2. **Use the included 240W cable for your laptop.** To get the fastest laptop charging Anker describes, use a cable rated for 5A, like the one in the box.
3. **Share the power.** Connect your laptop to one USB-C port, your phone to the other, and an older device or earbuds to the USB-A port.
4. **Pack it in the travel pouch.** Keep it in your carry-on, not your checked bag, since airlines generally require power banks in the cabin. Check your airline's rules before you fly.

It also makes a practical gift for a student, a frequent traveler, or anyone who works on a laptop away from home.
