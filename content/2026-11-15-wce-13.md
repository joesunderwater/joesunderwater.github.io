## How to use it

Charge the handle fully, then pick a mode. Daily Clean is the obvious starting point; Sensitive or Super Sensitive are there if your gums need a gentler session, and Whitening or Intense when you want something different.

Let the brush do the work. Guide the round head slowly from tooth to tooth instead of scrubbing back and forth, and watch the pressure light — green means you are in the right range. The display keeps track of time so you do not have to count.

If you want more guidance, pair the brush with the Oral-B app. It shows which areas you are hitting and which ones you are missing, which is the fastest way to catch habits like always skipping the back molars.

For trips, pop the brush into the included travel case. Check the Amazon listing for current details on replacement brush heads so you can keep spares on hand.
