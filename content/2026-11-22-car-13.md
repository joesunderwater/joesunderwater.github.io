## How to use it

- **Charge it first.** Charge it fully before it goes in the car, using a 5V/1A USB adapter as the listing recommends, and top it up every so often.
- **Check the right pressure.** Find your car's recommended PSI on the sticker inside the driver's door or in the owner's manual, not the maximum printed on the tire.
- **Connect and preset.** Attach the hose to the valve stem, pick the mode or custom value, and start it. It stops automatically at the target.
- **Mind the duty cycle.** AstroAI says to let it rest for 10 minutes after 15 minutes of continuous use. If overheat protection kicks in, let it cool before charging.
- **Use the light at night.** The flashlight and strobe modes help you see the valve and make you easier to see on a roadside.
- **Beyond the car.** Use the bicycle and ball modes for bikes, e-bikes, and sports balls at home.

An inflator is for low tires, not a fix for a puncture or damaged tire. If a tire keeps losing air, have it checked.
