## Who it's for

Fellow describes the Stagg EKG Pro as a kettle for everyone from brewing champions to beginners. It is a fit if you make pour-over coffee or loose-leaf tea and want water at a specific temperature rather than just “boiling.” The built-in brew stopwatch and preset temperatures make it approachable for someone still learning, while scheduling and hold mode suit people who want hot water ready on their timetable.

It is a water kettle only. Fellow says not to heat milk or other liquids in it, and it is not stovetop safe, since it is designed to be used only with its electric base.

## How to use it

For pour-over, set your target temperature with the dial, or use Guide Mode if you are not sure where to start. Once the water is ready, start the brew stopwatch, pour a little water to let the grounds bloom, then use the gooseneck to pour slowly in circles until you reach your target amount.

For tea, use Guide Mode to pick a preset for the type you are brewing, then use the same stopwatch for steeping time. If you are making several cups, adjustable hold mode keeps the water at temperature while you finish.

To make mornings easier, use the schedule feature so the kettle starts heating before you walk into the kitchen. If you live at elevation, set your altitude once in the menu.

No to both. Fellow says to use it only with its electric base and only with fresh, clean water.
