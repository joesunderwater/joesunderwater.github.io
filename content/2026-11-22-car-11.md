## How to use it

- **Install it on the front seatback.** Loop the straps around the headrest and seat, buckle them, and tighten until the organizer sits flat and does not swing. Check the listing for the exact strap setup.
- **Load the pockets by who uses them.** Put water bottles in the bottle pockets, and wipes, tissues, snacks, coloring books, and small toys in the mesh pockets where kids can reach them.
- **Set up the tablet before you leave.** Slide the tablet into the clear holder, start the movie or show, and let kids tap the screen through the cover without taking it out.
- **Let it take the kicks.** The mat is there so shoes hit it instead of the seat. Wipe it down when it gets dirty, and check the listing for care guidance.
- **Clear it out now and then.** A quick weekly empty keeps old wrappers and lost toys from piling up.

A second one on the other seatback means two kids each get their own pockets and screen, which can head off a few back-seat arguments.
