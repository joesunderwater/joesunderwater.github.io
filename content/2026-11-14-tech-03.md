## Who it's for

Based on the listing, Space One is aimed at people who want to block out everyday noise without a lot of fuss. It is a good fit if you:

- Commute by train or bus, or fly often. Soundcore says the ANC is especially effective against low, steady sounds like engine rumble, air conditioner hum, and train drone.
- Work or study in a noisy café or office where nearby voices are the main distraction.
- Want long listening sessions without charging every day.
- Care about sound quality and use a phone that supports the LDAC codec.

## Key features

Details below come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

- **Upgraded voice reduction.** Adaptive noise cancelling detects outside sounds and sound leakage and auto-calibrates, even if the headphones are not sitting perfectly on your ears. Soundcore compares this to its own Q30 headphones.
- **Five adjustable ANC levels.** Fine-tune noise cancelling for your environment in the soundcore app.
- **LDAC Hi-Res wireless audio.** 40mm custom dynamic drivers support LDAC, which Soundcore says delivers 3x more detail than standard Bluetooth codecs.
- **Long battery life.** Up to 40 hours of playtime with ANC on, and 55 hours with ANC off.
- **Comfort-focused design.** Ear cups rotate 8° to fit the shape of your head, and a soft integrated headband spreads out pressure for longer wear.
- **soundcore app.** Personalize sound with HearID and EQ modes, try Spatial Audio, adjust ANC, and get over-the-air firmware updates.
- **Three mics with AI,** listed in the product title for calls.

Soundcore notes that its noise-cancelling results come from testing under laboratory conditions.

## How to use it

1. **Download the soundcore app** and pair the headphones over Bluetooth. Install any firmware updates the app offers.
2. **Run HearID or pick an EQ mode** to tune the sound to your taste.
3. **Set the ANC level for your situation.** Use a stronger level on a plane or train and a lighter one at your desk.
4. **Turn on LDAC** if your phone supports it and you want the most detail from high-resolution music.
5. **Charge them before a trip.** With up to 40 hours of ANC playtime, a full charge can cover a long travel day and more.

They also make an easy gift for students, commuters, and remote workers.
