## How to use it

Clip the strainer onto the rim of your pot or pan so it covers part of the opening. Hold the pot by its handles, tilt it toward the strainer side over the sink, and let the water drain out while the food stays in the pot.

A few practical ideas:

1. **Pasta night.** Drain spaghetti or penne straight from the pot, then toss with sauce in the same pan.
2. **Vegetables and potatoes.** Drain boiled potatoes before mashing, or blanched green beans before they go into an ice bath.
3. **Rinse fruit in a bowl.** The listing says it drains water from fruits, too, and it attaches to bowls.
4. **Pack it for camping.** Since it folds flat, it is easy to tuck into a camp kitchen bin.

Use pot holders when draining hot water, and keep your hands clear of the steam.
