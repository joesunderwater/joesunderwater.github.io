Not every launch monitor needs a phone in your hand before you can see a number. The Voice Caddie Swing Caddie SC4 PRO is a portable Doppler-radar unit with its own built-in display and voice output, so it can call out shot data on its own outdoors, then connect to the VoiceCaddie S app when you want a 3D driving range or a simulated course at home.

## Who it's for

The SC4 PRO is a match for golfers who want a launch monitor that works as a standalone unit at the range and as a simulator link in the garage. Voice Caddie designed it for indoor and outdoor use, with a 5.3-inch LCD, voice announcements, a remote, and Bluetooth to a smartphone or tablet.

It also suits people who want simulated golf without a computer-only monitor. The device works with the VoiceCaddie S app for a 3D driving range and is compatible with E6 Connect, including a complimentary set of courses and a driving range. It is built around standard golf balls; Voice Caddie says it is not designed to assess non-standard balls, and practice or foam balls are not recommended.

## How to use it

Place the SC4 PRO about 5 feet behind the ball, on the same level as the hitting surface, with the ball lined up to the red mark on top of the unit. Voice Caddie recommends about 10 feet from the ball to a net or screen, and notes that you may need to raise the device if a driver tee is especially high. For some iron shots, the company says you can move the unit to about 4 feet behind the ball.

Turn it on and hit. Outdoors, read the screen or listen for the voice output. Indoors or for deeper practice, connect over Bluetooth to the VoiceCaddie S app and use the 3D range, or open E6 Connect if you want photorealistic courses and your device is supported. Use regular golf balls. If readings look off, re-check distance, level, and alignment before assuming the unit is at fault.

## Quick FAQ

### Do I need a phone to use it?

No. The built-in screen and voice output work as a standalone launch monitor. A phone or tablet is needed for the VoiceCaddie S app and for E6 Connect course play.

### Does it need special golf balls?

No special marked balls are required, but it is intended for standard golf balls, not foam or other non-standard practice balls.

### How far should it sit from the ball?

Voice Caddie specifies about 5 feet behind the ball, level with the hitting surface, and recommends about 10 feet from the ball to a net or screen.

### What simulator software does it use?

The VoiceCaddie S app provides a 3D driving range. The SC4 PRO is also compatible with E6 Connect, with a small set of courses included; extra courses may require a subscription.
