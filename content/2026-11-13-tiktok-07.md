## How to use it

Start with damp, detangled hair rather than soaking-wet hair, and work in sections so the brush can grip each one. Place the brush at the root, give it a moment for lift, then glide it slowly down the length of the section. As you reach the ends, roll the brush slightly under or out to use the rounded edges for a curved, bouncy finish.

Use the High setting for the bulk of drying, Low for finer or more delicate sections, and Cool to help set the shape once a section is done. Clip finished sections out of the way so you do not re-wet them while working on the rest.

For a polished look, finish with a light smoothing product of your choice. Always follow Revlon's included instructions and safety guidance, and unplug the tool to cool before storing.
