## How to use it

Because it comes pre-seasoned, you can start cooking right away. Heat the skillet gradually, add a little oil, and let it get hot before the food goes in. That's what gives you a good sear on steaks, chops, and burgers.

Ideas for getting the most from it:

- **Breakfast.** Lodge mentions eggs sliding easily on the seasoned surface. Bacon, hash browns, and pancakes work well too.
- **Stove to oven.** Sear chicken thighs or a steak on the stove, then finish in the oven, all in one pan.
- **Bake in it.** Cornbread, skillet cookies, and frittatas are classic cast iron recipes.
- **Take it outside.** Use it on the grill for vegetables or over a campfire on your next trip.
- **Serve in it.** The teardrop handle makes it easy to carry straight to the table.

Cast iron gets better with regular use. Clean it after cooking, dry it fully, and keep a thin layer of oil on it to protect the seasoning.
