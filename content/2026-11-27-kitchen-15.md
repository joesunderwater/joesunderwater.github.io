## How to use it

1. **Trim the ends** of the onion and peel it.
2. **Set the onion on a cutting board** and press the tines straight down into it, all the way through.
3. **Slice between the tines** with a sharp knife, using the tines as a guide for even thickness.
4. **Hold the handle, not the food,** so your fingers stay above and away from the blade.
5. **Pull the holder out and rinse it,** or put it in the dishwasher.

A few practical ideas:

- **Tomatoes for burgers and sandwiches.** The tines help keep tomatoes from sliding and losing juice on the board, which the listing calls out.
- **Steady slicing of lemons and limes** for drinks and garnishes.
- **Tenderizing.** The listing says the long tines can pierce meat to help tenderize it before cooking.
- **Store it carefully.** The tines are sharp, so keep it in a utensil holder or a spot where you won't reach in blind.
