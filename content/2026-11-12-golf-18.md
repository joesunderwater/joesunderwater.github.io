## How to use it

Connect the three sections of each stick so they form a 48-inch rod. On grass, push the pointed tip into the turf to hold a line. On a simulator or range mat, lay the sticks on the surface instead of trying to stake them into the mat. A common setup is one stick along your toe line, aimed at the target or just left of it for a right-handed player, and a second stick to show ball position or shaft path.

You can also set a stick on the target line just outside the ball, or use one as a visual for swing path. The point is to give your setup a reference you can see before you take the club back. After practice, take the sections apart and fold the sticks so they fit in the bag.

These rods help you see alignment; they do not guarantee a result on the course. Use them as a check, then hit shots with the same setup.
