## Who it's for

Therabody positions the Mini as a travel essential for quick relief from daily aches, tension, and stress. That makes it a fit for people who want a recovery tool that goes in a carry-on or desk drawer rather than a full-size massage gun that stays at home.

It also suits anyone who likes guided routines. The Mini connects over Bluetooth to the Therabody app, where routines and videos are designed to tell you when and how to use it. If you wear a fitness tracker, the app's Coach by Therabody feature can use your goals, activity, and wearable health data to suggest recovery routines.

It is often bought as a gift, too. Therabody markets it as a gift for men and women.

## How to use it

Start with the attachment that fits the area. Therabody recommends the Dampener for sensitive spots, the Standard Ball for general full-body work, and the Thumb for the lower back and knots. Use the single button to turn it on and step through the three speeds, and watch the LED indicators to see which speed you are on.

At the office, a few minutes on the shoulders, neck, or forearms between meetings is the kind of quick session the Mini is designed for. After a workout or a round of golf, the Therabody app's guided routines can take the guesswork out of where to start and how long to spend on each area.

When you travel, turn on the travel lock before packing it. Bring a USB-C cable, and you can charge it with the same cable you might already use for a phone or laptop.

If you have an injury, a medical condition, or ongoing pain, check with a healthcare professional before using any percussive massage device.

## Quick FAQ

### How long does the battery last?

Therabody lists up to 180 minutes of battery life. It charges over USB-C.

### Can I take it on a plane?

The listing describes the Mini as TSA compliant, and it has a travel lock so it won't switch on in your bag. Check your airline's rules before you fly.

### What attachments come with it?

Three: the Dampener, the Standard Ball, and the Thumb.

### Do I need the app?

No. The Mini works with one-button control. The Therabody app is optional and adds guided routines and personalized recommendations.
