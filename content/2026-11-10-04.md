## How to wear it

The listing’s own styling note is the easiest place to start: pair it with leggings or joggers. That is the classic cozy-fits formula — oversized on top, close or easy on the bottom — and it works for errands, travel days, and at-home wear. A fitted tank or long-sleeve layer underneath can add warmth without much bulk, which is useful if you plan to wear it on a flight or in a cold office.

For a slightly more “streetwear” look, try the hoodie over a shorter top or with jeans instead of joggers, and keep the rest of the outfit simple so the drop-shoulder line stays visible. Because the fit is oversized, tucking the front hem a little, or letting it fall straight, will change how long the hoodie reads. Check the length measurement on the size chart if you care whether it covers the hips.

This is also a straightforward layer for transitional weather: hoodie on, light jacket or shacket over it if you need more warmth. Confirm available colors on Amazon before you decide; the listing shows a range of solid options that can change over time.
