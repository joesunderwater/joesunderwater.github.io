## Who it's for

The Huntsman V3 Pro Mini is aimed at competitive FPS players who want quicker inputs and directional changes. Its small footprint also suits low-sense players who need room to move the mouse, players who travel to offline tournaments or LAN events, and anyone building a minimal white desk setup. If you rely on a number pad, function row, or dedicated arrow cluster, keep in mind that a 60% board leaves those out (though some modifier keys double as arrows, as noted below).

## Key features

- **Analog Optical Switches Gen-2.** Razer lists a 0.1–4.0 mm adjustable actuation range and a 100 million keystroke lifespan.
- **Rapid Trigger.** Keys reset at the slightest lift of a finger for quick, repeated inputs.
- **Razer Snap Tap.** Prioritizes the latest input between two selected keys without releasing the previous one, for near-instant direction changes. Razer notes a firmware update is required.
- **Onboard adjustments.** Change actuation height or Rapid Trigger sensitivity on the fly, guided by an onboard LED array, and save settings to the keyboard with no software required.
- **Dual-purpose modifier keys.** The bottom-right modifiers act as arrow keys when tapped.
- **Textured doubleshot PBT keycaps.** Built for durability, with side-printed secondary functions.
- **Compact 60% form factor.** Easy to position, leaves more room for your mouse, and easy to pack.

## How to use it

Set the keyboard slightly angled or off to the side to give your mouse arm more space. Before you play, update the firmware so Snap Tap is available, then pick the two keys you want it to prioritize, such as your left and right strafe keys.

Use the onboard controls and LED array to set actuation height: a shallow setting for movement keys in shooters, a deeper one if you tend to mistype. Adjust Rapid Trigger sensitivity the same way. Since settings save to the keyboard, you can unplug it, pack it in a bag, and bring the same setup to a friend's place or a tournament. Tap the bottom-right modifiers when you need arrow keys for menus or documents.

## Quick FAQ

### What is Snap Tap?

Razer describes it as a feature that prioritizes your latest input between two selected keys without making you release the first one. It requires the latest firmware.

### Can I change settings without software?

Yes. Razer says you can adjust actuation height and Rapid Trigger sensitivity on the keyboard itself, with an LED array as a visual guide, and save them to the keyboard.

### Does it have arrow keys?

Not dedicated ones. The bottom-right modifier keys work as arrow keys when tapped.
