## How to use it

Keep the glasses where the work happens. A pair hooked on the pegboard above the bench, or sitting on top of the miter saw, gets worn far more often than a pair in a drawer across the room.

- **Fit them first.** Extend the arms and adjust the nose pad so the glasses sit snugly without pinching. The rubber grips should keep them from sliding when you look down at your work.
- **Wear them for the whole task.** Put them on before you start cutting, sanding, grinding, or trimming, and keep them on through cleanup, when dust and debris get kicked up again.
- **Outdoor jobs.** With UV-blocking clear lenses, they work for yard work and outdoor projects as well as the shop.
- **Care.** Clean the lenses gently and store them so the lenses are not rubbing against loose tools, which helps the coating last.

If several people in your house help with projects, a larger pack lets you keep a pair at each work area and one for any helper who shows up.
