## Who it's for

This collar is sold as one size for small, medium, and large dogs. The band starts at 70 cm, or about 27.5 inches, and you trim it from either end to fit. It is useful for night walks, camping, or a dark yard, especially with a black or dark-coated dog. It is a visibility aid, not a substitute for a sturdy everyday collar or a leash.

If you need a collar that also holds tags and takes a strong leash clip, treat this as an extra light to wear with your regular collar or harness, unless the listing's closure works for how you walk your dog.

## How to use it

Measure your dog's neck, then trim the collar a little at a time from the end the manual indicates, leaving enough length to close it securely. Charge it fully before the first night walk. Choose a steady glow if flashing lights bother you, or a flash if you want more attention from cars. Check the closure before you step outside, because a loose end can slip off.

This is a visibility layer. Keep your dog on a leash where you normally would, and do not rely on the collar alone near roads. Recharge after evening use so it is ready for the next walk.
