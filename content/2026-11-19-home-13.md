## How to use it

Charge the scrubber fully before the first use. Choose the head for the job: the flat brush for big areas like a tub floor or tile wall, the corner brush for grout lines and around faucets, and the sponge for glass.

Spray your usual bathroom cleaner on the surface and let it sit for the time on its label. Then turn on the scrubber, start on the low speed, and move slowly so the spinning brush can work. Switch to high for stubborn buildup. Attach the extension rod when you need to reach the top of the shower walls or the floor without crouching.

When you finish, rinse the head and the brush under the tap, let them dry, and recharge. Keep in mind it is waterproof for rinsing, not for use underwater, so don't dunk it in a full tub.
