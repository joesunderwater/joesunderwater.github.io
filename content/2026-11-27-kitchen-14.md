## How to use it

1. **Rinse and dry the herbs,** then gather them into a small bunch.
2. **Snip over a bowl or board.** Each cut makes five slices at once, so a few snips go a long way.
3. **Clean the blades with the comb.** Run the cover/comb between the blades to push out trapped leaves.
4. **Wash, dry, and put the cover back on** before storing it in a drawer.

A few practical ideas:

- **Finish dishes at the table.** Snip chives over baked potatoes or basil over pasta right before serving.
- **Make quick herb butters and dressings.** Fine, even cuts mix in evenly.
- **Prep salad greens.** The listing calls out salad use, so try it on tender lettuces and leafy herbs.
- **Keep the cover on in the drawer** so the blades stay protected and fingers stay safe.
