## How to use it

Bio-Oil's directions are simple: massage it in a circular motion on the body or face until fully absorbed. The brand recommends applying it twice daily for a minimum of three months. A few drops go a long way, so start small and add more only if you need it.

Many people fit it in right after a shower in the evening and again in the morning. Use it only on skin that has fully healed: the brand says it is safe on a scar as soon as the surface skin has healed, but it should not be applied to an open wound or broken skin. It is for external use only, so avoid contact with your eyes. If you have questions about a specific scar or skin condition, check with your doctor or dermatologist.
