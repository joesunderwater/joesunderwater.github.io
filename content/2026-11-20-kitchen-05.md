## How to use it

1. Put greens, herbs, or berries in the basket and rinse them under the tap. The basket works as a colander.
2. Set the basket in the bowl and put the lid on.
3. Press the pump knob a few times to spin the water off.
4. Press the brake button to stop the basket, then lift it out.

Ideas for using it day to day:

- **Side salads for one or two.** Wash and dry a serving of lettuce, then toss it right in the clear bowl and bring it to the table.
- **Herb prep.** Dry herbs before chopping so they don't turn mushy on the cutting board.
- **Berries.** Rinse and gently spin berries for snacks or breakfast.
- **Meal prep.** Dry greens before packing them so lunches stay crisp.

When you're done, lock the pump flat and stack it in a cabinet.
