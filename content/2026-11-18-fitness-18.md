## How to use it

Set the stepper on a flat, stable floor with some clear space around it. Start with a lower step height using the adjustment knob. Step at an easy, steady rhythm for a few minutes to warm up, and keep your weight centered over the pedals.

Once the rhythm feels comfortable, raise the step height for deeper steps, or add the resistance bands. Try bicep curls, shoulder presses, lateral raises, or rows while you keep stepping. Shorten the bands for more tension or lengthen them for less. Watch the LCD monitor for time, steps, and steps per minute, and try to beat your last session.

Short sessions spread through the day add up, too. When you are finished, move it out of the way or tuck it into a closet.
