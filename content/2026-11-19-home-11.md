## How to use it

For carpet and rugs, the brand's tip is to use short, sharp strokes while pulling the broom toward you. That motion brings embedded hair up to the surface of the carpet, where it gathers into a clump you can pick up by hand or with a vacuum. Work in small sections rather than long sweeping passes.

On hard floors, sweep the way you would with any broom. The rubber head collects fine dust and hair. Then flip to the squeegee edge for spills, wet tile, or a quick pass on a glass shower door.

When you are done, rinse the rubber head under the tap or with a hose and let it dry. Set the handle length before each job so you are not hunched over.

A good routine for pet homes is a quick pass over the dog's favorite rug once or twice a week, followed by your usual vacuuming. The broom lifts the hair, and the vacuum picks up what's left.
