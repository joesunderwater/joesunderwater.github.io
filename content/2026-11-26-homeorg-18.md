## How to use it

1. **Empty the shelf and sort.** Group items into categories such as breakfast, snacks, baking, cans, and drinks.
2. **One category per bin.** Load each bin with a single category so you always know where things go.
3. **Put the most-used bins at eye level.** Save higher or lower shelves for backups and bulk items.
4. **Label the fronts.** A simple label on each bin keeps the system going when other people put groceries away.

In the fridge, use a bin for yogurt cups, condiment bottles, or snack packs. In the bathroom, they can hold extra toiletries under the sink. In closets, they work for socks, scarves, or kids' small toys.
