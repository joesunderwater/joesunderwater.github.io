## Who it's for

This drone is aimed at beginners and travelers who want aerial 4K video without a large, heavy aircraft. DJI highlights one-tap takeoff and landing, GPS Return to Home, stable hovering, and in-app learning resources. It is also a fit if you want a compact drone for landscape, travel, or family footage and prefer automated QuickShots rather than flying every move by hand.

Weight and local rules matter here. DJI lists a standard aircraft weight of 246 g, including the Intelligent Flight Battery, propellers, and a microSD card, and markets the Mini 4K as under 249 g. Registration and Remote ID rules vary by country and by how you use the drone. Check current FAA or local regulations before you fly; do not treat the weight claim as a substitute for that research.

## Key features

Details below come from DJI’s official Mini 4K materials and user manual:

- Foldable design with a standard aircraft weight of 246 g (under 249 g). Folded size is 138 x 81 x 58 mm.
- 4K UHD video and 12 MP stills from a 1/2.3-inch camera on a fully stabilized 3-axis gimbal. DJI also lists 4x digital zoom and 4K/30fps video.
- Maximum flight time of 31 minutes, measured while flying forward at 17 kph in windless test conditions. Real-world time is shorter in wind, at higher speeds, or in colder weather.
- Up to 10 km video transmission under stated FCC test conditions. That figure is a transmission range, not a recommended flight distance.
- Level 5 wind resistance, with a maximum wind-speed resistance of 10.7 m/s. Maximum service ceiling is 4,000 m (13,123 ft) with the Intelligent Flight Battery.
- Beginner-friendly tools include one-tap takeoff and landing, GPS Return to Home, and stable hovering, plus QuickShots modes: Helix, Dronie, Rocket, Circle, and Boomerang.
- Downward vision and infrared sensing systems help with hovering indoors and outdoors and with automatic Return to Home.
- Typical standard kits include the aircraft, a remote controller, one Intelligent Flight Battery, spare propellers, a gimbal protector, and a USB-C cable. Fly More-style bundles add extra batteries; confirm the exact contents on the listing you are viewing.

## How to use it

Download the DJI Fly app from DJI’s official website. DJI notes that the app has been removed from Google Play, so a phone’s default store may not be the right source. Charge the battery and controller, attach the propellers as the manual shows, and complete any required activation or firmware updates before the first flight.

Start in an open area, away from people and restricted airspace, and use Normal or Cine mode until you are comfortable. One-tap takeoff, hover, and Return to Home are the safety net; practice those before you try Sport mode or longer flights. QuickShots are the fastest way to get a structured clip: pick a subject, choose Helix, Dronie, Rocket, Circle, or Boomerang, and let the aircraft fly the pattern.

Because flight time is measured in calm test conditions, plan shorter sessions in the field and land with battery in reserve. If you want more air time, look at official multi-battery kits rather than stretching a single pack. Always follow local line-of-sight, altitude, and registration rules.
