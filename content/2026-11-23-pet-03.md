## Who it's for

This medium bowl is aimed at small to medium dogs that eat too fast and could use a calmer pace. Outward Hound rates this Notch pattern as a SLOW level feeder, the gentlest of its Slow, Slower, and Slowest options, so it is a starting point rather than the most challenging maze. The listing also notes it can be used by cats.

If your dog is a flat-faced breed or a determined gulper, look at the other Fun Feeder sizes and slow levels on the listing before you decide. The medium bowl holds 2 cups, which is meant to be a full meal for many medium dogs. Larger dogs that eat more than that may want the 4-cup size instead.

## How to use it

Scoop the meal into the maze, spreading kibble or wet food through the ridges rather than piling it in the center. Set the bowl on a level floor so the non-slip base can grip. Let your dog work through the pattern at their own pace. If the SLOW level still feels too easy, the listing points you toward slower maze options in the same line.

When dinner is over, rinse the bowl or put it on the top rack of the dishwasher. Check now and then that food is not packed into the grooves, especially with wet food.
