1. **Slide your passport in** so the photo page is easy to reach at check-in and border control.
2. **Load your cards** into the card slots. Keep just the cards you need for the trip.
3. **Tuck in your boarding pass** once you print it or pick it up at the counter.
4. **Add a little local cash** for taxis, tips, or a coffee after landing.
