## How to use it

1. **Plan by category.** Give each cube a job, such as tops, bottoms, sleepwear, and underwear and socks.
2. **Fold or roll neatly.** Smooth, flat folds help with the anti-wrinkle benefit.
3. **Close, then compress.** Zip the cube shut, then use the second zipper to compress it.
4. **Stack in your carry-on.** The cubes stack neatly, which makes the most of a smaller bag.
5. **Unpack into drawers.** At your hotel, place the cubes straight into drawers or the closet, as the listing suggests.

Styling and packing ideas:

- **Capsule wardrobe.** Pair this neutral set with a capsule of mix-and-match pieces for a long weekend.
- **Work trip split.** Keep dress shirts and slacks in one cube and casual or workout clothes in another.
- **Shoe-free zone.** Keep shoes in a separate bag so the beige fabric stays clean.
- **Closet organizer.** Between trips, the cubes can hold folded scarves, sweaters, or kids' clothes.
