## How to use it

For everyday dusting and polishing, use a cloth dry. For counters, sinks, and stovetops, dampen it with water or spray your usual cleaner on the surface and wipe. Fold the cloth into quarters so you have several clean sides to work with, and flip to a fresh side as each one gets dirty. For glass and stainless steel, a final pass with a dry cloth helps leave a streak-free finish.

The three colors make it easy to set up a simple system of your own. For example, use one color for the kitchen, one for the bathroom, and one for the car. That way the cloth you use on the toilet never ends up on the counter.

Wash the cloths separately before the first use. After that, toss them in the washing machine with like colors. Keeping microfiber away from lint-heavy laundry like towels helps the cloths stay lint-free.
