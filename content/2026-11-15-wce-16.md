## How to use it

On a workday, set notifications so only the apps that matter reach your wrist. That way you can triage messages in a meeting without pulling out your phone. The always-on display and the Jet Black finish keep the watch looking sharp with a suit or a sweater.

For training, use Heart Rate Zones and Pacer during runs, and check training load over the week to see how hard you are pushing. Wear the watch to bed to get your sleep score and Vitals data in the morning. The fast-charge spec is handy here: a short top-up while you shower and get dressed can cover a big part of the day.

If you travel or work out alone, take a few minutes to set up emergency contacts and Check In so the safety features are ready when you need them.
