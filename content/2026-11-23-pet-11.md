## How to use it

Measure your pet's sitting height and width, then compare them with the 14.5- and 12-inch limits. Let a new user explore the backpack at home first. Leave it open with a familiar blanket, toy, or treat inside, and do not force a cat or dog in. Once they will step in calmly, close the mesh, clip any safety tether if your carrier includes one, and try a short walk around the house before an actual outing.

Adjust the shoulder straps and fasten the chest buckle so the pack sits close to your back. Keep trips reasonably short at first, watch for overheating, and never leave a pet unattended in a parked carrier. Afterward, pull out the pad and wipe down the interior.
