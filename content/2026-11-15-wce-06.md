A good speaker is one of those upgrades you notice every single day: background music while you work, a podcast while you cook, a record on a Saturday night. The Sonos Era 100 landed on the White Collar Electronics board because it handles all of that from one compact unit. Sonos builds it around stereo sound from a single speaker, Wi-Fi streaming, Bluetooth, and hands-free voice control, in a size that fits on a shelf, desk, or nightstand.

## Who it's for

The Era 100 is a fit if you want one speaker that sounds good in a home office, bedroom, kitchen, or next to the bar cart without running wires to separate components. Sonos describes the compact design as fitting on a bookshelf, kitchen counter, desk, or nightstand.

It also makes sense if you already own Sonos gear or plan to build out a multi-room setup over time, since it is managed in the Sonos app alongside other Sonos products. Apple users get AirPlay 2 support, and anyone with a phone can pair over Bluetooth with the press of a button. If you have a turntable, the Era 100 can take a wired source as well, with an adapter sold separately.

## Key features

Details below come from Sonos’ official Era 100 page and the Amazon listing for this ASIN (the black model, E10G1US1BLK):

- **Stereo from one speaker:** two angled tweeters handle stereo separation and high frequencies, and one midwoofer covers vocals and bass. Sonos says the midwoofer is 25% larger than the one in the previous generation, and the processor is 47% faster.
- **Three class-D amplifiers** tuned to the speaker’s acoustic design.
- **Trueplay tuning:** the Sonos app measures your room’s acoustics and adjusts the EQ. You can also adjust bass, treble, and loudness yourself.
- **Wi-Fi streaming** (Wi-Fi 6 compatible), plus **Bluetooth** pairing via a dedicated button on the back.
- **Apple AirPlay 2** support.
- **Line-in via USB-C:** connect a turntable or other source with a 3.5 mm cable and the Sonos Line-In Adapter, or connect to your router with the Sonos Combo Adapter. Both adapters are sold separately.
- **Voice control:** works with Sonos Voice Control and Amazon Alexa, using a far-field microphone array. A physical mic switch on the back disconnects power to the microphones.
- **Capacitive touch controls** for play, pause, skip, volume, and grouping, plus humidity resistance for spaces like a bathroom or kitchen.
- Size is roughly 7.2 in tall, 4.7 in wide, and 5.1 in deep, per Sonos.

## How to use it

Setup, according to Sonos, takes a few minutes: plug in the power cable, connect your phone or tablet to Wi-Fi, and open the Sonos app. Run Trueplay once the speaker is in its final spot, since the tuning depends on the room around it. If you move it from the office to the bedroom later, run it again.

For a home office, the Era 100 can sit on a desk or shelf and play focus music over Wi-Fi while your phone stays free for calls. In the kitchen or on a bar cart, voice control lets you change tracks or volume without touching anything. For a vinyl setup, pick up the Line-In Adapter and a 3.5 mm cable to bring a turntable into the system.

Because it works in the Sonos app, you can also group it with other Sonos speakers. Sonos lists stereo pairing as an option if you add a second Era 100 later.

## Quick FAQ

### Does the Sonos Era 100 work over Bluetooth as well as Wi-Fi?

Yes. Sonos lists both Wi-Fi streaming and Bluetooth audio streaming, with a dedicated button on the back for pairing.

### Can I connect a turntable?

Yes, with accessories. The USB-C port accepts a 3.5 mm source through the Sonos Line-In Adapter, which is sold separately. Some turntables may also need a preamp, so check your equipment.

### Which voice assistants does it support?

Sonos says it works with Sonos Voice Control and Amazon Alexa. Availability varies by language and country, and the mic switch on the back turns the microphones off.
