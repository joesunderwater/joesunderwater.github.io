## Who it's for

These plugs are for anyone who wants to control everyday plug-in electronics without a hub or an electrician. They suit:

- People who use Alexa or Google Assistant and want voice control of lamps and fans.
- Anyone who wants lights to turn on at a set time, like at sunset or before they wake up.

## How to use it

Plug a Kasa smart plug into a wall outlet, then open the Kasa app and follow the setup steps to connect it to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. The app walks you through each step, including linking the plug to Alexa or Google Assistant. Plug your lamp, fan, or other device into the smart plug and leave the device's own switch on so the plug can control it.

Name each plug clearly in the app, like "living room lamp" or "bedroom fan," so voice commands are easy to remember. Then set up schedules, countdown timers, or Away Mode for each one. Common uses include a lamp that turns on in the evening, a fan that shuts off after you fall asleep, or holiday lights that run on a timer.

Stay within the plug's 15 amp rating and follow the safety notes in the manual.
