## How to wear it

For home and sleep, wear the set as it comes: relaxed top, drawstring pants, pockets for a phone or remote. The dropped shoulder and ribbed hem are what give the top that easy, slightly slouchy lounge shape.

For travel or errands, the same set is the “airport outfit” idea the listing points to. Keep the pieces together for a finished look, add sneakers or slides, and use the side pockets so you are not also carrying a large bag. If you want the set to feel a little less like pajamas in public, add a structured layer — a shacket, denim jacket, or hoodie — over the crewneck. You can also split the set, as the listing suggests, and wear the pants with a different top.

Print choice changes the mood more than anything else. A solid color reads quieter; tie-dye or floral reads more casual and on-trend. Check the current options on Amazon and look at the product photos in daylight if the print matters to you.
