## Key features

Details below come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

- **1:1 head-to-toe view.** A 150°×150° ultra-wide field of view captures visitors from head to toe and packages tucked against your door.

## How to use it

1. **Choose your power.** Go wire-free on battery, or connect it to existing AC doorbell wiring for continuous power.
2. **Set it up in the Wyze app.** The listing describes a quick setup of about a minute.
3. **Mount it at the door** so the square view covers both faces and the doormat area where packages land.
4. **Add a microSD card** if you want free local storage, or subscribe to Cam Plus for cloud clips.
5. **Pick a chime.** Add a compatible Wyze chime or camera if you want to hear the doorbell inside.
6. **Recharge as needed** by detaching the doorbell and plugging in the included USB-C cable.
