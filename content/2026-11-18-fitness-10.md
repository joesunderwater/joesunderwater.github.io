## How to use it

Set the walking pad on a flat, hard floor under your standing desk, with clear space behind it. Plug it in, step on, and use the remote to start slowly. A comfortable walking speed lets you type, read, or talk on a call without losing your balance.

Try short walking blocks to start, such as 20 to 30 minutes, then build up across the day. Keep an eye on steps and distance on the LED display to track progress toward your goal.

Because the pad is only about 4 inches tall, it can slide under a bed or couch when you need the floor space back. Always stop the belt fully before stepping off.
