## How to use it

Choose the blade insert you need and set it into the base over the container. For chopping, cut larger vegetables into pieces that fit the grid, set them on the blade, and press the lid down. The cut pieces drop into the container. For slicing or shredding, swap in the right insert and use the food holder for small items.

A few practical ideas:

1. **Holiday prep.** Dice onion, celery, and carrot for stuffing or soups in a few presses.
2. **Fresh salsa.** The brand mentions chopping onions, tomatoes, and peppers for salsa.
3. **Coleslaw.** Use a shredding insert to shred a whole cabbage.
4. **Eggs and fries.** The listing names it an egg slicer and French fry cutter, too.

When you're done, use the cleaning fork to clear the grid, then rinse or load the dishwasher-safe parts.
