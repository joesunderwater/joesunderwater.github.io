## How to use it

1. **Measure clearance.** Check that you have more than 4.5 in under the bed frame, and enough width and depth for a 33 x 17 in container.
2. **Sort by category.** Use one container per category, such as winter sweaters, spare sheets, shoes, or kids' toys.
3. **Load and zip.** Fill each container, zip it closed, and slide it under the bed with the window facing out so you can see contents.
4. **Rotate seasonally.** Swap summer and winter clothes between your closet and the containers when the seasons change.

If you have extra containers, they also work on high closet shelves or under a sofa.
