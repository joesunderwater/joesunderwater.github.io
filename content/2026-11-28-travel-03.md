## How to use it

1. **Sort first.** Group clothing by type, by day, or by family member before you start filling cubes.
2. **Fill the right size.** Put bulky items like sweaters and jeans in the larger cubes. Use the smaller cubes for underwear, socks, swimwear, and accessories.
3. **Zip it closed, then compress.** Close the cube, then use the compression zipper to pull it down to a slimmer shape.
4. **Pack flat and tight.** Lay the larger cubes along the bottom of your suitcase, then fit the smaller ones around them.
5. **Unpack straight into drawers.** At the hotel or on the ship, slide each cube into a drawer and you're done.

A few ideas:

- **Color-code the family.** Assign each person a cube or two so nobody digs through someone else's clothes.
- **Formal-night cube.** On a cruise, keep dressier outfits together in one cube.
- **Beach cube.** Swimsuits, cover-ups, and a hat can live together for quick pool runs.
- **Return-trip space.** Compressing clothes can free room for souvenirs. Watch airline weight limits, since compression saves space, not weight.
