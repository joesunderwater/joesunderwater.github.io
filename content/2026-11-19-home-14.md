A humidifier puts moisture back into the room. ## Who it's for

This humidifier is for anyone dealing with dry air at home, especially during the heating season. With a listed effective range of 215 to 505 square feet, it is sized for bedrooms up to larger living spaces. The 6L tank and long runtime suit people who don't want to refill every day.

It is also a fit for people who like smart-home gear. You can control it through the free VeSync app, set schedules, and let auto mode manage the humidity. Levoit says the materials are BPA-free and suitable for babies and adults, which makes it a common pick for nurseries, but follow the manual's placement guidance.

## How to use it

Set the humidifier on a flat, water-safe surface, away from walls and electronics. Lift the lid, pour water into the tank up to the max line, and set the lid back. Levoit says distilled or purified water is preferred.

Turn it on and choose a mode. Auto mode lets the sensor adjust output for you. Sleep mode is meant for quiet overnight use, and you can switch off the display light so it doesn't glow in a dark bedroom. Point the rotating nozzle toward open space rather than at furniture.

For hands-off use, connect it to the VeSync app and set a schedule, such as turning on an hour before bed. Add a few drops of essential oil to the aroma tray if you like a scent.

Clean the tank and base regularly with the included brush, following the manual. If the no-water indicator shows up by mistake, the listing suggests checking that the tank is seated correctly, that water isn't above the max line, and that the float and outlet valve are clean.
