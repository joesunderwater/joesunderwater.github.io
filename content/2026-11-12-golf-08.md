## How to use it

Set the net up on a level surface with enough clearance behind and beside it for mishits. Outdoors on grass or dirt, use the four U-stakes if you want more stability in the wind. Place a hitting mat in front, at a distance that lets the ball reach the net safely, and keep the area behind you and to the sides clear of people, windows, and vehicles.

Hit full shots into the net and let the ball-return feature gather balls toward you when it can. If you add a launch monitor, follow that device’s net-distance guidance rather than crowding the mouth of the net. When practice is over, disassemble the net and pack it in the carrying case.

This is a practice net, not a blackout enclosure. You can later add a separate impact screen if you want projection, but confirm that any screen you buy actually fits this model; GoSports’ ELITE screen is specified for the Elite net, not automatically for every 10 x 7 net.
