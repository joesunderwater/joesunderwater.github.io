## How to use it

Use it as a standard upright for carpets and rugs, guiding it with the swivel steering around chair legs and table edges.

For stairs, sofas, and baseboards, release the Lift-Away pod and carry it with you, attaching the crevice tool for edges and corners or the upholstery tool for fabric surfaces and pet hair on furniture. Shark's listing suggests the crevice tool with the pod for baseboards, ceilings, corners, and narrow spaces.

Empty the dust cup when it reaches the fill line, and rinse the washable filters on the schedule in the manual. Let them dry fully before you put them back. Keeping the filters clean helps the vacuum keep its suction.
