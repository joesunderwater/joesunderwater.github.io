## How to use it

ComboJoy lists these steps on the listing:

1. Fill a glass with your drink and add ice.
2. Place the wood chimney (smoker top) on top of the glass.
3. Add your preferred wood chips to the stainless steel mesh.
4. Turn on the torch and let the smoke infuse your drink for 5 to 10 seconds.
5. Remove the smoker top and enjoy.

Make the ice balls ahead of time with the included molds so they are ready when you pour. Try different wood chip flavors with different spirits to see what you like; a whiskey that tastes great with one chip may pair better with another.

Before the first use, fill the torch with butane. If it does not light after filling, the listing says to adjust the black button on the bottom toward + or - as needed. As with any open flame, use the torch away from anything flammable, keep it away from kids, and let the smoker top cool before you put it away.
