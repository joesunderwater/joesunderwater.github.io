## How to use it

Choose a weight close to the brand's suggested starting point of about 5% of your body weight. Put the vest on and tighten the buckle straps so it sits snugly against your body. A snug fit keeps it from shifting while you move.

Start with your normal walking route and the same pace. Add time or distance before you add more weight. Once that feels comfortable, try it during bodyweight circuits like squats, lunges, step-ups, or push-ups. Keep your phone or keys in the arm pockets. Early-morning and evening walkers get extra visibility from the reflective strips.

After a sweaty session, wipe the vest clean and let it air out before storing it.
