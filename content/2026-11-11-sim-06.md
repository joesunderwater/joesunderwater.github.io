A starter pedal set usually measures how far the brake travels. A load-cell brake measures how hard you press it, which makes it easier to repeat the same braking point from corner to corner. The Thrustmaster T-LCM Pedals are a three-pedal, all-metal set built around that idea, with a load-cell brake rated up to 100 kg, magnetic sensors on the throttle and clutch, and official support for PC, PlayStation, and Xbox.

## Who it's for

These pedals are for sim racers who want a more consistent brake than a basic potentiometer set, and who already have a compatible Thrustmaster wheel or a PC that can take a direct USB pedal connection. Thrustmaster positions the T-LCM as an expert-level pedal set for precise brake modulation, with adjustable springs so you can tune the feel for a desk, a wheel stand, or a cockpit.

They are a fit if you race on PS5, PS4, Xbox, or PC and want one pedal set that can move among those platforms. On consoles, they connect to a compatible Thrustmaster wheel base with the included RJ12 cable, not straight to the console. On PC, a direct USB connection is recommended. A firm mount matters: a 100 kg load-cell brake rewards a planted pedal tray.

## Key features

Details below come from Thrustmaster’s official T-LCM product and support pages:

- Three-pedal set with throttle, brake, and clutch. Pedal heads, arms, and the footrest are described as 100% metal.
- A load-cell force sensor on the brake, rated for up to 220 lb / 100 kg of pressure, so the game sees how hard you press rather than only how far the pedal moves.
- H.E.A.R.T Hall-effect magnetic sensors on the throttle and clutch, specified at up to 16-bit resolution (65,536 values per pedal), with no potentiometers in that sensor path.
- A set of six included springs for adjusting mechanical brake force, so you can match the pedal to a lighter table setup or a more planted cockpit.
- Independently adjustable pedal height, inclination, and spacing. Calibration software can also adjust brake force and dead zones for all three pedals. Thrustmaster notes that the software is licensed by PlayStation and Gran Turismo.
- A large textured footrest and a claimed weight of more than 11 lb / 5 kg (official weight is 5,200 g) for stability. Dimensions are 13 x 15 x 8.7 inches (330 x 380 x 220 mm).
- In the box: the pedal set, an Allen key, a removable USB cable, a removable RJ12 cable, the six-spring set, and a manual.
- Official platform support includes PC, PS4, PS5, and Xbox. Compatible Thrustmaster bases listed by support include the T300, TX, T-GT II, T248, T598, T818, and several earlier models. Always confirm your exact base on Thrustmaster’s current list.

## How to use it

Mount or brace the pedals so the load-cell brake cannot slide, then set spacing and inclination so your right foot can move from throttle to brake without stretching. On PC, connect the USB cable directly to the computer; Thrustmaster recommends that path for full resolution and says the set is plug-and-play, with calibration software as the only required app. On PlayStation or Xbox, leave the USB cable disconnected and use the RJ12 cable into a compatible, firmware-updated Thrustmaster wheel base. If the base’s MODE LED does not flash as the manual describes, the pedals may be seen only as a standard set until the firmware is current.

Start with a moderate brake spring, then use the calibration software to set brake force and dead zones. The point of a load cell is consistency: you should be able to repeat a braking pressure, not fight the pedal on every lap. After any spring or spacing change, recalibrate so the game sees a clean zero and a clean full-pressure input.

## Quick FAQ

### Do these pedals work on PS5, Xbox, and PC?

Yes, with the right connection. On PC, USB direct is recommended. On PlayStation and Xbox, connect the RJ12 cable to a compatible Thrustmaster wheel base and update that base’s firmware. They do not plug straight into a console.

### What is a load-cell brake?

It is a brake that measures applied force, up to 100 kg on this set, rather than only pedal travel. That is what makes braking more repeatable from lap to lap.

### Can I adjust the pedal feel?

Yes. Six springs are included for the brake, the three pedals can be adjusted for height, inclination, and spacing, and the calibration software can set brake force and dead zones.

### Do I need a cockpit?

A firmly mounted or well-braced setup is strongly recommended. The load-cell brake is designed for high pressure, and a loose floor setup can slide before you reach a useful force.
