## How to use it

1. **Check your devices.** Look for "100–240V" on each charger or device label. Most phone, tablet, and laptop chargers are dual voltage, but always confirm.
2. **Select the plug type** for your destination, using the country list above.
3. **Plug the adapter into the wall,** then plug your device's own charger into the universal input or connect cables to the USB ports.
4. **Use the Power Delivery USB-C port** for the device that needs the fastest charge, such as a laptop or a newer phone.

Packing ideas:

- **One-bag charging kit.** Put this adapter, a compact power bank, and your cables in a single electronics organizer.
- **Hotel nightstand.** Charge phones and earbuds for the whole family from one outlet.
- **Multi-country trips.** Switch plug types as you cross borders instead of buying a new adapter at each stop.
