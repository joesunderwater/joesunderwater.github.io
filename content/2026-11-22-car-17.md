Anyone who has grabbed a steering wheel after the car sat in a sunny parking lot knows why windshield shades exist. A good sun shade blocks the sun coming through the biggest piece of glass in the car, which helps keep the dash, wheel, and seats from baking. The EcoNour windshield sun shade is a foldable version that pops open, sits behind the windshield, and folds back into its pouch when you drive off. It is on the Car Accessories board as an easy upgrade for hot-weather parking.

The photo on this page is an illustrative image of the product, not Amazon's photo. Check the Amazon listing for exact details before you order.

## Who it's for

This shade suits commuters who park outside, anyone in a hot or sunny climate, and drivers who want to protect the dashboard from long-term sun exposure. EcoNour says this size fits small sedans, hatchbacks, and mini SUVs. The brand sells the shade in multiple sizes, so measure your windshield and use the size chart on the listing before you order. A shade that is too small leaves gaps, and one that is too big will not sit flat.

## How to use it

- **Measure first.** Check your windshield against the listing's size chart so you get the right fit.
- **Open it inside the car.** Pop the shade open and set it against the inside of the windshield, adjusting the overlap so it covers as much glass as possible.
- **Hold it with the visors.** Flip both sun visors down to press the shade against the glass.
- **Remove before driving.** Always take the shade down before you start driving so your view is clear.
- **Fold and stash.** Fold it back up, slip it into the pouch, and keep it in a door pocket, under a seat, or behind a seat.

It pairs well with side window shades if you also want to cut sun on the back seats, especially with kids or pets riding along.
