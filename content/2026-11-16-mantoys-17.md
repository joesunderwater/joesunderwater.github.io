- **People who like how things work.** You build the V12 engine with moving pistons and a working differential.

## How to use it

Treat it as a weekend project. Pour a coffee, open the bags in order, and follow the printed instructions or the LEGO Builder app's 3D view. The drivetrain steps are the fun part, because you can see how the differential and engine connect before the bodywork covers them.

When the build is done, try the functions. Swing the butterfly doors up, open the hood, lift the engine cover to show the V12, and roll the car to watch the pistons move. Then find it a home. It looks sharp on a desk, a bookshelf, a garage workbench shelf, or in a man cave next to other car memorabilia.

It also works as a shared project. Build it with a kid or a friend who loves cars, and talk through what the differential does as you go.
