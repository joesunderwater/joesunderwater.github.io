## How to use it

Find a clear outdoor area away from people, dogs, and windows. Press the claw down over the ball until it holds, then grip the handle. Extend the launcher behind you and swing it forward in a smooth arc, following through and looking where you want the ball to go, not at the handle. The listing notes a short learning curve of about 10 to 20 throws, and that the launcher tends to throw a bit downward, so aim higher than you might think.

When your dog brings the ball back, scoop it with the claw instead of picking it up. Check the ball for damage, and replace it if it is torn or too worn to grip safely. This is high-energy exercise, so watch for overheating and keep sessions to a sensible length.
