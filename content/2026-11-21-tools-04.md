## How to use it

Oscillating tools take a little practice, but the basics are simple:

- **Pick the blade for the job.** Plunge blades for cutting straight into wood, drywall, or plastic; the semicircle blade for longer straight cuts; the scraper for old caulk, adhesive, or paint; the sanding pad for corners and edges.
- **Swap tool-free.** Flip the locking lever, change the attachment, and lock it back down.
- **Start slow.** Use a lower speed setting to start a cut or when sanding delicate surfaces, then increase speed for tougher material.
- **Use the auxiliary handle.** Two hands give you better control on flush cuts, like trimming door jambs at floor level.
- **Let the tool work.** Light, steady pressure usually cuts cleaner than pushing hard.

Wear eye protection, keep the cord clear of the blade, and unplug the tool before changing accessories.
