## How to use it

Put 2 AA batteries in the shaver. Lay the garment or fabric flat on a table or ironing board so it is smooth and taut, with no wrinkles or folds under the head. Choose a depth setting suited to the fabric, starting with a gentler setting on delicate knits. Turn it on and glide the head lightly over the pilled area in small motions without pressing down hard.

Check your progress as you go and stop once the fuzz is gone. Empty the lint catcher often, since a full catcher makes the shaver less effective. Test a small hidden spot first on anything delicate or loosely knit. On couches and blankets, work in sections and keep the fabric as flat as possible.

When you're done, empty the lint catcher, switch it off, and store it in a drawer or travel bag.
