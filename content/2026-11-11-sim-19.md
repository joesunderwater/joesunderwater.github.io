If your favorite part of Microsoft Flight Simulator is the long haul in an airliner, a joystick never quite feels right. The Thrustmaster TCA Yoke Pack Boeing Edition pairs a full-size yoke with a matching throttle quadrant, and it is officially licensed by both Boeing and Xbox. Thrustmaster describes the yoke as the first with a 1:1-scale yoke wheel inspired by the Boeing 787 Dreamliner, built to replicate how a real Boeing yoke moves.

## Who it's for

This pack is for civil aviation fans, especially anyone who mostly flies airliners. Thrustmaster lists it as "best for civil aviation" at an expert level. It is licensed for Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, and it is also compatible with PC, so it suits Microsoft Flight Simulator players on either platform. Thrustmaster's compatibility list also includes X-Plane 11 and 12 and Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 on PC.

It is a substantial piece of hardware. Thrustmaster lists the yoke at about 11.8 × 15.4 × 15.0 inches (W × D × H) and 14.8 lb, and the quadrant at 5.1 × 7.7 × 5.9 inches and 1.3 lb, so plan for a sturdy desk with room in front of you.

## Key features

Details below come from Thrustmaster's official product page for the TCA Yoke Pack Boeing Edition:

- **Boeing 787-inspired yoke** with a 1:1-scale yoke wheel, officially licensed by Boeing and Xbox.
- **Suspended PENDUL_R mechanism** that recreates the feel of a floor-mounted pendular yoke, with an 8.3-inch (21 cm) range of travel.
- **Two additional axes on the yoke base**, so you can take off without the throttle quadrant if you want.
- **100% metal internal frame** for sturdiness and stability.
- **Adjustable springs:** use 2 to 4 springs at once to set resistance.
- **TCA Quadrant Boeing Edition** with **4 swappable levers**, so the speed brake and flaps levers can go where you prefer.
- **Linear resistance on the thrust levers**, inspired by the real Dreamliner quadrant.
- **Boeing-style autopilot knob** on the quadrant for altitude, airspeed, and heading.
- **H.E.A.R.T magnetic technology** with 16-bit resolution on the main axes of the yoke and quadrant.
- **T.A.R.G.E.T software on PC** (free download) for testing, configuring, and programming Thrustmaster controllers.
- **In the box:** the yoke, the quadrant, a TM Flying Desk Mount, a USB-C to USB-A cable, a tablet or smartphone stand, an Allen key, 4 interchangeable levers, a crossbar, and attachment pieces.

## How to use it

Decide on the spring setup before you mount everything. The spring installation flyer in the box shows how to change the number of springs; start in the middle and adjust once you have a few flights in. Clamp the yoke to your desk with the included desk mount, and place the quadrant within easy reach of your throttle hand.

Arrange the four quadrant levers for the aircraft you fly most, for example the thrust levers in the center with speed brake and flaps on either side, swapping them if you prefer the other hand. Use the included stand to keep a tablet or phone with charts or checklists close by.

On Xbox, connect the yoke and set up controls inside Microsoft Flight Simulator. On PC, you can also download T.A.R.G.E.T from Thrustmaster's support site to build presets. Practice smooth, small yoke inputs; the long pendular travel rewards gentle movements on approach.
