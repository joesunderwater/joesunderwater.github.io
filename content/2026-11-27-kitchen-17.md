## How to use it

1. **Cook the potatoes** until fork-tender, then drain them well.
2. **Load the basket** with chunks of hot potato, up to the 15 oz capacity.
3. **Hold the ricer over a bowl or pot** and squeeze the long handles together to press the potato through the holes.
4. **Repeat** until all the potatoes are riced, then fold in butter, milk, or cream to taste.
5. **Rinse right away** or put it on the top rack of the dishwasher.

A few practical ideas:

- **Rice potatoes while hot.** Pressing them warm is the usual technique for the lightest texture.
- **Try other vegetables.** The listing suggests soft carrots, turnips, and yams.
- **Make baby food.** Press soft-cooked vegetables for a smooth puree.
- **Use for gnocchi or potato cakes,** where an evenly riced base helps the dough come together.
