Most starter racing pedals measure how far a pedal travels. A load-cell brake measures how hard you press it, which is closer to a real car and makes it easier to hit the same braking point lap after lap. The Logitech G PRO Racing Pedals are a three-pedal set built around that idea, with a 100 kg load-cell brake, Hall-effect sensors, and a modular design you can space, invert, or re-spring to taste.

## Who it's for

These pedals are for sim racers who already have a wheel, or who are building a cockpit, and want a more consistent brake than a basic potentiometer pedal. Logitech presents them as a professional-grade upgrade: a load-cell brake for muscle memory, swappable springs and elastomers for feel, and removable modules so you can match pedal spacing to a real car or to your rig.

They are a fit if you are ready to brake by pressure instead of by travel, and if you can mount the set securely. A 100 kg load-cell brake rewards a firm, planted pedal tray; on a slick floor or a light folding chair, the whole set can move before you reach full pressure. Check the Amazon listing and Logitech’s current compatibility notes for the wheel and platform you plan to use, including any Racing Adapter required for your setup.

## Key features

Details below come from Logitech’s official PRO Racing Pedals product and support materials:

- Three-pedal set with throttle, brake, and clutch.
- 100 kg load-cell brake that reads applied force rather than pedal travel. Logitech describes a default force setting around 30 kg, with the full 100 kg range available for full-axis output when you want a firmer, more race-car-like brake.
- Swappable springs for the throttle and clutch, and swappable elastomers for the brake, so you can make the brake feel softer, like a street car, or firmer, like a race car, without a workshop teardown.
- Contactless Hall-effect sensors on the throttle and brake for position sensing and durability.
- Modular, removable pedal units with horizontal spacing adjustment. The modules can also be inverted in a custom cockpit if you want a hanging-pedal layout.
- A Racing Adapter is part of the broader Logitech G racing ecosystem and can be used to adjust brake force without stopping to recable the pedals. Confirm whether that accessory is included or sold separately on the listing you are viewing.
- Official size is about 22.9 x 34.1 x 44.5 cm, with a weight of about 5.02 kg, which is heavy enough to stay planted better than a light plastic pedal set, especially when hard-mounted.

## How to use it

Mount the pedal frame to a cockpit or brace it so the load-cell brake cannot slide, then set the spacing so your right foot can move from throttle to brake without stretching. Start with a moderate brake-force target rather than the full 100 kg. The point of a load cell is consistency: you should be able to repeat a braking pressure, not fight the pedal on every lap.

Swap the brake elastomer if the pedal feels too soft or too hard, and change the throttle or clutch springs if those pedals need more or less resistance. Because the modules come off and slide, you can also match the layout of a particular car, including an inverted mount in a purpose-built rig. After any mechanical change, recalibrate the pedals in your racing software so the game sees a clean zero and a clean full-pressure input.

If you use a Logitech G wheel or the Racing Adapter, set brake force where you can reach 100% input with a firm press, not a stomp that upsets the car. Then spend a session on a familiar track building muscle memory. Check platform compatibility before you buy if you race on a console rather than a PC.

## Quick FAQ

### What is a load-cell brake?

It is a brake that measures how much force you apply, up to 100 kg on this set, rather than how far the pedal travels. That is what makes braking more consistent from lap to lap.

### Are all three pedals included?

Yes. The PRO Racing Pedals include throttle, brake, and clutch, with adjustable spacing and swappable springs or elastomers.

### Do I need a racing cockpit?

A firmly mounted or well-braced setup is strongly recommended. The load-cell brake is designed for high pressure, and a loose floor setup can slide before you reach a useful force.

### Will they work with my wheel or console?

They are designed as a sim racing upgrade and can be used with Logitech G racing hardware, but compatibility depends on your wheel and platform. Check the Amazon listing and Logitech’s current notes, including any required adapter.
