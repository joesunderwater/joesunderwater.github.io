## How to use it

- **Install on the front seats.** Loop the top strap around the headrest and buckle it, then run the bottom strap around the base of the seat back and tighten until the mat sits snugly.
- **Check clearance.** Make sure the mat and straps do not interfere with seat adjustments, seat-back controls, or how your child's car seat is installed. Follow your car seat manufacturer's instructions.
- **Load the pockets.** Tablets, books, small toys, wipes, and snacks fit nicely in the pockets. Keep heavy or hard items out of the pockets so nothing becomes a projectile in a sudden stop.
- **Clean it regularly.** Wipe off mud and spills, or remove the mat and wash it when needed, following any care tag.

They are also handy without kids, for example to keep dog paws or sports gear from scuffing the seat backs.
