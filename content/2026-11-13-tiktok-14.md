## How to use it

The listing's suggested use is simple: apply one to two times a day after cleansing, using clean fingers or a cotton pad, and do not rinse.

A practical routine might look like this:

1. Cleanse and pat skin dry.
2. Wet a cotton pad with the liquid and swipe it over your face, or press it on with clean fingertips.
3. Let it absorb, then continue with serum and moisturizer.
4. In the morning, finish with sunscreen, which is a smart habit any time you use an exfoliant.

If you are new to BHA, many people begin with a few evenings a week and build up from there as their skin adjusts.
