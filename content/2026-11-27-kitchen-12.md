## How to use it

1. **Open the handles** and set the cutting wheel just inside the rim of the can.
2. **Squeeze the handles together** so the wheel pierces the lid.
3. **Turn the oversized knob** to move the wheel around the rim until the lid is cut.
4. **Lift the lid away carefully,** since a freshly cut edge can be sharp.
5. **Use the built-in opener** on the other part of the tool for bottle caps.

A few practical tips:

- **Wash and dry right away.** The care instructions call for hand washing and drying immediately, which helps keep the gears and wheel in good shape.
- **Keep it where you prep.** A dependable opener earns a spot in the top drawer, especially during holiday cooking when cans of broth, pumpkin, and cranberry sauce stack up.
- **Wipe the wheel between cans** if you are opening several in a row, so residue does not build up.
