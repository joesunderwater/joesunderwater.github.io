## Who it's for

This organizer is for families whose shoes pile up by the door, for small apartments and dorms with limited closet space, and for anyone who wants a rack that doesn't take up floor space. UVIAHOMI says it fits sneakers, boots, heels, and kids' shoes, and that it holds up to 20 pairs of men's shoes (up to size 15) or 30 pairs of women's shoes.

It also works for more than shoes. The brand suggests towels, toys, snacks, and hats, in bedrooms, bathrooms, entryways, and dorms. The main fit check is your door: it fits standard doors from 1.37 in to 1.98 in thick.

## How to use it

1. **Measure your door.** Make sure it's between 1.37 and 1.98 inches thick, and check that the door still closes with the hooks over the top.
2. **Hang it.** Place the four metal hooks over the top of the door and let the organizer hang flat.
3. **Load from the bottom up.** Put heavier boots and sneakers in the lower pockets and lighter flats, sandals, or kids' shoes up top.
4. **Sort by person or season.** Give each family member a few rows, or keep everyday pairs at eye level and off-season shoes higher.

On a pantry or bathroom door, the pockets can hold snacks, rolled towels, or toiletries instead.
