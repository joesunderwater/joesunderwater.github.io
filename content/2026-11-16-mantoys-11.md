## Who it's for

The Mini 3 is for people who want aerial photos and video without a big, heavy drone. It suits travelers, outdoor fans, and anyone who wants to film landmarks, coastlines, or family trips from above. If you post to social media, True Vertical Shooting lets you capture tall subjects like waterfalls and skyscrapers in portrait orientation.

Its weight is also a selling point for casual flyers. DJI says that at under 249 g, FAA registration and Remote ID are not required when you fly for recreational purposes in the US. Rules change and vary by country, so check the FAA website and your local regulations before you fly.

## How to use it

Before your first flight, download the DJI Fly app. DJI notes that the Mini 3 must be activated in the app. Charge the battery, insert a microSD card, and check local laws and airspace rules for where you plan to fly.

Start in an open area with no obstacles and get comfortable with basic control before trying long-range shots. When you want cinematic footage without complex stick work, choose a QuickShot flight pattern. Use Panorama for wide landscape scenes. For clips headed to your phone feed, switch to True Vertical Shooting.

If you plan long days out, the listing mentions a Fly More combo with more total flight time. Check which bundle the Amazon listing offers before you buy, and keep in mind that the Battery Plus pushes the drone over 249 g.

## Quick FAQ

### Do I need to register the DJI Mini 3?

DJI says that in the US, FAA registration and Remote ID are not required for recreational flying at under 249 g. Check the FAA website and local rules, since requirements vary.

### What video quality does it shoot?

It films 4K HDR video using Dual Native ISO Fusion. HDR video tops out at 30 fps.

### How long can it fly?

Up to 38 minutes with the standard battery, measured by DJI in windless conditions at a constant speed. The optional Intelligent Flight Battery Plus extends that to up to 51 minutes, but adds weight.

### Do I need an app?

Yes. The drone must be activated in the DJI Fly app before use.
