## Who it's for

CeraVe makes this cream for normal to dry skin on the face and body. The brand says it is gentle enough for the whole family, ages 3 and up, and it can double as a hand cream. It suits people who want one moisturizer instead of separate face, hand, and body products, and anyone who gets dry, rough patches on elbows, knees, and hands in winter.

If you prefer a very light, gel-like texture for your face, this may feel richer than you want. It is a thick cream, though CeraVe describes it as absorbing quickly without a greasy feel.

## How to use it

CeraVe's direction is simply to apply liberally to face and body as often as needed, day or night. In practice, the best time is right after a shower or face wash, while your skin is still a little damp. A small scoop warmed between your palms spreads more easily over arms and legs.

For the face, a pea-sized amount is usually plenty. Smooth it on after cleansing and before sunscreen in the morning, or as your last step at night. Pair it with a gentle cleanser like CeraVe Hydrating Facial Cleanser for a simple two-step routine.

The tub format is handy for hands and feet too. Keep it near the sink and apply a little after washing your hands. Use a clean spoon or spatula if several people share the tub, and keep the lid closed between uses. Avoid direct contact with eyes, as the listing directs. If you have sensitive skin, patch test first.
