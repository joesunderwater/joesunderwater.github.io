Not every automatic feeder needs a clock or an app. Sometimes you just want a bowl of dry food and a bowl of water that refill themselves as they empty. This Amazon Basics set is on the Pet Gadgets board as a simple two-piece gravity station for cats and dogs: a food hopper and a water bottle, both with clear sides so you can see when it is time to fill them again.

The photo on this page is an illustrative image of the product, not Amazon's photo. Check the Amazon listing for exact color, size, and details before you order.

## Who it's for

The set is aimed at cat or dog households that want a steady supply of dry food and water without programming meals. It works in single-pet homes and in multi-pet homes that share one station. Because it is the small size, it is a better fit for cats and small or medium dogs than for a large dog that empties a hopper in a sitting.

It is a gravity system, not a timed feeder, so food is available whenever the bowl is low. If your pet needs strictly scheduled portions, a programmable feeder is a better match. This one is for free-feeding dry food and keeping water topped up.

## How to use it

To fill the feeder, unscrew the top lid and pour dry food into the wide-mouth hopper. Food flows into the bowl as your pet eats. To fill the waterer, tip the bottle upside down, pour water in through the wide mouth, attach the base, then flip the unit upright. The listing describes a connector that helps keep water from leaking until the bottle is securely on the base.

Set both pieces on a level floor where the rubber feet can grip, and leave enough room for your pet to stand comfortably. Check the clear sides during the day so you refill before either hopper runs dry. Wash the parts by hand on a regular schedule; they are not listed as dishwasher-safe.

## Quick FAQ

### Is this a timed automatic feeder?

No. It is a gravity set. Food and water refill the bowls as the levels drop, with no programming and no batteries.

### How much does each piece hold?

The listing gives 152 fl oz for the food feeder and 135.3 fl oz (about 1 gallon) for the water dispenser. Confirm current capacity and size options on the Amazon listing.

### Can I use wet food in the feeder?

The set is described for dry food. Wet food is not a good match for a gravity hopper.
