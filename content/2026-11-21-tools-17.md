## How to use it

For wood and metal, leave hammer mode off and pick the speed that fits the job. Low speed gives more control for driving screws into deck boards, fence pickets, or cabinet hardware. High speed is better for drilling clean holes with twist or spade bits.

For brick, block, or concrete, use a masonry bit, switch to hammer mode, and let the drill do the work. Press steadily and pull the bit out now and then to clear dust. Wear eye protection, and use a dust mask when drilling masonry.

The 1/2 inch ratcheting chuck takes a wide range of bit shanks, so you can use it for larger hole saws and spade bits as well as small pilot bits. Keep one battery on the charger while the other is in the drill, and you can work through bigger projects without long breaks.

Since a clip isn't included, consider a VERSATRACK wall setup or a pegboard hook to store the drill and batteries together.
