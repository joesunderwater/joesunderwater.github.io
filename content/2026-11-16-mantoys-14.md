LEGO labels this set for ages 10 and up and markets it as a hypercar building toy. ## How to use it

Open the bags in order and follow the instructions step by step. The LEGO Builder app makes it easier to see tricky steps because you can zoom in and rotate the model in 3D. As you go, you will assemble the moving V8 engine and the differential, which is a good look at how a real drivetrain works.

When the car is done, use the detachable knob at the front to try the steering, then open the doors to see the synchro-helix mechanism rotate and swing out. At about 11 inches long, it fits easily on a desk, bookshelf, or office credenza.

Building it with a kid? Let them handle the steps they enjoy and take turns. LEGO describes Technic sets as an introduction to engineering concepts.
