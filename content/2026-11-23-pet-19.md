The first nights in a new home are hard on a puppy that just left littermates. The original Snuggle Puppy is on the Pet Gadgets board as a comfort plush with two simple features: a pulsing heartbeat and an optional heat pack, tucked into a soft biscuit-colored toy. It is a crate-training and settling aid for medium breeds, and many people also bring it out during storms, fireworks, or time alone.

## Who it's for

This medium Snuggle Puppy is sized for dogs that will mature to about 25 to 70 pounds. The brand also makes smaller and larger plushes for dogs under 25 pounds or over 70 pounds, so pick the size that matches the adult weight you expect. It is meant for puppies transitioning home, crate training, and dogs of any age that settle better with a warm, heartbeat-style companion.

It is a comfort toy, not a chew toy. The brand recommends supervising early use, especially with a heavy chewer, and removing it if your dog starts to shred the plush.

## Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing and Snuggle Puppy's product information for this ASIN:

- **Medium size, biscuit color.** About 8 inches wide by 12.25 inches long, for dogs that grow to about 25 to 70 pounds.
- **Motion-activated SmartBeat heart** with a pulsing Real-Feel heartbeat. Batteries are included. Standard mode runs about 8 hours and restarts when it detects motion. Advanced mode runs continuously while it senses motion, pauses after about 30 minutes of stillness, and starts again when moved. Hold the button to turn it off.
- **One disposable overnight heat pack** in the box. It is air-activated. The brand recommends using only genuine Snuggle Puppy heat packs, not ordinary hand warmers.
- **Machine-washable plush,** on a gentle cycle with mild detergent, air dried. Always remove the heart and the heat pack before washing.
- **What's in the box.** The medium plush, the SmartBeat heart with batteries, and one disposable heat pack.

## How to use it

Remove the plastic battery tab from the heart, choose Standard or Advanced mode, and slide the heart into the belly pouch. Activate the heat pack according to the instructions and add it to the pouch if you want warmth for that first night. Place the Snuggle Puppy in the crate or bed, not as a substitute for a safe sleep setup.

Take the heart and heat pack out before any wash, then air-dry the plush. Replace heat packs as needed, and check the toy for damage. If your dog is a chewer, use the Snuggle Puppy only when you can watch, or when it is in a crate where it will not be shredded.
