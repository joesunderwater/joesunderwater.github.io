The Fujifilm Instax Mini EVO combines a digital camera and an instant printer in a retro-style body. Instead of printing every shot automatically, it lets you preview your photos on a screen and print only the ones you want on Instax Mini film. It also works as a printer for photos from your phone through the free INSTAX MINI EVO app. That two-in-one trick has made it a popular pick for parties, weddings, travel, and scrapbooking.

## Who it's for

The Mini EVO suits anyone who loves the look of instant prints but doesn't want to waste film on missed shots: scrapbookers, journalers, party hosts, and travelers. It's also a good fit for people who mostly shoot on their phones but want physical prints, since it can print from a smartphone over Bluetooth. Keep in mind that Instax Mini film and a microSD card are sold separately, according to the listing.

## Key features

- **Hybrid instant camera.** Take a digital photo, check it on the 3.0-inch LCD, then pull the print lever to print it as a 2" x 3" Instax Mini photo.
- **100 looks.** Ten lens effects and ten film effects, combined with the lens and film dials, give 100 ways to shoot.
- **Selfie-friendly.** A built-in selfie mirror and dual shutter buttons for portrait and landscape shooting.
- **Phone printing and remote shooting.** Fujifilm says the free INSTAX MINI EVO app lets you print photos from your smartphone over Bluetooth, use your phone as a remote shutter, and save digital copies of your prints.
- **Storage.** Fujifilm lists internal memory for about 45 images, plus a microSD/microSDHC slot (card not included).
- **Print modes.** Fujifilm offers instax-Rich Mode for a bright look and instax-Natural Mode for a cooler, classic look.
- **Camera controls.** Fujifilm's spec sheet lists autofocus, built-in flash, a self-timer, exposure compensation, and white balance settings.
- **Rechargeable battery.** A built-in lithium-ion battery that Fujifilm rates at about 100 prints per full charge.

## How to use it

Charge the camera, load a pack of Instax Mini film, and insert a microSD card if you want to keep more than the internal memory holds. Turn the lens and film dials to pick a look, frame the shot with the screen or the selfie mirror, and press the shutter button that fits how you're holding the camera. When you get a shot you love, pull the print lever. Fujifilm says prints develop in about 90 seconds.

At a party or wedding, set it on a table for guests to grab and print a few favorites for a guest book. Before a trip, install the app so you can print your best phone photos too, and use the remote shutter for group shots. Prints make an easy base for scrapbooks, journals, and fridge photo walls.
