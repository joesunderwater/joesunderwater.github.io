## How to use it

Hold the zester at an angle with the end tabs resting on a cutting board, or hold it over a bowl or plate. Draw the food across the blade in one direction, using light pressure.

Some ideas based on the listing's suggested uses:

- **Citrus.** Zest lemons, limes, or oranges for vinaigrettes, stir fries, salads, and baked goods. Turn the fruit as you go so you take only the colored rind.
- **Cheese.** Grate Parmesan over pasta, eggs, or salads right at the table.
- **Garlic and ginger.** Grate cloves or peeled ginger straight into a pan or marinade.
- **Spices and chocolate.** Grate whole nutmeg or cinnamon, or shave chocolate over desserts and drinks.

Microplane also mentions a few off-label tricks, like shaving a cork to fit back in a bottle or scraping dark spots off toast and cookies.

To clean, rinse right after use. For bits stuck in the teeth, use a kitchen brush on the underside of the blade, or put it in the dishwasher. Store it on a magnetic strip or in a drawer, and keep the sharp blade away from fingers.
