## How to use it

1. **Update the eufy app** on your phone first, as the listing recommends.
2. **Set up the HomeBase Mini indoors** and connect it to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
3. **Add each camera** in the app.
4. **Place the cameras.** Snap them onto a metal surface or set them on a flat ledge, shelf, or window sill. Good starting spots are the front door, driveway, back door, and a main living area.
5. **Set activity zones and detection types** so you get alerts for what matters, like people and vehicles.
6. **Turn on Facial Recognition** if you want alerts that tell familiar faces from strangers.
