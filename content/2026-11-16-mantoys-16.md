Some cars are just cars. The Toyota Supra MK4 from the first Fast and Furious movie is a piece of pop culture. The LEGO Technic Fast and Furious Toyota Supra MK4 (set 42204) lets you build a model of that street racer, and it landed on the Man Toys board as a fun, nostalgic build for car guys and movie fans. ## Who it's for

LEGO and Amazon list this set for builders ages 9 and up. That makes it an approachable Technic build, not a marathon project. It is a good fit for:

- **Fast and Furious fans** who want a model of one of the franchise's best-known cars.
- **Car guys** who like seeing how an engine, steering, and body panels go together.
- **Families and friends** who want to build together. LEGO pitches this set as a way to share the movies while you build.
- **Gift shoppers** looking for a husband, dad, or teen gift that ends up on display instead of in a drawer.

If you want a long, highly detailed adult build, this one is on the lighter side. It is better suited to a relaxed evening or weekend build.

## Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing and the official LEGO product page for set 42204:

- **810 pieces** in the set.
- **6-cylinder engine** you assemble and can see under the opening hood.
- **Working steering**, controlled by turning a knob on top of the car.
- **Opening hood** and **removable Targa top roof**.
- **Opening doors** and **2 NOS bottles in the trunk**, a nod to scenes from the first Fast and Furious movie.
- **Realistic movement and mechanisms**, part of the LEGO Technic approach to introducing engineering ideas.
- **LEGO Builder app support**, where you can zoom in, rotate models in 3D, save sets, and track your progress.
- **Finished size:** over 3 in. (8 cm) high, 11.5 in. (30 cm) long, and 5 in. (13 cm) wide.

## How to use it

Set aside an evening or a lazy weekend afternoon, clear a table, and sort the bags as you go. Printed instructions are included, and the LEGO Builder app adds a 3D view you can zoom and rotate if a step is hard to read on paper.

Once it is built, play with the functions. Turn the knob on top to steer, pop the hood to show off the 6-cylinder engine, take off the Targa top, and open up the car to find the NOS bottles. Then give it a spot where people will see it. It works well on a home office desk, a garage shelf, a bookcase in the den, or next to the TV for movie night.

As a gift, it pairs well with a Fast and Furious movie night. Build it together, then put on the first film and point out the details.
