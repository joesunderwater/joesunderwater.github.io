## Who it's for

The DJI Mic 2 is aimed at creators who record interviews, vlogs, podcasts, livestreams, and talking-head videos. The two-transmitter setup makes sense if you regularly record with a co-host or guest, or if you want stereo sound.

It is also a fit if you shoot on a phone. The kit includes both USB-C and Lightning phone adapters, plus a 3.5mm camera audio cable for cameras. And if you already use DJI cameras, a transmitter can connect directly to several of them without the receiver.

## How to use it

Charge everything in the case first. When you are ready to record, take out the transmitters and receiver — they come pre-linked, so they should connect right away.

For phone video, plug the receiver into your phone with the USB-C or Lightning adapter that matches your device. For a camera, use the included 3.5mm TRS cable. Clip a transmitter to each speaker's shirt, or use the magnetic clips, and add the windscreens if you are recording outdoors.

Turn on internal recording on the transmitters for important shoots. That gives you a backup on each mic, and the 32-bit float recording means a sudden laugh or shout is less likely to ruin the take. Use the headphone out on the receiver to monitor audio while you record.

If you shoot with a compatible DJI Osmo camera, connect a transmitter directly through OsmoAudio and leave the receiver in the case.
