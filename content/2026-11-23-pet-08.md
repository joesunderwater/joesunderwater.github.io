## How to use it

Charge the clipper before you start. Brush the coat out first so the clipper is not chewing through knots. Snap on a guide comb, hold the skin taut, and clip in the direction of the coat, moving slowly around legs, ears, and skin folds. For a long, thick coat, trim the bulk with the scissors first, as the listing suggests.

When a pass is done, pop the cutter head off and rinse it, then oil the blade as the manual directs. Do not soak or rinse the motor housing. Store the combs and scissors with the clipper so the next trim is ready to go.
