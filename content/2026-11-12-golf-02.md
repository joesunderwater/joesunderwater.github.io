## Who it's for

The MLM2PRO suits golfers who want a compact launch monitor for a home simulator bay and for outdoor range work, and who care about measured spin and video as much as carry distance. Rapsodo designed it for use indoors and outdoors, paired with a compatible Apple or Android phone or tablet.

It is also a fit if you want simulation and structured practice in one device. Course play, virtual ranges, and some advanced metrics are tied to Rapsodo membership features, so this is a better match for someone comfortable using the MLM2PRO app and checking what the current plan includes.

## Key features

Details below come from Rapsodo’s official MLM2PRO product pages and user manual:

- Mobile launch monitor and golf simulator that combines dual optical cameras with Doppler radar.
- Impact Vision: an integrated high-speed camera that captures 240 frames per second for a slow-motion look at club path and contact.
- Shot Vision: a wide-angle 2K video camera that records swing video with a shot tracer for ball-flight visualization.
- 15 metrics in total, with eight listed as measured, including ball speed, club speed, launch angle, launch direction, angle of attack, club path, spin rate, and spin axis. Other listed data includes smash factor, carry and total distance, descent angle, side carry, apex, and shot type.

- Simulation on more than 30,000 Rapsodo courses, plus virtual range experiences, including a Target Range mode with many target distances. Course play and some training features require a membership; check the current listing and app for what is included at purchase and what renews later.
- R-Speed non-impact swing-speed training and Rapsodo Combine practice, plus R-Cloud session history and video tools as offered with membership features.
- Full indoor and outdoor use. Apple and Android compatible.
- Built-in rechargeable lithium-ion battery; the manual lists a battery life of about 2 to 4 hours. USB-C charging, a tripod connection, and an alignment aid are on the device. Confirm the current in-box list on Amazon.

## How to use it

Mount the unit on the included tripod, use the alignment aid to set it on your target line, and place it at the distance Rapsodo specifies for indoor or outdoor shots. Connect it to the MLM2PRO app on a compatible phone or tablet, then choose a range session, a combine-style practice, or, with the appropriate membership, a simulated course.

For measured spin indoors, use RPT-marked balls rather than a random range ball, and give yourself enough ball-to-screen or ball-to-net space for the radar and cameras to see the shot. Review Impact Vision and Shot Vision after swings that need a closer look at contact or start line. R-Speed can be used for swing-speed work that does not require hitting a ball.

If you already use simulation software, Rapsodo lists compatibility with programs such as GSPro, E6 Connect, E6 Apex, and Awesome Golf. Confirm current compatibility and any extra licenses before you buy.
