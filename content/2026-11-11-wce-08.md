Coffee has a short window where it tastes the way you wanted it. Leave a mug on a desk through a meeting, and it cools past that window. The Ember Mug 2 is a temperature-control mug designed to hold a drink at a set point, either on its charging coaster all day or on battery for a shorter stretch away from the desk.

## Who it's for

This mug is for people who drink coffee or tea slowly at a desk, in a long meeting, or while working from home, and want the cup to stay at a chosen temperature instead of cooling in 15 minutes. The 10 oz size is the smaller Ember Mug 2; a 14 oz version also exists with a slightly different battery rating, so confirm you are looking at the 10 oz white model.

It is also a fit if you like using a phone app to set an exact temperature, or if you want a mug you can rinse more freely than a typical electronic cup. Ember rates the Mug 2 IPX7, meaning it is designed to be submersible up to 1 meter, which makes hand-washing easier. It is not a travel tumbler with a lid for a commute, and it should not go in a microwave.

## Key features

Details below come from Ember’s official support materials for the Ember Mug 2:

- 10 oz (about 295 ml) temperature-control mug. This listing is the white model.
- App-controlled temperature range of 120°F to 145°F (50°C to 62.5°C). If you use it without pairing a phone, it maintains a default of 135°F.
- Built-in battery life of up to 90 minutes off the charging coaster for the 10 oz mug, measured at a 135°F target. Ember notes that pour temperature, ambient temperature, and additions such as milk can change that time. The 14 oz Mug 2 is rated up to 80 minutes, which is a different model.
- On the charging coaster, the mug can maintain the set temperature all day while also charging.
- A full charge takes about two to three hours on the coaster. Ember says a charged mug is safe to leave on the coaster; charging stops automatically when the battery is full.
- IPX7 rating, described as fully submersible up to 1 meter, for easier cleaning. The Mug 2 is not dishwasher safe; Ember reserves dishwasher use for later models such as Mug 3. Do not put it in a microwave.
- Construction is durable stainless steel with an FDA food-grade, ceramic-reinforced coating.
- Ember charging coasters are interchangeable across Ember mugs that use the same charging system.

## How to use it

Pour in a drink that is already hotter than your target temperature. Ember designs the mug to bring a hot beverage down to a set point and hold it there, not to heat a cold or room-temperature drink up to coffee temperature. Pair the Ember app if you want a temperature other than the 135°F default, then set a value between 120°F and 145°F.

Leave the mug on the charging coaster during desk work if you want all-day temperature control. Lift it off when you walk to a meeting; the 10 oz battery is rated for up to 90 minutes at 135°F, which is enough for a shorter stretch away from the desk. When you are done, hand-wash it. The IPX7 rating is there so you can clean it more confidently, but it still should not go in the dishwasher or the microwave.

If the mug reads as empty or stops heating, Ember’s usual advice is to pour in liquid hotter than the set temperature so the sensors can detect a real drink. Charge it on the coaster until the indicator shows a full charge if you plan to use it off the base.

## Quick FAQ

### Does it heat cold drinks from room temperature?

No. Ember designed it to bring an already hot beverage to a set temperature and hold it there. Start with freshly brewed coffee or hot tea.

### How long does it stay warm away from the coaster?

The 10 oz Mug 2 is rated for up to 90 minutes off the coaster at a 135°F target. On the coaster, it can hold the set temperature all day.

### Can I use it without the app?

Yes. Unpaired, it maintains a default temperature of 135°F. The app is how you choose a different set point between 120°F and 145°F.

### Is it dishwasher or microwave safe?

No. The Mug 2 should be hand-washed and must not go in a microwave. Ember lists later models, not this one, as dishwasher safe.
