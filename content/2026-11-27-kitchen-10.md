## How to use it

Pick the size you need. For dry ingredients, dip the oval end into the jar or container and sweep the leveler across the top for an exact amount. For liquids like vanilla or oil, use the round end. When you're done, snap the spoons back together so the set stays in one place.

A few practical ideas:

1. **Measure straight from the spice jar.** No need to pour spices into your palm first.
2. **Level your baking soda.** Use the leveler for consistent baking results.
3. **Use both ends in one recipe.** Measure a dry spice with one end and a liquid with the other, since each end holds the same amount.
4. **Store them together.** Keep the stack in a drawer or stick it to a stainless steel surface.
