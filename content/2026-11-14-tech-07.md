## Key features

- **25W Qi2.2 wireless charging.** Anker says an iPhone 17 Pro can reach 50% in about 22 minutes, and notes that actual performance may vary.
- **TEC active cooling.** According to the listing, the cooling system keeps the iPhone surface below 89°F at peak output. Anker's ActiveShield 5.0 runs ongoing safety checks.
- **Live smart display.** A 1.65" HD touchscreen shows real-time wattage, temperature, and charging status for each device, plus onscreen safety alerts.
- **Anker app control.** Pair over Bluetooth or NFC to see charging history and adjust screensavers, brightness, charging modes, and firmware updates.
- **Three charging modes.** Boost Mode for quick top-ups, Ice Mode for cooler charging while you use the phone, and Sleep Mode, which turns off the cooling and dims the display for quiet overnight charging.
- **Adjustable stand.** The phone stand adjusts up to 80° for hands-free video calls or streaming.
- **Build and extras.** Stainless steel arms and a weighted base. A 65W USB-C adapter and a 5 ft USB-C to USB-C cable come in the box. The listing mentions a 24-month warranty.

## How to use it

Plug the included 65W adapter into the wall, connect the cable to the station, and snap your iPhone onto the magnetic pad. Then set your Apple Watch on its charger and drop your AirPods case on the earbud spot. Anker recommends a 65W or higher USB-C adapter for full 25W charging and proper cooling, so stick with the adapter in the box.

On a nightstand, switch to Sleep Mode so the fan stays off and the screen dims. At a desk, tilt the phone stand up for video calls and use Ice Mode if you'll be using the phone while it charges. Boost Mode is the one to pick when you need a fast top-up before heading out.
