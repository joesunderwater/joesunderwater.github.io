## Key features

One important note: this is **not a voltage converter**. EPICKA says it may not be suitable for high-power appliances like hair dryers, curling irons, and straighteners.

## How to use it

Before you leave, look up the plug type used at your destination. When you arrive, slide out the matching plug, lock it in place, and plug the adapter into the wall. Then connect your devices to the USB-C and USB-A ports, or plug a device charger into the AC socket.

Some packing ideas:

- Bring one adapter instead of a separate charger for every device.
- Keep your phone, earbuds, and watch charging overnight from one outlet.
- Pack the spare fuse somewhere you'll remember, in case you ever need it.
- Leave high-power hot tools at home, or bring dual-voltage versions with the right plug.

Since there's no storage bag in the box, a small pouch or packing cube keeps it together with your cables.
