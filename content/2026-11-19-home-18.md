## How to use it

Open the caddy and click a fresh scrubbing pad onto the end of the wand. Following the directions on the package, scrub the bowl, under the rim, and around the waterline where stains tend to form.

When you are done, hold the wand over the trash and pop the pad off. Store the wand back in the caddy. That's the whole routine, with no brush to rinse or drip-dry.

Keep the caddy in the bathroom so it's easy to do a quick clean once or twice a week. When you run low, the listing area shows refill packs, so check there for the current options and scents. Always follow the safety directions on the package, keep refills away from children and pets, and don't mix with other cleaners.
