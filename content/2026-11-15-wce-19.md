## Who it's for

The MV7i is aimed at podcasters, streamers, and musicians. It also suits anyone who spends a lot of the day on video calls and wants to sound better. Shure says it works over USB-C with a Mac, PC, or phone and has an all-metal design. It is also certified for Open Broadcaster Software (OBS) for people who stream.

It is especially useful if you do not have a treated room. Shure's built-in noise reduction and automatic level control are designed for ordinary offices and spare bedrooms. The second input makes it a good fit if you want to interview a guest or record an instrument alongside your voice.

## How to use it

For a home-office setup, plug the MV7i into your computer over USB-C and select it as the microphone in your meeting or recording app. Turn on Auto Level Mode so you do not have to adjust gain when you lean back or speak up. Tap-to-mute on the LED panel is a quick, visible way to silence yourself when someone walks in.

If your office has hard walls or HVAC noise, try the Voice Isolation and denoise features in the MOTIV Mix app. While you are there, set the LED color to match your desk setup. For a podcast with a guest, connect a second microphone to the XLR/¼-inch combo input and record both voices through the MV7i. Musicians can use the same input for an instrument.

For placement and setup, follow Shure's own guidance for the MV7i.

## Quick FAQ

### Do I need a separate audio interface?

No. Shure says the MV7i has a built-in interface and connects over USB-C to a Mac, PC, or phone.

### Can I record two sources at once?

Yes. The XLR/¼-inch combo input accepts a second microphone or an instrument for dual-channel recording.

### Will it help in a noisy room?

Shure lists Voice Isolation Tech and real-time denoise processing to help cut room noise, echo, and distractions. Results depend on your space.

### Can I mute it quickly?

Yes. The LED touch panel supports tap-to-mute.
