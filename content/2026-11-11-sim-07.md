Paddle shifters are fast, but they cannot replace the feel of moving a stick through a gate. The Thrustmaster TH8A is a gearbox add-on built for that job: a metal shifter that switches between a 7+1 H-pattern plate and a sequential plus-or-minus plate, with adjustable resistance and official support on PC plus PlayStation and Xbox when it is paired with a compatible Thrustmaster wheel.

## Who it's for

The TH8A is for sim racers who want to shift for themselves in road cars, rally, drift, trucks, or classic-car titles, rather than relying only on wheel-mounted paddles. The two included shift plates are the reason it can cover both a seven-speed-plus-reverse H-pattern and a simple sequential up-down gate. The 13 cm metal stick and a detachable knob are meant to feel closer to a real gearbox, and the knob can be swapped for a universal aftermarket shifter knob.

It is a strong match on PC, where Thrustmaster says it works with racing wheels over USB. On PlayStation and Xbox, it is an add-on for compatible Thrustmaster wheel bases, not a standalone console controller and not a universal fit for every wheel brand. Check Thrustmaster’s current lists before you assume a title will see both H-pattern and sequential modes.

## Key features

Details below come from Thrustmaster’s official TH8A product, manual, and support pages:

- Dual-mode shifter with two included plates: an H-pattern (7+1) plate, installed by default, and a sequential (+/−) plate that you fit when you want an up-down gate.
- 100% metal internal mechanism and a 100% metal, 13 cm gear stick. The shift knob is removable and is described as compatible with universal real shifter knobs.
- H.E.A.R.T Hall-effect magnetic sensors with no tact switches or potentiometers in the sensing path, so position reading is contactless.
- Adjustable gear-shift resistance, plus adjustable shift travel through PC software. Firmware is upgradable, and settings can be stored in internal memory.
- A 360-degree adjustable clamping system for tables, shelves, or cockpits, plus screw threads for hard-mounting to a rig if you remove the clamp.
- Detachable cables for DIN-to-USB and DIN-to-DIN (mini-DIN/DIN) connections.
- Official platform support includes PC, PS4, PS5, and Xbox. Dimensions are 25 x 32 x 16 cm, and the unit weighs 2 kg.

## How to use it

Decide first whether you want H-pattern or sequential, then fit the matching plate with the included Allen key as the manual shows. On PC, connect the DIN-to-USB cable directly to the computer; Thrustmaster recommends that path so both modes can work as intended. Connecting the shifter to a wheel base with the DIN cable on PC may limit you to sequential mode.

On PlayStation or Xbox, use the mini-DIN/DIN cable into a compatible Thrustmaster wheel base, not the console’s USB port. H-pattern versus sequential support can still depend on the wheel and the game, so use Thrustmaster’s current compatibility notes rather than assuming every title sees every mode. After the physical setup, adjust clamp angle, mounting side, and shift resistance until the gate feels positive without being a fight. On PC, the official software is the place to fine-tune travel and store that setup.

If you later hard-mount the shifter to a cockpit or wheel stand, use the built-in screw threads and confirm that your rig has a matching shifter plate. A solid mount matters as much as the mechanism: a flexing table clamp will make even a metal gate feel vague.

## Quick FAQ

### Does it work on PS5, Xbox, and PC?

On PC, yes, over USB, and Thrustmaster says it can work with racing wheels on that platform. On PlayStation and Xbox, it is designed to connect to a compatible Thrustmaster wheel base with the DIN cable. It is not a standalone console shifter for every wheel brand.

### Can it do both H-pattern and sequential shifting?

Yes. A 7+1 H-pattern plate and a sequential (+/−) plate are both included. You change plates to switch modes, and console game support for each mode can still vary.

### Can I change the shift knob?

Yes. The knob is detachable, and Thrustmaster describes it as compatible with universal real shifter knobs.

### How do I mount it?

A 360-degree clamp is included for a table or shelf, and the body has screw threads for cockpit mounting if you prefer a hard mount.
