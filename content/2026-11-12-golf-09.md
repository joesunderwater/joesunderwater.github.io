## Who it's for

The SPG-7 is for home golfers who want a dedicated, quickly assembled net rather than a large steel enclosure, and who value the ball coming back after the shot. Spornia lists it for indoor or outdoor use, for coaches and players, and for everyday full-swing practice in a garage, backyard, or indoor space.

You still need enough room to swing. Spornia advises evaluating a minimum of about 10 feet of depth, plus width and height for a 7 x 7 x 7 ft net with an additional foot of roof height. If your space is tighter than that, or you want a wide 10-foot projector screen, a larger enclosure may be a better fit.

## How to use it

Clear a level area with enough depth for a full swing and for the 7-foot-deep net, then pop the frame up and attach the target sheet, roof, and any included fixtures according to Spornia’s instructions. Place your hitting mat so shots strike the target sheet, which helps the ball roll back and reduces noise. Use the chipping basket when you want short-game practice rather than full swings.

For driver practice, confirm you have ceiling clearance for the net plus the extra roof height, and that mishits still stay inside the 7-foot-wide mouth. If you later add a simulator screen, use a screen Spornia lists as compatible rather than hanging a random sheet that may not absorb impact the same way.

When you are finished, fold the net down and store it in the carry bag. Inspect the target sheet and netting regularly, especially after outdoor use.
