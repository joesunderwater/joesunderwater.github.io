## How to use it

Open the pop-up frame, add the support rods, and use the fixing pins if you are on grass or another surface that can take them. Face the pocket side when you want to aim at specific targets; flip the net to the open side when you are hitting slightly longer chips. Place the 13 x 17 inch dual-turf mat at a comfortable chipping distance, and start with the foam balls, especially indoors.

Treat the pockets as accuracy targets, not as a measure of how far a real wedge shot would fly. When you go back to a simulator or the course, remember that foam-ball distances and spin will differ. Fold the net when you are finished so it stores flat, and keep the foam balls and pins with the mat so the kit stays together.

If you practice outdoors with a real golf ball, make sure the surroundings can handle a mishit; this is still a small chipping net, not a full-swing cage.
