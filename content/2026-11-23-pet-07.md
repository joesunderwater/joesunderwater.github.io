Clipping nails with a scissor-style trimmer is fast, but the sudden snip is exactly what makes a lot of dogs pull away. A grinder files the nail down in small passes instead, and it is easier to avoid cutting the quick. The Casfuy upgraded nail grinder is on the Pet Gadgets board as a cordless, two-speed option for dogs, cats, and other small pets, with a quieter motor and a cap of ports so you can match the opening to the nail.

The photo on this page is an illustrative image of the product, not Amazon's photo. Check the Amazon listing for exact color and details before you order.

## Who it's for

This grinder is aimed at pet owners who want to do nails at home, including people with puppies, small dogs, large dogs, or cats. The two speeds let you start gently on a nervous pet or a thin nail, then move to the higher speed for thicker nails. It is also a fit if you prefer filing to clipping, or if your pet already tenses at the sound of a clipper.

It is not a once-and-done tool for a pet that has never had nails touched. Plan on short sessions and a lot of treats, especially the first few times.

## How to use it

Charge the grinder before the first use. If your pet has long fur around the paws, trim or hold that hair back so it cannot catch in the spinning bit. The listing also suggests slipping a sock or stocking over the paw and pushing just the nails through. Choose the port that fits the nail, start on low speed, and grind for only a few seconds at a time, then check your progress. The listing advises keeping any single pass to no more than about five seconds and changing the angle as you smooth the tip.

Work in a well-lit spot so you can see the nail, stop well before the quick, and take breaks if your pet gets restless. When you are done, cap the grinder and store it out of reach. Follow the included manual for first-time use with a nervous dog or cat.
