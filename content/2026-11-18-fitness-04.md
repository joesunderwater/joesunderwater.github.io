## Who it's for

The listing aims this at athletes and anyone dealing with muscle fatigue and soreness, with suggested use on the back, arms, and legs. TOLOCO also calls it a holiday gift idea for women and men, family, and friends.

The listing has clear safety warnings. Do not use it on the neck, spine, bones, head, face, or eyes. Only use it on soft tissue (muscles). The brand says pregnant women, young children, and people with osteoporosis, heart disease, blood clots, or neurological conditions should avoid it or use caution. If any of those apply, check with your doctor first.

## How to use it

Charge it fully with the included USB cable and a wall adapter you already have. Pick a head that fits the area. Larger, rounder heads are usually good for big muscles like the thighs and glutes, and smaller ones for targeted spots.

The listing's directions are simple: turn on the massage gun, select the speed you want, and gently glide it over sore muscles for 1 to 2 minutes per area. Start on the lowest speed and let the device do the work rather than pressing hard. Move slowly across the muscle instead of holding it in one spot.

Use it after workouts, after runs, or at the end of a long day at your desk. Keep it away from bones, joints, the neck, and the spine, as the listing warns.
