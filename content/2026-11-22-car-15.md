- **Measure first.** Check your bottle's base diameter and your car's cup holder against the listing's 2.7–4.2 inch bottle range and 2.6–4 inch base range.
- **Install.** Set the base into the cup holder, hold it, and twist the top until the base expands and sits snugly without wobbling.
- **Turn the offset the right way.** Rotate it so the wider top clears the shifter, console lid, or the cup holder next to it.

- **Use the phone slot.** Drop your phone, wallet, or glasses in the side holder, and keep handling your phone to when you are safely parked.
