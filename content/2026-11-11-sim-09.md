A full sim rig is easy to want and hard to leave in a living room. The Next Level Racing GT Lite is a foldable cockpit that tries to solve that: a padded, breathable fabric seat and a wheel-and-pedal frame in a GT driving position, with hubs that let the whole thing fold down when you are done.

## Who it's for

The GT Lite (model NLR-S021) is for people who want a dedicated racing seat and a rigid place to hard-mount a wheel and pedals, but who need to store the cockpit after a session. Next Level Racing designed it as a space-saving GT-position cockpit, not as the Formula-and-GT F-GT Lite, which is a different model with two driving positions.

It is a fit if you use a Logitech, Thrustmaster, or Fanatec wheel, or another base that can clamp or bolt to a pre-drilled plate. Official support materials say it can also handle entry-level direct-drive bases up to 10 Nm, with the MOZA R9 given as an example. It is not positioned as a heavy, high-torque professional chassis. Next Level Racing lists a supported user height of 120–200 cm (4 ft to 6 ft 6 in) and a maximum user weight of 130 kg (287 lb).

## Key features

Details below come from Next Level Racing’s official GT Lite support page and the Amazon listing for NLR-S021:

- A foldable GT-position cockpit with Next Level Racing Hubs, which the company says can withstand 150 kg of force each and allow the frame to fold for storage.
- A breathable fabric seat, with adjustable wheel, pedal, and shifter positions and angles.
- Pre-drilled hard-mount points for wheels, pedals, and shifters, plus support for wheels that use a clamp. Official materials list compatibility with major wheels and pedals and pre-drilled patterns for Logitech, Thrustmaster, and Fanatec.
- A gear-shifter mount included in the box, which can be installed on the left or right side. Next Level Racing also lists compatibility with a range of Thrustmaster, Logitech, MOZA, and Fanatec shifters.
- The cockpit can be folded with electronics still installed, and official FAQ notes include built-in roller wheels for moving and storing it.
- Assembled size in the GT position is 176 x 93 x 95 cm (69.2 x 36.6 x 37.4 inches). Folded size is 104 x 93 x 30 cm (40.9 x 36.6 x 11.8 inches). The cockpit weighs 14.4 kg (31.7 lb).
- In the box: the cockpit frame, instructions and tools, bolts to hard-mount a wheel, shifter, and pedals, and a gear-shifter adapter.

## How to use it

Assemble the frame, then set the seat, wheel plate, and pedal tray so your hips, hands, and feet line up in a GT posture: slightly upright, with the wheel at a comfortable reach and the pedals close enough for firm brake pressure. Hard-mount the wheel and pedals when you can; a bolted connection is more rigid than a clamp, especially if you use a stronger wheel or a load-cell brake.

Fit the shifter mount on the side you actually use, then tighten the hubs before the first session. When you are finished, fold the cockpit as the manual shows. Official materials say it can fold with the electronics still installed, which is the feature that makes closet or room-to-room storage realistic. Use the roller wheels when you move it rather than dragging the frame.

Stay inside the published height and weight ranges, and treat the 10 Nm direct-drive note as a limit rather than a challenge. If you later want a reclined Formula position as well as GT, that is the F-GT Lite’s job, not this model’s. Check the Amazon listing for the current bolt kit and any finish or edition differences, such as a Carbon Grey version, which is a separate option.

## Quick FAQ

### How is this different from the F-GT Lite?

The GT Lite is a foldable cockpit in a GT driving position. The F-GT Lite is a different model that switches between Formula and GT positions.

### Will it work with my wheel and pedals?

It is pre-drilled for Logitech, Thrustmaster, and Fanatec hardware and supports clamped wheels. Official materials also mention entry-level direct-drive bases up to 10 Nm. Confirm your exact wheel and pedals on the current compatibility notes.

### Can I fold it with the wheel still attached?

Yes. Next Level Racing says the design can fold and pack away with electronics installed, and the official FAQ mentions built-in roller wheels for moving it.

### What size user is it for?

The published range is 120–200 cm (4 ft to 6 ft 6 in) in height and up to 130 kg (287 lb).
