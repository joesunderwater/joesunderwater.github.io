## How to use it

Start easy. Sprinkle a few pieces of kibble or small treats on top of the felt so your dog understands the game, then tuck food deeper into the flaps as they get the hang of it. Use it flat on the floor, or fold it into the bowl shape if you want a more contained meal. Stay with your pet while they work. If they start chewing or tearing the felt, take the mat up.

When the session is over, shake out crumbs, hand wash as needed, and let it dry before it goes back in the bag. Do not leave a fabric foraging mat with an unattended chewer.

## Quick FAQ

### Is this a chew toy?

No. It is a supervised foraging mat. Remove it if your pet starts tearing the felt.

### Can I use it for a full meal?

Yes, if you hide kibble throughout the flaps. Start with visible pieces so your dog learns to search, then hide food more thoroughly.

### How do I clean it?

The listing recommends hand washing the felt. Shake it out after each use and let it dry completely before storing.
