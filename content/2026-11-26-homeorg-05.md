## How to use it

The simplest approach is to do the whole closet at once so everything matches:

1. **Pull out the old hangers** and sort clothes as you go, setting aside anything you no longer wear.
2. **Hang lightweight items first.** Shirts, blouses, and dresses go straight onto the velvet hangers.
3. **Use the notches** for camisoles, sundresses, and anything with thin straps.
4. **Fold pants over the bar.** The velvet bar helps keep jeans and trousers from sliding.
5. **Keep heavy coats separate.** Follow the brand's guidance and use sturdier hangers for winter coats and bulky jackets.

Because the hooks swivel, you can turn every hanger the same direction for a neater look.
