## How to use it

Use it like a classic cream cleanser:

1. Wet your face with lukewarm water.
2. Massage a small amount of cleanser over your skin in gentle circles, including over light makeup.
3. Rinse thoroughly and pat dry.
4. Follow with the rest of your routine, such as moisturizer at night and sunscreen in the morning.

Because the listing says it works for both morning and night, it is easy to make this your one everyday face wash. The large bottle also makes sense as a body or hand wash for anyone who likes a gentle, fragrance-free option.
