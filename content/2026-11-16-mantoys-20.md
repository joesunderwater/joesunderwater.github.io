## How to use it

Treat it as the core of a small outdoor kit. On a camping trip, use the blade for camp chores, and use the serrated part for rougher cutting. When it's time to start a fire, pull the ferro rod from the sheath and strike it to throw sparks onto dry tinder. Gerber notes a fire-striking edge along the spine for this.

Use the sheath's sharpening stone to touch up the edge between trips, so the blade is ready the next time you need it. Keep the whistle in mind as a basic signaling tool if someone in your group gets separated.

Between trips, wipe the knife clean and dry before you sheath it. Store it with your camping or hunting gear so the whole kit is in one place. As a gift, pair it with a weekend camping trip or a new pack.
