## How to use it

Pair the Pencil over USB-C, then snap it to the side of your iPad so it is ready whenever you open the case. For school or meetings, open Notes and write directly on the page, then mark up slides or PDFs as you go. For planning, load a digital planner template into your favorite note app and use the Pencil to check off tasks and block out your week.

If you like to draw, Freeform is a free place to start sketching ideas. Tilt the Pencil for broader shading strokes, and if you have a supported iPad Pro or iPad Air, use hover to see where your mark will land before touching the screen. Signing documents is quick too: open the PDF, sign with the Pencil, and send it back without printing.
