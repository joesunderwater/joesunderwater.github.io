## How to use it

- **Charge it the night before.** Top up the power bank at home or at the hotel so it is full when you head out.
- **Keep it in your carry-on, not your checked bag.** Airlines generally require spare lithium batteries to travel in the cabin. Check your airline's rules before you fly.
- **Use the short cable for quick top-ups.** The attached USB-C cable is handy for phones and other USB-C devices. Swap in a different cable for devices with other ports.
- **Share it on group trips.** Since the cable is detachable, it is easy to pass around to whoever is running low.
- **Store one at home for outages.** Keep it charged in a drawer with a flashlight so your phone stays on during a power cut.
