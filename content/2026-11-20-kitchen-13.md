The Anker Nano Travel Adapter goes the other way: Anker describes it as having a credit card-sized footprint, with foldable, retractable prongs and five ways to charge. ## Who it's for

This adapter suits travelers who mostly charge phones, earbuds, watches, and other small electronics and want something that slips into a pocket or a small tech pouch. It's a good fit for weekend city breaks, backpacking, and business trips where you only carry a carry-on. If you need to run a laptop with a grounded 3-prong charger or a high-power appliance, read the notes below first, since this adapter has limits.

## Key features

These details come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN:

- **Universal plug compatibility.** The adapter covers Type A (US, Canada, Japan, China), Type C (EU), Type G (UK, Singapore), and Type I (Australia).
- **Power 5 devices at once.** It has 1 AC outlet, 2 USB-A ports, and 2 USB-C ports.
- **20W USB output.** A single USB-C port delivers up to 20W. Anker says that charges an iPhone 16 to 50% in 28 minutes. When ports are shared, USB output is 15W max.
- **Compact size.** It measures about 3.39 x 1.97 x 0.98 inches (86 x 50 x 25 mm) and weighs 3.77 ounces. Anker says it's 43% smaller than similar models.
- **Foldable, retractable prongs.** Press and hold the side button and slide to extend the prongs until they lock.
- **2-pin design.** Anker says the 2-pin to 2-pin design helps reduce the risk of electric shock.
- **In the box.** 1 Anker Nano Travel Adapter (5-in-1, 20W), a welcome guide, and an 18-month warranty.

A few limits to know: it is **not a voltage converter** (it works with 100–240V devices), the AC outlet does not support 3-prong grounded plugs, and the AC port supports up to 6A.

## How to use it

To extend the prongs, press and hold the side button and slide it all the way until the prongs lock. Don't force the slider without pressing the button. Plug it into the wall, then connect your phone to a USB-C port for the fastest charge. Use the other ports for earbuds, a watch, or a power bank.

Packing ideas:

- Pair it with one short USB-C cable and a USB-C to Lightning cable to cover most phones and earbuds.
- Keep it in a jacket pocket or a small pouch so it's easy to reach at the airport or hotel.
- Charge your phone and a power bank overnight so both are ready for a day of sightseeing.

## Quick FAQ

### Can I use it with a hair dryer?

The listing says it is not a voltage converter and the AC port supports up to 6A. Check your appliance's voltage and power rating against the Amazon listing before plugging it in.

### Will my laptop charger work in it?

The AC outlet does not support 3-prong grounded plugs, including grounded laptop chargers. A laptop charger with a 2-prong plug that supports the local voltage may work within the adapter's ratings.

### How fast does it charge a phone?

A single USB-C port delivers up to 20W. Anker says that charges an iPhone 16 to 50% in 28 minutes. Shared ports drop to 15W max.
