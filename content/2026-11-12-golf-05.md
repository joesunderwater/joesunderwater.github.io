## How to use it

Measure the room first: width, height, and the depth from the hitting area to the screen, including the space your launch monitor needs behind or beside the ball. Assemble the steel frame according to the included instructions, attach the impact screen so it is tight and even, then add the side and top panels and the interior foam padding.

Place your hitting mat so a full swing stays inside the bay, and mount or stand the projector so the image fills the 4:3 screen without large unused borders. A short-throw projector is often easier in a shallow room, but you should match throw ratio and resolution to this screen format. Keep people and pets outside the hitting area, and inspect the screen and netting before sessions.

The enclosure contains the shot; it does not replace a launch monitor or simulator software. Pair it with the monitor and projector you already own, or plan those purchases around the 12 x 9 x 6 footprint and the 10-foot ceiling recommendation.
