## How to wear it

Treat The Comfy like the warmest layer in the house. Pull it on over a T-shirt and joggers or your pajamas, flip the hood up, and settle in. When your feet get cold, tuck your legs inside the body for full coverage. That is the move the patented design is built around.

Because your arms are in real sleeves, you can keep it on while you get up for snacks, answer the door, or type on a laptop. The ribbed cuffs keep the sleeves from sliding down over your hands. Use the giant front pocket for your phone, the remote, or a book.

It also travels well to places where you sit still in the cold. Think of a chilly campsite, a backyard fire, or the bleachers at a game. Toss it in the wash when it needs it. Check the care label for the exact settings.
