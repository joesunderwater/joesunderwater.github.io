## How to use it

1. **Stand bottles in the center.** Put shampoo, conditioner, lotion, and other liquids upright in the middle compartment, where the listing says they are designed to stand.
2. **Use one side for makeup or skincare** and the other for daily items such as a toothbrush, razor, and hair ties.
3. **Put damp items in the PVC front pocket.** A wet washcloth, a used razor, or a toothbrush can go here, away from dry items.
4. **Unzip and leave it open on the counter.** Because it opens wide, you can use it as a mini station instead of unpacking everything.
5. **Keep it stocked.** Store travel-size duplicates in it between trips so packing takes seconds.
