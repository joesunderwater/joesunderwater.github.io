## How to use it

Start by charging the battery fully before your first project. Then:

- **Set the clutch for screws.** Use a lower clutch setting for small screws in soft materials like particleboard furniture, and raise it for longer screws in wood. The clutch slips when the screw is seated, which helps prevent stripped heads and overdriven screws.
- **Pick the right bit.** Use the drill bits for pilot holes and anchors, and the driver bits for screws. Match the bit to the material: wood, metal, or plastic.
- **Hang it right.** For shelves and wall decor, drill a pilot hole, insert your anchor, and drive the screw with a driver bit.
- **Put it back in the case.** Keeping bits in the case means you won't spend ten minutes hunting for the right Phillips head next time.

When the battery runs low, recharge it, and if you add another POWERCONNECT tool later, the same battery will work in it.
