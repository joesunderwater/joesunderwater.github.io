## How to use it

Measure your pet from nose to base of tail and from the floor to the top of the head or ears, then compare those numbers with the carrier's interior space. Assemble the top and bottom with the screws so the shell is secure. For a first trip, leave the carrier out with a familiar towel or bed inside so it does not appear only on vet day.

Use the top door if your pet resists the front opening. Latch both doors fully before you lift the handle, and set the carrier on a stable seat, ideally secured in the car so it cannot slide. After the trip, wipe the interior and let it dry. Do not assume this size is airline-approved; check your airline's rules if you need to fly.
