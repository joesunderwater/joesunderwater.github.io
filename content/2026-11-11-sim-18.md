## How to use it

Connect the stick to your Xbox or PC; Thrustmaster describes it as plug and play. Check Thrustmaster's support site for any current PC drivers or firmware notes. Decide on your layout first: attached throttle if you are flying from the sofa or have limited desk space, detached if you want stick and throttle spread out like a real cockpit.

Pick one rudder method and stick with it while you learn. Twisting the handle is intuitive for combat and space games. If you find yourself adding rudder by accident, lock the twist and use the tilting lever on the throttle instead. Set stick resistance to something firm enough that small corrections feel controlled.

In each game, check the controls menu for a HOTAS or joystick preset, then map the buttons you use most, such as landing gear, flaps, view, and weapons, to the positions you can reach without looking. If you later add the T.Flight Rudder Pedals, switch your rudder input to the pedals in the game settings.
