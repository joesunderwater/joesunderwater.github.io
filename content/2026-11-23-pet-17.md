## Who it's for

This large size is made for dogs in the 25- to 120-pound range, and for walkers or runners who want both hands free. The waist belt adjusts from about 27 to 48 inches. It suits neighborhood walks, easy jogs, and hiking where you still want a quick handhold if your dog starts to pull.

It is not a no-pull training harness, and a strong puller can still move you if the belt is your only point of control. If your dog lunges hard, use the padded handles and consider whether a waist leash is the right setup for that dog.

## How to use it

Fit the waist belt snugly around your hips, not your stomach, so it cannot slide up, and clip the leash to your dog's harness or collar ring as you prefer. Adjust the leash length for a walk or a jog, and keep a padded handle in easy reach for crossings, crowds, or a sudden pull. Load the zipper pouch with bags and keys so you are not fishing in pockets.

Practice in a quiet area first so you and your dog get used to the belt moving with you. At night, add a light if you need more visibility than reflective thread can give. When you are done, unclip, empty the pouch, and check the bungees, clip, and stitching for wear.
