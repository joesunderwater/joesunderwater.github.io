## How to use it

HEETA's directions are straightforward. Wet your hair and apply shampoo. Then gently massage your scalp with the brush in small circular motions, working section by section from the hairline to the nape. Rinse thoroughly, then store the massager in a dry place.

To work in conditioner or a hair oil, use the same circular motion with lighter pressure so the product spreads evenly. Let the bristles do the work and do not press hard. The brand advises using it with caution, or checking with a healthcare professional first, if you have any medical conditions affecting your scalp.
