A desk keyboard has one job that is easy to get wrong: it should feel fast and quiet enough for all-day typing, and it should disappear when you are not looking at it. The Logitech MX Keys S is the current full-size MX productivity keyboard, with low-profile dished keys, smart backlighting, and pairing for up to three devices. It is also the natural partner to an MX mouse if you are building a clean, wireless desk.

## Who it's for

This keyboard is for people who type for a living, or at least for a full workday, and want a low-profile, scissor-switch feel rather than a tall mechanical board. Logitech designed it for a natural wrist angle and for desks that already include a computer, a laptop, and maybe a tablet. Easy-Switch pairing lets you move among up to three devices with a key press.

It is a fit for Windows, macOS, Chrome OS, Linux, and iPadOS. Advanced features are deepest in Logi Options+ on Windows and macOS; other systems get basic typing and switching. This listing is the Graphite model. If you want a smaller board, Logitech also sells the MX Keys Mini, which is a different product.

## Key features

Details below come from Logitech’s official MX Keys S product and support specifications:

- Full-size, low-profile wireless keyboard with spherically dished keys and a scissor-switch mechanism, designed for a stable, quieter keystroke and a laptop-like typing feel.
- Smart illumination that lights the keys when your hands approach and adjusts brightness to the room. Duration and brightness can be set in Logi Options+.
- Easy-Switch pairing for up to three devices, with a key press to move among them.
- Wireless connection over Bluetooth Low Energy or the included Logi Bolt USB receiver.
- Rechargeable 1500 mAh battery, charged over USB-C with the included USB-A-to-USB-C cable. Battery life is up to 10 days with backlighting on at predefined settings, or up to five months with backlighting off.
- Programmable keys and Smart Actions in Logi Options+, so one keystroke can run a multi-step shortcut.
- Dimensions of about 20.5 x 430.2 x 131.63 mm and a weight of 810 g. The Graphite model’s plastic parts include recycled content, according to Logitech.

## How to use it

Plug in the Logi Bolt receiver or pair over Bluetooth, then assign each of the three Easy-Switch slots to a computer or tablet you use regularly. Install Logi Options+ if you want to tune backlighting, remap keys, or create Smart Actions for repetitive tasks such as opening a set of apps or pasting a standard snippet.

The backlighting is most useful in a dim room or on an evening desk: let it auto-light when your hands approach, and turn the duration down in the app if you want to stretch battery life. If you only need the keys lit occasionally, leaving the backlight off is how you get closer to the multi-month battery rating. Charge it with the USB-C cable when the battery runs low; you can usually keep typing while it charges, depending on how you have it set up.

If you already use an MX Master mouse, the same Options+ software can manage both, including switching them together across computers. Check the Amazon listing for language layout and whether you are buying the standard MX Keys S or a Mac-specific version.

## Quick FAQ

### How many devices can it connect to?

Up to three, using Easy-Switch. You can connect over Bluetooth or the Logi Bolt receiver and move among those devices with a key press.

### How long does the battery last?

Logitech rates it at up to 10 days with backlighting on at predefined settings, or up to five months with backlighting off. USB-C charging is included.

### Does it have a backlight?

Yes. The keys light up when your hands approach and can adjust to the room. You can control brightness and duration in Logi Options+.

### Will it work with a Mac and a Windows PC?

Yes. It is compatible with Windows, macOS, Chrome OS, Linux, and iPadOS. Use Logi Options+ on Windows or macOS for the deeper customization.
