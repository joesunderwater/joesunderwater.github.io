## Who it's for

This 4 x 5 ft mat is for golfers setting up a home simulator, garage bay, or teaching space who want a larger stance and hitting area, and who prefer to tee the ball with a real wooden or plastic tee rather than only a rubber range tee. ## How to use it

Lay the mat on a flat, stable floor so it does not rock under your stance. Give yourself enough room to make a full swing into a net or impact screen, and place the ball on the turf for irons, or insert a wooden or plastic tee when you want to practice driver and woods. Keep your stance on the mat rather than half on the floor, so your height relative to the ball stays consistent.

Use the way the club moves through the turf as feedback. A heavy contact should feel different from a clean strike; that is the point of a thicker, denser mat. Rotate your hitting spot over time so wear is not concentrated on one patch, and keep the turf clear of debris. If you later add a launch monitor, set the ball in a consistent place on the mat so the monitor’s alignment stays honest.

For outdoor use, bring the mat in when you are finished if it will sit in standing water or harsh sun for long periods, and follow any care notes on the listing.
