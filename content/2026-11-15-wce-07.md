## Who it's for

Apple positions the Ultra 3 as a sports and adventure watch, aimed at runners, triathletes, mountain bikers, backcountry hikers, climbers, swimmers, and divers. It also suits anyone who wants a single premium watch that can handle early workouts and late meetings without swapping devices.

The cellular model is a fit if you want to take calls, stream music and podcasts, and stay reachable without your iPhone nearby. Like other Apple Watch models, it is designed to work with an iPhone, and cellular service requires a carrier plan.

## Key features

Details below come from the Amazon listing for this ASIN and Apple retail information:

- **Rugged build:** a titanium case and sapphire crystal display, with water resistance up to 100m for swimming, diving, and high-speed water sports.
- **Display:** Apple says the Ultra 3 has a larger display that emits more light at wider angles, and the screen can be used as a flashlight.
- **Battery:** up to 42 hours of normal use and up to 72 hours in Low Power Mode. A workout with full GPS and heart rate tracking can run up to 20 hours in Low Power Mode.
- **Training tools:** precision dual-frequency GPS, Pacer, Heart Rate Zones, Custom Workouts, running power, training load, and Workout Buddy (powered by Apple Intelligence from a nearby compatible iPhone).
- **Health features:** ECG and irregular rhythm notifications, alerts for possible hypertension and unusually high or low heart rate, the Vitals app for overnight metrics, sleep score, and blood oxygen readings. Availability of some health features varies by region.
- **Safety:** fall detection, crash detection, Check In, and built-in satellite communications for Emergency SOS when you are outside cellular and Wi-Fi coverage.
- **Cellular with 5G:** make calls, stream audio, and listen through AirPods or the built-in speaker without your iPhone.
- **Action button:** a customizable physical button for things like starting a workout or turning on the flashlight.

## How to use it

Set the Action button to whatever you reach for most. For runners, that is usually starting a workout; for everyone else, the flashlight or a shortcut may be more useful. Before a long race or hike, switch on Low Power Mode to stretch battery life toward the longer figure Apple lists.

For the workday, the natural titanium case reads as a clean, technical look with a dress shirt or an oxford. Apple sells several band styles for the Ultra line, so you can swap from the Ocean Band to something dressier for the evening.

Take a few minutes to set up Emergency SOS, Check In, and your emergency contacts in the Watch app on your iPhone. These features only help if they are configured before you need them.

## Quick FAQ

### Does the Ultra 3 work without my iPhone nearby?

The GPS + Cellular model can make calls, stream music and podcasts, and use the built-in speaker without your iPhone nearby, provided you have an active cellular plan. Initial setup still requires an iPhone.

### How long does the battery last?

Apple lists up to 42 hours of normal use and up to 72 hours in Low Power Mode. Real results depend on settings and usage.

### Can I swim or dive with it?

Apple rates it water resistant up to 100m and describes it as suitable for swimmers, divers, and water sports. Check Apple’s guidance for specific diving limits.
